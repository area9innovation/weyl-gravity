\[
ds^2=2 du dv + dx^2 + dy^2 + H(u,x,y) du^2,
\]

\[
B_{uu}=-\frac14(\partial_x^2+\partial_y^2)^2H.
\]

There are no nonlinear terms in $H$.  This is an arbitrary-profile local
identity, not a finite harmonic or Fourier truncation.

The slice contains both branches.  An Einstein representative is
$H_E=f(u)(x^2-y^2)$, for which $\Delta_\perp H_E=0$.  A genuinely
non-Einstein Weyl representative is $H_X=g(u)x^3$, for which
$\Delta_\perp H_X=6xg(u)\ne0$ but $\Delta_\perp^2H_X=0$.  Every sum
$aH_E+bH_X$ is an exact Bach-flat metric.

Consequently the complete restricted Taylor tensor vanishes before transfer:

\[
q_2(H_E,H_E)=q_2(H_E,H_X)=q_2(H_X,H_X)=0.
\]

Thus every restricted transferred bracket
$\ell_2=\pi_{\rm cl}q_2(\iota_{\rm cl}\otimes\iota_{\rm cl})$ is zero,
independently of the homotopy.  The Einstein and extra-Weyl directions close
together on this aligned support-local sector, and no negative direction is
regenerated by $\ell_2$.  The pairing sign of the already-present extra
branch is not classified here.

This is `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC` only.  It is a restricted support-local metric
