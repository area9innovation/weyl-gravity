## Exact-point regeneration

The point route does not evaluate the inherited whole-cell Taylor hull at
its centre.

Instead it:

1. substitutes \(\omega=\omega_0\) into the exact radial coefficient matrix
   before coefficient generation;
2. makes every frequency-linear coefficient and every frame derivative
   exactly zero;
3. retains only outward radial-integration remainders;
4. regenerates the practical infinity endpoint with the omega interval
   collapsed exactly to \(\omega_0\);
5. propagates the \(XI0/XI1\) and \(XI2/XI3\) carrier planes as normalized
   Grassmann graphs.

Volterra \(z\)-cells.  The 224 exact-point radial factors are in progress.
