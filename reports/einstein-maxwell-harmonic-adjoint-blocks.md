
## Projection rules and stop conditions

their pairings require opposite `S1` momentum, equal `ell`, conjugate `m`, and
opposite frequencies.  Rotation targets transform at `L=1`; they can couple
different angular sectors whenever the Clebsch--Gordan product contains
