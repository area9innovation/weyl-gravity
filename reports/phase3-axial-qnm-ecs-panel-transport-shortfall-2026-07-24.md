# Phase 3 ECS panel-transport shortfall

A 16-panel, 128-bit `acb` transport rail now covers the proposed QNM circle.
It uses order-16 Taylor propagation from \(r=45\) to \(r=4\), step \(1/4\),
with a Cauchy enclosure for every omitted tail.

Per-step recentering improves the global Gronwall radius from order
\(10^{33}\) to order \(10^{16}\), but rectangular dependency remains fatal.
No outgoing Evans column or \(b/a\) contour function is certified.

The quantified successor is an affine Lohner/Taylor-model transport or a
Riccati/Grassmannian formulation, combined with the newly certified
canonical intrinsic tangent ECS initializer.
