# Natural rank-310 SDR on a relative-open Bach-flat class

Let (g) lie in the certified radius-(1/4) ADM neighbourhood of unit
Nariai inside the smooth Bach-flat locus.  The ADM inequalities fix a common
future timelike covector and a positive spatial metric.  The future unit
normal together with the positive spatial square root from (h) to (h_N)
therefore gives a smooth global fibre isometry.  Its induced maps on tensors,
forms and metric-split tractors, followed by multiplication by

\[
 \rho_g=(d\operatorname{vol}_g/d\operatorname{vol}_{N})^{1/2},
\]

put all bundle pairings in one fixed cyclic bundle.  These maps and their
inverses are pointwise.

In split coordinates the rank-310 differential uses fixed algebraic Kostant
maps (p_0,J_0,r_0) and the natural operators

\[
L_0, L_1, d_{\rm aut}, K, M^D, \Phi, B_{\rm action}
\]

with their formal adjoints.  Hence its difference from unit Nariai occupies
only

\[
\Delta g,\Delta k,\Delta M,\Delta B,
\Delta g^\sharp,\Delta k^\sharp.
\]

This is an invariant coefficient-complete statement: each operator is fixed
by the metric and finitely many of its jets.  It does not assert a serialized
component PBW expansion.

The normal BGG identities give the Kostant complement and the corrected gauge
reconstruction.  In four dimensions Bach-flatness is exactly the Yang--Mills
condition for the normal tractor connection, so the detour rows form a
complex.  Linearizing the diffeomorphism/Weyl-invariant action at a Bach-flat
solution gives (B_{\rm action}K=0) and its adjoint.  These are exactly the
relations of the certified six-block finite HPL theorem.  Therefore

\[
 I_g=(1-H_N\Delta)I_N,
 \qquad
 \pi_g=\pi_N(1-\Delta H_N),
 \qquad
 H_g=H_N-H_N\Delta H_N
\]

obey every chain, retract, side and cyclic identity.  The two HPL series
terminate after one correction.  The retained differential is
(pi_gQ_{310,g}I_g), including both forced quadratic gauge/splitting terms,
and is the natural action-derived metric Bach complex.

Finally, the triangular variables

\[
x=a-d_{{\rm aut},g}J_0s-L_{1,g}h,
\qquad
y=\lambda+c\Phi_gh
\]

and their forced cotangent transform are finite-order and BV canonical.
Conjugating the split SDR therefore proves the result on every original field,
equation, antifield and identity row.

No analytic inverse of the non-Einstein metric Bach endpoint is used.  The
result is a class-wide support-local cyclic SDR, not yet a class-wide metric or
rank-310 Green theorem.
