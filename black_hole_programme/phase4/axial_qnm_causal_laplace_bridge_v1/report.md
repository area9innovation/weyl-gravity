# Causal retarded/Laplace continuation theorem

## Result

On the gauge-invariant axial \(\ell=2\) transverse-traceless spin-two
sector, the Einstein--Weyl parent family is normally hyperbolic and has
retarded Green operators.  The pure-Weyl metric response is their first
signed squared-mass derivative:

\[
G_{hh,{\rm W}}^{\rm ret}
=-\frac{1}{4\alpha_{\rm W}}
\left.\partial_mG_m^{\rm ret}\right|_0
=\frac{1}{4\alpha_{\rm W}}
G_0^{\rm ret}\mathcal A G_0^{\rm ret}.
\]

The right side is a composition of retarded kernels and is therefore
retarded.

For \(\operatorname{Im}\omega<-c\), below the energy-growth bound, the
causal Laplace transform equals the ordinary finite-energy radial
resolvent.  Its square-integrable endpoint solutions are precisely
future-horizon ingoing and infinity outgoing.  Compact source and
observation cutoffs therefore identify it with the physical outgoing
transfer in that initial half-plane.

The global ECS Fredholm and complete massive Jost constructions give its
unique meromorphic continuation to the certified QNM disk.  Since

\[
\omega_n'(0)=\frac{2i}{3\omega_n}\kappa_n\ne0,
\]

the continued causal transfer has

\[
G_{hh,{\rm W}}(\omega)
=-\frac{\omega_n'(0)P_n}
{4\alpha_{\rm W}(\omega-\omega_n)^2}
+O((\omega-\omega_n)^{-1}).
\]

The coefficient is nonzero and rank one.

## Boundary

This is a mode-reduced causal resonance theorem.  It is not a full metric
BV propagator and does not prove that a specified real causal matter source
excites the pole.  Most importantly, it does not shift the full
inverse-Laplace contour.  Threshold/branch-cut, high-frequency, and non-pole
remainder control remain necessary before the local residue can be asserted
as a term in the complete retarded waveform.
