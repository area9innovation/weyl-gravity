# Axial local/nonlocal positivity, v1

This package certifies:

- no rational local dynamically compatible metric operator makes the
  nonsplit spin-two flux positive, even without imposing \(\eta^2=1\);
- a compatible fundamental symmetry always exists on the combined future
  space, while factorization over \(\mathscr I^+\oplus\mathcal H^+\) is a
  separate scattering condition;
- the matrix-sign construction is spectral but not invariant under general
  nonunitary frame congruences;
- the exact infrared variables are weighted by
  \(\omega^{1/2},\omega^{1/2},\omega^{3/2}\);
- local mass/Bach cocycle equality is only the first gate toward a global
  Jost or QNM-slope identity;
- rational reducibility away from the same-sign terminal solutions is
  confined to \(4i\omega\in\mathbb Z\).

It does not classify the entire complex reducibility locus or construct a
canonical physical \(C\).
