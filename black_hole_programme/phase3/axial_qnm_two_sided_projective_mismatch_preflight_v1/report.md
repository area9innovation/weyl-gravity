# Two-sided physical projective mismatch preflight

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `REDUCED-MODE`.

The horizon and outgoing reduced logarithmic derivatives use opposite phases. Their common physical mismatch is therefore `Delta=q_H-q_out+2*I*omega`; its tau and omega derivatives were assembled on all 16 panels.

Boundary nonvanishing passed on 0/16 panels. The serialized endpoint artifacts retain independent midpoint/radius remainders but not a common cross-endpoint affine omega generator. Summing those certified remainders makes every panel contain zero. The computation therefore stops at the first gate; argument-principle, K0 and interval-Newton gates were not run.
