# Infinity physical-plane consumer

The full \(12\times12\) moving-frame factor rail certifies exact radial
coverage and factorwise invertibility.  Its final interval hull is too
wrapped to classify endpoint flux.  It is therefore an input and independent
check, not the physical endpoint classifier.

The rank-usable consumer propagates the two finite formal infinity trace
planes separately:

\[
 I^-=\operatorname{span}(XI0,XI1,EI0),\qquad
 I^+=\operatorname{span}(XI2,XI3,EI2).
\]

Each is a three-complex-dimensional, six-real-dimensional plane in the
twelve-real-dimensional axial state.  The frozen factorization is

\[
 Y=G_c(Z)A,
\]

where \(Z\) is a Grassmann graph in one of the 20 complex three-plane charts
and \(A\) is a separate \(6\times6\) amplitude.  Recharting and radial
propagation use

\[
 Z'=VU^{-1},\quad A'=UA,
\]

and

\[
 M=\Phi_{II}+\Phi_{IJ}Z,\quad
 N=\Phi_{JI}+\Phi_{JJ}Z,\quad
 Z'=NM^{-1},\quad A'=MA.
\]

The implementation will use the validated shared degree-two Taylor matrix
kernel.  Retaining the quadratic generator term prevents every
\(A_1B_1e^2\) product from being discarded into an uncorrelated remainder
after each multiplication.

The terminal gate is deliberately stronger than two separate rank-six
checks.  After reconstructing both \(12\times6\) planes at \(r=4\), the
consumer must certify

\[
 \operatorname{rank}[Y^-_4\;Y^+_4]=12
\]

uniformly on every frequency child.  Only then may a future-horizon-regular
plane be decomposed into the two asymptotic classes.

The exact selector, realification, state-order and chart contract is stored
in `artifacts/infinity_physical_plane_contract.json`.  It does not yet
establish propagated endpoint planes or any scattering, flux, ghost, CPT or
unitarity statement.
