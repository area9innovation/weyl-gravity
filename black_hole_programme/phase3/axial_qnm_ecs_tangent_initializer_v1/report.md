# Intrinsic tangent ECS initializer

For the canonical projective representative

\[
\mathcal I_{\rm red}
=\frac{i(r-2)(2\omega^2r+3\omega^2+12)}
{5\omega r^4},
\]

differentiating the contracted spin-two Volterra equation gives a second
contracted inhomogeneous equation.  Exact rational majorants on the
\(\pi/4\) ECS ray yield

\[
|v_\tau(45)|\le0.041595,\qquad
|\partial_xv_\tau(45)|\le0.001458.
\]

These bounds are uniform and analytic on the complete proposed QNM disk.
They certify the tangent initializer in the canonical reduced cocycle gauge.
Converting it to the factor-frame coefficient \(b\) still requires the
explicit analytic endpoint gauge and normalization term.
