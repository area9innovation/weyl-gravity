# Exact projective sensitivity system

For
\[
v_{xx}-2i\omega v_x-Vv+\tau\mathcal I v=0
\]
the \(q=v_x/v\) chart obeys
\[
q_x=2i\omega q+V-\tau\mathcal I-q^2.
\]
At \(\tau=0\),
\[
\eta_x=(2i\omega-2q)\eta-\mathcal I,\qquad
\xi_x=(2i\omega-2q)\xi+2iq.
\]

In the reciprocal chart \(p=1/q\),
\[
p_x=1-2i\omega p-Vp^2+\tau\mathcal I p^2,
\]
with
\[
\eta_p=-\eta_q/q^2,\qquad \xi_p=-\xi_q/q^2.
\]

The three coordinates must be switched by one correlated Möbius operation.
The exact projective mismatch derivatives at a match point are
\(\Delta_\tau=\eta_H-\eta_\infty\) and
\(\Delta_\omega=\xi_H-\xi_\infty\), giving
\(\omega_\tau=-\Delta_\tau/\Delta_\omega\) at a simple zero.  No endpoint
line or contour value is evaluated here.
