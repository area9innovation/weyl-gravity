## What was established

1. **Reduced equations.** For `diag(-a(r), b(r), r^2, r^2 sin^2 th)` the
   Bach tensor is diagonal, exactly trace-free, exactly divergence-free,
   with `B_phph = sin^2 th · B_thth`: two independent rows before conformal
   gauge fixing.  The exact rows are stored in the certificate.
2. **Laurent-class completeness.** In the conformal gauge `b = 1/a` with
   `B = w - u/r + gamma r - k r^2 + c2/r^2 + c3 r^3`, a Groebner basis of
   the exact coefficient ideal is `{c2, c3, w^2 + 3 u gamma - 1}`:
   Bach-flatness holds **iff** `c2 = c3 = 0` and `w^2 + 3 u gamma = 1`.
   Completeness beyond this Laurent class (Riegert's theorem) is a
   literature target, not a repository result.
3. **Mannheim–Kazanas reproduction (required benchmark).**
   `B = 1 - 3βγ - β(2-3βγ)/r + γr - k r^2` is exactly Bach-flat; the
   parameter dictionary to Mannheim–Kazanas (1989) is the identity.
   Schwarzschild (`γ=k=0`, Ricci-flat) and Schwarzschild–(A)dS (`γ=0`,
   `R_ab = 3k g_ab`) pass as Einstein controls.  The MK chart covers the
   `w`-sheet through `w=1`; the complementary sheet (e.g. `w=-1, γ=0`) is a
   Kantowski–Sachs-type branch with `r` timelike, outside the MK chart.
4. **Einstein/extra split.** The trace-free Ricci defect factors exactly:
   `E_thth = -γ(r-3β)/2`.  Within the family: Einstein ⟺ `γ = 0`.  Every
   `γ ≠ 0` member is a genuine extra Weyl branch background.
5. **Residual gauge structure.** The form-preserving residual
   (diffeo × Weyl) transformations are generated by a dilation and the map
   `r = ρ/(1-cρ), Ω = 1-cρ`.  On the cubic `Q(x) = -u x^3 + w x^2 + γx - k`
   (`x = 1/r`, `u = β(2-3βγ)`, `w = 1-3βγ`) the c-map acts **exactly** as
   the translation `x → x - c` and the dilation as `Q(x) → λ^2 Q(x/λ)`.
   Orbit rank is 2, so the family has a single continuous gauge invariant
   `J = u^2 · disc(Q)`, and `disc(Q) = 0` is precisely the degenerate-
   horizon locus: the gauge-invariant content of the family is its horizon
   root structure plus `J`.  Caveat: `Ω = 1-cρ` vanishes at `ρ = 1/c`, so
   the c-map is a local equivalence only; it is not used to identify
   horizon geometries globally.
6. **Horizon fixture.** `(β, γ, k) = (3/2, 12/19, 1/19)` gives
   `rB(r) = -(1/19)(r-1)(r-3)(r-8)`: inner horizon `r=1`, black-hole event
   horizon `r=3` (chart surface gravity `5/57`), cosmological horizon `r=8`
   (`-35/304`), all simple roots, static regions `(0,1)` and `(3,8)`,
   non-Einstein witness `E_thth = -3(2r-9)/19 ≠ 0`, `J = 2822400/47045881`.
7. **Singularities.** Exact invariants on the family:
   `R = 6(βγ - γr + 2kr^2)/r^2`, `WeylSq = 12β^2(2-3βγ+γr)^2/r^6`,
   plus RicciSq and Kretschmann.  Poles only at `r = 0` (curvature
   singularity iff `β ≠ 0`; scalar-curvature singularity iff `βγ ≠ 0`);
   every invariant is finite at every horizon.
8. **Horizon-regular chart.** In ingoing Eddington–Finkelstein form
   `ds^2 = -B dv^2 + 2 dv dr + r^2 dΩ^2` the family is exactly Bach-flat,
   the chart metric is smooth with `det g = -r^4 sin^2 th`, and
   `g^rr = B(r)` exactly, so constant-`r` hypersurfaces are null precisely
   at the roots of `B`.
9. **Mutations.** Dropping the `-3βγ` correlation, or adding a `r^-2`
   tail to the fixture, produces exactly nonzero Bach components in both
   the producer and the independent verifier pipelines.

## What was NOT established

- completeness of the Bach vacuum beyond the Laurent ansatz class;
- any physical matter/clock conformal frame (horizon regularity in a
  physical frame is therefore open);
- a horizon/infinity phase space, boundary terms, differentiable charges,
  flux balance, or first law (BH-1);
- a causal exterior initial-boundary theorem, perturbations, quasinormal
  modes, stability, or any quantum statement (BH-2+).

The chart surface gravities use `κ = B'(r_h)/2` for the chart vector
`∂_v`; the family is generically not asymptotically flat, so no preferred
normalization of the horizon generator is claimed.
