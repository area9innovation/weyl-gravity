# Automatic-face rotation-zero links

Candidates 17--21 each have one two-ray scalar-cone face on which a complete
factor of the bilinear cross-fibre resonance vanishes.  At any fixed nonzero
occupation on a relative support stratum of such a face, quotienting the
fixed node-norm spheres by their independent phase circles gives a compact
connected product of complex projective spaces.  The action-derived current
induces nonzero signed Fubini--Study forms; the negative Einstein-minus
factor reverses orientation but does not spoil symplectic nondegeneracy.

The diagonal lifted `SO(3)` action is Hamiltonian with moment map
`(mu_J1,mu_J2,mu_J3)`.  Its moment map is proper because the projective
product is compact.  Theorem 1.1(b) of Lerman, Meinrenken, Tolman and
Woodward, *Non-abelian convexity by symplectic cuts*, Topology 37 (1998)
245--259, DOI `10.1016/S0040-9383(97)00030-X`, makes every moment-map fibre
connected.  The certified axisymmetric section proves that the zero fibre is
nonempty.  Restoring the connected node-phase torus therefore leaves the
unquotiented fixed-occupation link connected.

This classifies all nonzero fixed-occupation rotation-zero links on the five
automatic faces.  Candidate 16 has only the origin as an automatic face.  No
claim is made for the active resonance varieties: their restricted current
must first be shown symplectic before the connectedness theorem can be used.

The external theorem was checked against the primary arXiv source
`dg-ga/9603015`: its Theorem 1.1 assumes a connected symplectic orbifold, a
Hamiltonian action of a compact Lie group and a proper moment map, and part
(b) states that every moment-map fibre is connected.  Here connectedness and
the Hamiltonian `SO(3)` action come from the fixed-support projective product,
and compactness supplies properness.
