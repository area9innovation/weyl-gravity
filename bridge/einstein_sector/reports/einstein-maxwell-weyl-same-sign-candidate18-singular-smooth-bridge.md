# Candidate 18 singular-to-smooth bridge

Although candidate 18's singular subquotient has two pieces, they are joined
inside the complete fixed-occupation rotation-zero carrier.

For `0<=theta<=pi/2`, let `w=(cos(theta),sin(theta))`.  With positive internal
metric `A` and negative absolute weight `beta`, set

```text
u = sqrt(6 N_plus/(w^T A w)) w,
v = sqrt(6 N_minus/beta) w.
```

Assign the two entries of `u` and `v` to the two parity factors, always on the
central `m=0` angular vector, and set all spectators to zero.  The positive
and negative occupations are constant exactly, and all lifted rotational
moment maps vanish.

At the endpoints one parity factor is zero, giving one point in each singular
component.  In the open interval both rank-one factors are nonzero, so the
complex resonance carrier is smooth.  This proves the two singular pieces
are connected through the full zero fibre at every positive occupation.

It does not prove the entire zero fibre connected or classify the global
presymplectic leaf space.
