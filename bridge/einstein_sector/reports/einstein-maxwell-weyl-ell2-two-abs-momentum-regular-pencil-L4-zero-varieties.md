# Regular-pencil L4 zero varieties

Candidates 7, 11 and 19 share a regular four-dimensional internal matrix
pencil, although they remain distinct circumference backgrounds.  After the
common positive coefficient conversion, all four parity matrices are
invertible.  The squared-pencil operator

`T=C_ap^{-1} C_pa C_aa^{-1} C_pp`

has positive trace and determinant and positive characteristic discriminant
on each fibre, with exact rational interval witnesses.  Hence its two
eigenvalues are distinct and positive, and the full pencil has four distinct
real roots.

Over the binary-quartic model the target equations diagonalize to four
products `(S_axial+z_i S_polar) D_i=0`.  The polynomial ring is an integral
domain.  The all-`m` resonance variety therefore has exactly six linear
irreducible components: a dimension-20 scalar-fibre-zero component, a
dimension-10 doublet-fibre-zero component, and four dimension-10 real mixed
eigenline components.

Candidate 13 is the sole remaining cross-fibre ideal.  Same-fibre sources,
Taub intersections, bounded and smooth-secular corrections, residual
observables, causal maps and quantum interpretations remain fail-closed.
