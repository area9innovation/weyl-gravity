# Collision scalar-separation classification

The scalar bounded receiver now has an exact answer on all 21 isolated
`ell=2`, two-absolute-momentum collision backgrounds.

For candidate indices 1--15 the signed momenta are `(1,-2)`.  In fact one
construction works for every `rho>0`.  Let `t1,t2` be the midpoints between
the `q_minus` and `p_extra` frequencies on the two absolute momenta.  The
covector `(a,b,c)=(1,t2/2-t1,-t1*t2/2)` gives

```text
Q_1(omega)  = (omega-t1)(omega+t2/2),
Q_-2(omega) = (omega-t2)(omega+2*t1).
```

After the negative `q_minus` current sign is included, it is strictly
positive on every nonzero `q_minus`, `p_extra`, and `q_plus`
occupation on both fibres, in both parities and for every `m`.  Since
`D` lies in the span of `mu_H`, `mu_Px`, and `R_c`, their common zero is the
origin.  The complete bounded generic cone is therefore `{0}` at each of
these 15 distinct circumferences, independently of its finite-frequency
resonance ideal.

For indices 16--21 the momenta are `(1,2)`.  Each row instead has an exact
positive four-term Farkas dependence among the signed shell feature vectors
`(omega^2,n omega,n^2)`.  This both rules out a strict scalar separator and
constructs a nonzero scalar `H/Px/Rc` common-zero occupation.  The full
bounded cone is not thereby certified: its candidate-specific resonance
functionals must still vanish.

The result keeps all 21 `rho` values as distinct backgrounds.  Exceptional
and generalized-zero carriers, all-orders integration, causal correction,
residual observables, particles and quantum states remain outside scope.
