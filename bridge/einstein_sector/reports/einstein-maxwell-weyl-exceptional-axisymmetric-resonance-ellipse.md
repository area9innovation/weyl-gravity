# Axisymmetric exceptional resonance ellipse

The complete `L=1` difference matrix, exceptional `L=2` self-pairings and
invertible `d` control maps admit a nonzero resonance-compatible family:

```text
x=r_x*s,  p=i*r_p*s,  |s|=1,
16*r_x^2+3*r_p^2=115*d^2.
```

The two polar `ell=2` controls are fixed explicitly; axial controls uniquely
remove the mixed-parity defect.  This is not yet a tangent-cone branch.  The
action-derived exceptional and generic-extra currents make its Hamiltonian
moment map strictly negative, while `P_x` and rotations vanish at `k=m=0`.
An opposite-sign Einstein-minus occupation is therefore necessary, and its
new cross sources are the next bounded gate.  All-m, causal, residual and
quantum claims remain open.
