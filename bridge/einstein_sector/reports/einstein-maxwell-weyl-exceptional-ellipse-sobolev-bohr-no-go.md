# The complete k=0 obstruction survives a Sobolev--Bohr completion

The smooth Wiener hypothesis is stronger than the coefficientwise Fredholm
argument needs.  Fix an integer `s>=6` and close finite Einstein-minus sums in
the order-four graph norm

```text
max_(0<=j<=4) sup_t ||partial_t^j u(t)||_(H^(s-j)(S2)).
```

This gives bounded uniformly almost-periodic Sobolev fields while retaining
every branch, parity, `ell` and `m` label.  It strictly contains the
all-orders weighted-`l1` Wiener class.

On `S2`, this regularity makes every product occurring in the fourth-order
Weyl--Maxwell Hessian continuous into `H^(s-4)`.  Banach-valued Bohr-frequency
extraction and pairing with a smooth spherical adjoint are continuous.
Bochner--Fejer finite approximants therefore reduce every possible competitor
on a minus shell to the already certified finite pair census.  No competitor
survives the limit, leaving

```text
d*C_parity(lambda)*c_(ell,m,parity).
```

Every nonzero ellipse point has `d!=0`, both pivots are nonzero for all
physical `ell>=2`, and Taub balance requires at least one nonzero minus
coefficient.  Bounded compatibility forces all of them to vanish.  Hence the
complete declared `k=0` carrier remains `OBSTRUCTED` in this finite-order
Sobolev--Bohr topology.

This is not a sharp energy-space theorem.  Low regularity, infinite secular
inversion, nonzero momentum, causal propagation, residual descent and quantum
interpretation remain fail-closed.
