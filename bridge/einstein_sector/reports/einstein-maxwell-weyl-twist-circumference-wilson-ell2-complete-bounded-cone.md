# Circumference and Wilson spectators over the twist-wave cone

At `k=0`, the constant circumference coordinate `c` and flat Wilson holonomy
`W_x` form an exact product factor over the certified `A,B` plus complete
`ell=2` bounded cone:

```text
Z2_bounded(c,W_x,A,B,wave)
  = R_c x R_Wx x Z2_bounded(A,B,wave).
```

The circumference statement follows from the exact circle-radius family,
which transports every `k=0` oscillator with a bounded mixed correction; its
direct generalized-zero source has no `c`-times-twist term.  The Wilson
statement is stronger: `delta F(W_x dx)=0`, so every mixed Euler source
vanishes identically.

Consequently `c` and `W_x` are arbitrary, `B=0`, and the remaining equations
are precisely the constant-position `q/p` incidence equations and
`mu_H=mu_J1=mu_J2=mu_J3=0`.  The radion position `a`, circumference velocity
`d`, and electric tangent `Q_e` are not included; they have genuine dynamical
source columns and remain separate gates.
