# Universal signed 1:-2 scalar separator

The candidate-13 bounded-origin theorem extends to every positive circle
parameter on the complete generic signed `n=1,-2` carrier.

Let `t1` be the midpoint between the `q_minus` and `p_extra` frequencies on
`n=1`, and `t2` the corresponding midpoint on `n=-2`.  With

```text
B=t2/2-t1,  C=-t1*t2/2,
D=S_H+B*S_P+C*S_R,
```

the two shell polynomials factor exactly as

```text
Q_1(omega)  =(omega-t1)(omega+t2/2),
Q_-2(omega) =(omega-t2)(omega+2t1).
```

The mass ordering puts `q_minus` below each midpoint and `p_extra,q_plus`
above it.  Hence `Q` is negative on the negative-current branch and positive
on both positive-current branches.  Every absolute-current occupation has a
strictly positive coefficient in `D`.  Therefore

```text
{mu_H=mu_Px=R_c=0}={0}
```

for every `rho>0` on this opposite-signed carrier.  In particular, the first
15 exact admissible two-fibre collision circumferences have real bounded
generic tangent cone `{0}`.  Rotation and finite-frequency resonance equations
are redundant for this no-go.  Candidates 16--21 use signed momenta `1,2`, so
the midpoint factorization does not apply; they remain `OPEN`.

Other momentum ratios, exceptional or generalized-zero inputs, smooth and
causal correction classes, all-orders integration, residual observables and
quantum claims remain outside scope.
