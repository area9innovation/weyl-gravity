# Two target-doublet L3 resonance varieties

Candidates 1 and 16 have scalar inputs and two target-adjoint components per
parity.  Exact row reduction leaves the same two first-transvectant equations
on both physical fibres.  Their `sqrt(3)` combinations diagonalize into two
binary-quartic Jacobians.  A Jacobian vanishes exactly when its equal-degree
quartics are proportional, so each equation defines a `5 x 2` rank-one cone.
The full all-`m` variety is their irreducible Cartesian product, complex
dimension 12 in dimension 20.  Aggregate progress leaves nine fibrewise
ideals open; same-fibre sources, Taub joins and correction classes remain open.
