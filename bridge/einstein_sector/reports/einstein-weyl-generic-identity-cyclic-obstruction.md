# Generic fixed-identity cyclic obstruction

The certified generic axial and polar polynomial chain maps use the physical
identity field inclusion.  On solution cohomology their action-derived forms
satisfy

`iota^* Omega_WM - Omega_EM = Omega_EM(., D .)`,

where `D=R-I=(3/2)(M-lambda I)`.  In each parity,

`D^2=(9/2) lambda I`, `det D=-9 lambda/2`.

Thus the pairing defect is nonzero and nonradical for every physical
`lambda=ell(ell+1)>=6` and every allowed compact momentum.  A strict cyclic
enhancement that keeps the identity field map and the standard action
pairings would induce a symplectic solution map, so it is obstructed.

This does not classify a corrected nonidentity field map, a cyclic morphism up
to chain homotopy, or pairing-changing improvements.  Those remain separate
open gates.
