# Exceptional `ell=1` radion/velocity pivots

The direct four-dimensional Weyl--Maxwell Euler source was recomputed for the
radion position `a` and circumference velocity `d` crossed with the axial and
polar exceptional dipoles at `omega_e^2=4/3`.  The action-row and harmonic
normalizations are the exceptional `lambda=2` ones, not a specialization of
an `ell=2` source payload.

Against physical adjoint witnesses `(0,-1/3,0,1)` in the axial block and
`(0,1,0,0)` in the polar block, the projected sources are

```text
axial: a -> 4*i*(4*sqrt(3)*t+15*i)/9,  d -> 8*sqrt(3)*i/9,
polar: a -> -i*(2*sqrt(3)*t-3*i),      d -> -sqrt(3)*i.
```

Thus the leading `a*t` coefficient is nonzero in both parities, and after
`a=0` the `d` coefficient is also nonzero.  Rotational multiplicity one
promotes both statements from the direct `m=0` fixture to every `m`.

This does not yet solve the mixed bounded cone.  The live difference channel

```text
omega_(ell=2 extra)-omega_(exceptional)=omega_(exceptional)
```

allows an exceptional-times-`ell=2`-extra source to enter the same `L=1`
adjoint block and potentially screen the constant `d` pivot.  The next gate
is the eight axial/polar collision columns, solved jointly with the `d`
control column and the exceptional `L=2` self-defect.  Nonzero momentum,
causal propagation, residual descent and quantum interpretation remain open.
