# Finite-generic bounded zero-frequency cokernel

For arbitrary finite sums of generic Weyl--Maxwell oscillators, the
zero-frequency homogeneous correction operator has two dynamical invariants:

```text
D'''' = s_D,    -A_x'' = s_X.
```

On bounded finite-quasiperiodic corrections, their constant Fourier
coefficients are two formal mean cokernel directions.  The metric direction
is the circle-pressure functional

```text
R_c(u) = (1/2) sum_j k_j^2 h_j.
```

The Maxwell direction is present in the operator cokernel but absent from the
quadratic source map.  Integrating the exact Maxwell `x` equation over the
closed spatial slice makes it a total time derivative.  Hence the
zero-frequency coefficient of every finite Fourier bilinear vanishes.

Together with the exact static `L=1` and `L>=2` quotient theorems, this proves
that the bounded zero-frequency source is removable exactly when

```text
mu_H = mu_Px = mu_J1 = mu_J2 = mu_J3 = R_c = 0.
```

This theorem includes all generic `ell>=2` momenta, parities, magnetic quantum
numbers, degenerate polarizations, and relative phases.  It excludes
generalized-zero inputs and every nonzero-frequency resonance question.
