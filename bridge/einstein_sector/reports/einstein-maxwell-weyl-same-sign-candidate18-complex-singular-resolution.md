# Candidate 18 complex singular resolution

Candidate 18's complete complex carrier is

```text
C^10_spectator x Rank_{<=1}(5x2)_plus x Rank_{<=1}(5x2)_minus.
```

One rank-one cone has complex dimension six.  Its ten minor equations have
Jacobian rank four at every nonzero rank-one matrix and rank zero at the
vertex, so the vertex is its complete singular locus.  The standard
incidence resolution is

```text
Tot(O_P4(-1) direct-sum O_P4(-1)) -> Rank_{<=1}(5x2).
```

The complete candidate-18 singular locus therefore has exactly two
irreducible components:

```text
C^10 x {0} x R  and  C^10 x R x {0}.
```

Each has complex dimension sixteen and they meet in the ten-dimensional
spectator space.  Taking the product of the two incidence resolutions and
the spectator space gives a smooth connected 22-dimensional resolution with
connected fibres.

This retains all ten positive current-orthogonal spectators.  It is a
complex-algebraic input only: the real fixed-occupation current, node phases,
lifted rotations and global zero-fibre topology remain open.
