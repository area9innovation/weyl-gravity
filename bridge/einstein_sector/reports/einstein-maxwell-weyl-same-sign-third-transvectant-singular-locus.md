# Candidate-17/20 third-transvectant singular locus

For one parity factor let

```text
K_T3 = {(f,g) in C^5 x C^5 : T3(f,g)=0}.
```

The complete Jacobian loses rank precisely when a nonzero quadratic covector
`lambda` annihilates both linearized transvectant maps.  The corresponding
`5 x 5` matrix `B_lambda` is skew and has rank four for every nonzero
`lambda`.  Its kernel is generated by

```text
v(lambda) =
(3 l2^2, -3 l1 l2, l0 l2+2 l1^2, -3 l0 l1, 3 l0^2).
```

Thus

```text
Sing(K_T3) =
{(a v(lambda), b v(lambda)) : [lambda] in P^2, (a,b) in C^2}.
```

Geometrically, `f` and `g` are proportional squares of the same binary
quadratic.  The singular locus has complex dimension four, its
projectivization is the smooth connected Segre--Veronese variety
`P^2 x P^1`, and it has the incidence resolution

```text
Tot(O_P2(-2) direct-sum O_P2(-2)) -> Sing(K_T3).
```

For the two parity factors, the complete singular locus has exactly two
irreducible components,

```text
(S_plus x K_minus) union (K_plus x S_minus),
```

each of dimension eleven, meeting in the eight-dimensional product
`S_plus x S_minus`.

This classifies the complex singular carrier for candidates 17 and 20.  It
does not yet intersect these strata with the real fixed-occupation
conditions, quotient node phases, impose lifted rotations, or establish
global connectedness.
