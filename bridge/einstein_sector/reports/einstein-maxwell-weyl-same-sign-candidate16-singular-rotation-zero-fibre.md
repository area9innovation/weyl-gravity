# Candidate 16 singular strata and rotation-zero fibre

Candidate 16 is the product of two rank-at-most-one `5 x 2`
determinantal cones.  A single factor is smooth away from its vertex: the
ten `2 x 2` minors have Jacobian rank four at rank one and rank zero at the
origin.  On the positive two-node-norm link, the product singular locus is
therefore exactly two disjoint endpoint strata, each isomorphic to `CP^4`.

Resolve each factor by

```text
Tot(O_CP4(-1) direct_sum O_CP4(-1)) -> RankLeq1(5x2).
```

The product resolution is smooth of complex dimension 12.  Imposing the two
positive node norms gives compact connected weighted `S^3 x S^3` fibres over
`CP^4 x CP^4`; the two node phases act freely.  The reduced resolution is
therefore a compact connected Kähler tenfold.  Its exceptional fibres over
the two target endpoint strata are connected `CP^4`s.

The lifted diagonal `SO(3)` action commutes with both node phases and the
resolution map is equivariant.  The imported axisymmetric bounded point makes
the zero fibre nonempty.  Kirwan connectedness makes the resolved moment-map
zero fibre connected, and its continuous image is the complete singular
target zero fibre.  Hence the candidate-16 fixed-occupation rotation-zero
link is connected without declaring the singular target an orbifold.

This does not glue different scalar occupations, perform final residual
descent, prove all-orders integration, or supply causal, observational or
quantum transport.
