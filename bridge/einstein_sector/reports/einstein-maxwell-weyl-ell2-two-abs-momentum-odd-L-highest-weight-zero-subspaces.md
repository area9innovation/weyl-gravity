# Odd-L highest-weight mixed zero subspaces

All nine cross-fibre resonance systems with target `L=1` or `L=3` contain a
nontrivial mixed all-`m` zero subspace.  Support both declared signed-frequency
input carriers at `m=2`.  Their tensor product has `M=4`, so its projection to
an odd target with `L<4` vanishes before any branch, parity or target-adjoint
coefficient matrix is applied.

This certifies mixed nonzero points on all three `L=1` and all six `L=3`
physical circumference fibres.  The `L=1` rows are typed carefully: their
second input is the declared negative-frequency carrier, and its real-tangent
positive-frequency conjugate has `m=-2`.  No carriers are identified merely
because their mode names match.

The result strengthens the warning supplied by the five scalar `L=4`
decompositions: obstruction of every branch-basis fixture is not an
all-amplitude no-go.  It does not classify the complete odd-`L` ideals,
same-fibre sources, Taub intersections, bounded or smooth-secular corrections,
residual descent, or causal and quantum maps.
