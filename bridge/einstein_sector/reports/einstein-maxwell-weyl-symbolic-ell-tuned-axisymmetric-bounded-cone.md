# All-ell tuned axisymmetric bounded cone

For each `ell>=2`, the complete bounded cone in the declared one-`|k|`,
axisymmetric q-minus/q-plus carrier is the origin plus two mixed-parity
components.  Put `r_ell=omega_minus/omega_plus`.  On either sheet the sharp
condition is

```text
(1-r_ell)/(1+r_ell) <= |p_+|^2/|p_-|^2 <= (1+r_ell)/(1-r_ell).
```

The q-plus occupations solving `H=P_x=0` are explicit and nonnegative exactly
on this interval.  The one-sided resonance planes meet the moment cone only
at the origin.  Relative phases remain arbitrary subject to the common sheet
sign.  This is a complete second-order cone only for the tuned axisymmetric
standard-branch carrier; multiple momenta, extra inputs, all-orders and causal
claims remain open.
