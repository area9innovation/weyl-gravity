# Exceptional dipole difference-frequency census

The exceptional dipole self-product resonates with the polar `L=2` extra
shell at frequency `2*omega_e=4/sqrt(3)`.  The earlier census left open
whether unequal-frequency `k=0` inputs could reach that same shell.

They cannot.  For two generic inputs, angular selection restricts their
angular momenta to `ell_2=ell_1+d`, `d=0,1,2`.  Across all nine primary-branch
pairs, exact elimination of the two square roots gives 27 integer
polynomials.  None has an integer root `ell_1>=2`.  If one input is a
physical or exceptional dipole, angular selection restricts the generic
partner to `ell=2,3`; all twelve exact residuals have minimal polynomials
with nonzero constant term.

Thus no difference-frequency `k=0` oscillator pair reaches the exceptional
`2*omega_e,L=2` target.  Combined with the positive-sum census, the sole
external `k=0` route is the already isolated generalized-zero global times
`ell=2` extra column.  This closes frequency arithmetic only: the live source
coefficient, its bounded common-zero locus, opposite nonzero momenta, causal
propagation and quantum interpretation remain open.
