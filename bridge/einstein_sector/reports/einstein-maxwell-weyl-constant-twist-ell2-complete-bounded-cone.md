# Complete constant-twist plus ell=2 bounded cone

For the carrier consisting of a constant axial twist position and the complete
axial/polar `ell=2,k=0` Weyl--Maxwell wave space, the bounded second-order
tangent cone is now explicit.

When the twist is nonzero, rotate it to the `z` axis.  The Einstein plus and
minus shells retain only their axial/polar `m=0` coefficients.  The extra
shell retains all four `m=0` coefficients and, at every nonzero `m`, only

```text
span{polar_e1, -4*sqrt(3)*axial_e1+15*polar_e2}.
```

These linear resonance restrictions must be intersected with the four exact
stabilizer equations `H=J_1=J_2=J_3=0`.  Their simultaneous vanishing is both
necessary and sufficient for a bounded correction in this carrier.  Wave
self-sources are covered by the fixed-ell theorem, twist self-sources by the
exact static branch, and all `L=1,3` twist--wave outputs are off every `p/q`
shell.

The cone is not merely the aligned face.  Equal extra amplitudes in the
`polar_e1` kernel direction at `m=+2` and `m=-2` have zero angular moment;
an Einstein-minus `m=0` occupation balances their energy exactly.  Thus
nonaxisymmetric waves survive the nonzero-twist second-order test.

The nonresonant step uses the actual reduced operators, not shell arithmetic
alone.  At `L=1`, the exceptional axial `h_t=0` and polar `U=0` determinants
are nonzero at every input frequency.  At `L=3`, the generic physical-ring
invariant factors `(1,1,p,pq)` make the determinant `p^2 q`, again nonzero.
The second-order expansion of the ungauged nonlinear Noether identity proves
that every on-shell quadratic source lies in the compatible source subspace,
so these quotient inverses apply.

The theorem does not include twist velocity or other homogeneous directions,
other angular momenta, nonzero momentum, unrestricted secular corrections,
causal propagation, all-orders integration, residual descent or quantum
states.
