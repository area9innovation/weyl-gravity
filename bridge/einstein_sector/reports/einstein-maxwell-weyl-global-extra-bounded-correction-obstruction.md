# Bounded-correction obstruction on the global--extra orbit

Every nonzero point of the certified homogeneous/twist--`ell=2` extra
common-zero orbit satisfies

```text
B^2 = Q_e^2/2 + (2/3)X,
X > 0.
```

Hence `B` cannot vanish.  The directly replayed twist self-source contains,
in the zero-frequency polar `L=2` `metric_00` row,

```text
-(21(A+B*t)^2+17B^2)/3,
```

whose quadratic coefficient is `-7B^2`.  On the orbit this becomes
`-(7/2)Q_e^2-(14/3)X`, which is strictly negative.  All other contributions
to this channel are either time independent or occur at frequencies
`omega_e` and `2*omega_e`; none can cancel the `t^2` coefficient.

The stationary linearized Weyl--Maxwell operator maps bounded finite-
quasiperiodic corrections with bounded derivatives to bounded sources.  It
therefore cannot reproduce this quadratic growth.  Thus every nonzero point
of the orbit is obstructed in the bounded/finite-quasiperiodic correction
class.

This does not obstruct smooth exponential-polynomial corrections, where
polynomial right inverses are admissible.  Their complete mixed construction
remains the next gate.  The causal/retarded class remains
`NO_CERTIFIED_MAP`.
