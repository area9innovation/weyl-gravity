# Candidate-13 pure-extra Taub join

The prime candidate-13 resonance cone is supported entirely on generic
`p_extra` modes: two internal polarizations in each parity on the two signed
compact momentum fibres `n=1` and `n=-2`.  It therefore lies inside the
domain of the complete generic pure-extra moment-map theorem.

For a real tangent represented by positive-frequency coefficients `c_j`, the
time-translation moment map restricts to

`mu_H=-(L/4) sum_j omega_j^2 c_j^dagger (G_X tensor W_2)c_j`.

Both axial and polar extra Gram matrices have inertia `(2,0)`, `W_2` is
positive definite, and `omega_j^2=k_j^2+16/3` is positive.  Distinct compact
momentum blocks are orthogonal.  Hence `mu_H<0` on every nonzero real tangent
in the complete declared ambient carrier, not merely on a dense subset of the
resonance cone.

It follows immediately that

`Z_res(candidate13) intersect {mu_H=0}={0}`.

The common zero of all five compact stabilizer moment maps is therefore also
the origin.  No same-fibre source calculation is required for this no-go:
the constant-lapse adjoint-cokernel pairing already obstructs every nonzero
real candidate-13 pure-extra tangent.  The obstruction applies both to
bounded/finite-quasiperiodic and smooth-secular correction classes; allowing
secular propagation terms does not change a stabilizer Taub pairing.

This does not classify the larger carrier obtained by adjoining `q`-primary
Einstein modes, where the time Taub form is indefinite and cancellations can
occur.  Same-fibre source matrices, charge-varying families, causal/retarded
corrections, final residual observables and quantum states remain open or
have no certified map.
