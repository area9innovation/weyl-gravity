# No finite Einstein-minus dressing rescues the ellipse

Let `w_l=sqrt(l(l+1)-sqrt(2*l(l+1)))`.  Exact derivative inequalities give
`0<l-w_l<1/2` and `1<w'(x)<2/sqrt(3)` for `x>=2`.  Hence

```text
w_(a+b-1) < w_a+w_b < w_(a+b).
```

There are no angularly allowed three-minus sum or difference resonances.  The
upper derivative bound also prevents an exceptional `ell=1` or control
`ell=2` oscillator from shifting one occupied minus shell into another.

Therefore the nonzero `d`-cross map acts independently on every occupied
minus `(ell,m,parity)` block.  Since every ellipse point has `d!=0` and charge
balance requires at least one minus coefficient, every arbitrary finite
Einstein-minus dressing is bounded-obstructed.  Smooth secular extension is
still certified.  Additional nonminus carriers and infinite completions
remain open.
