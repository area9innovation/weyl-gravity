# No identity resonance on the first two-|k| carrier

Take the complete generic `ell=2` primary inventory on compact momentum fibres
`|n|=1` and `|n|=2`.  The arbitrary-momentum divisor reduces all cross-fibre
pairs to 198 canonical rows:

```text
2 relative spatial signs
times 3 first branches
times 3 second branches
times 11 target shells at L=1,2,3,4.
```

Every row was evaluated exactly.  None has both the linear circumference
coefficient and constant divisor coefficient equal to zero.  Hence there is no
cross-fibre shell collision that persists for every circumference.  The
nonzero-Fourier `L=0` output is separately exact.

It follows that outside a finite algebraic set of circumferences every
nonzero-frequency `|n|=1` times `|n|=2` source block is off shell.  This is not
yet the two-fibre tangent cone: at each isolated candidate circumference the
projected quadratic source must still be computed, and same-fibre and
zero-frequency Taub rows remain separate.
