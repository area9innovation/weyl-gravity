# Additional dipole oscillators do not rescue the ellipse

Adjoin arbitrary finite physical and extra `ell=1,k=0` oscillators to the
exceptional resonance ellipse, standard global data and a smooth Wiener--Bohr
Einstein-minus sum.  Both dipole oscillator blocks have the same negative
Hamiltonian-moment-map sign as the ellipse, so they increase the need for an
opposite-sign minus occupation.

Their frequencies are `2/sqrt(3)` and `2`.  An `ell=1` product can shift a
minus harmonic only to `L=ell-1,ell,ell+1`, while every adjacent minus-shell
gap is strictly less than `2/sqrt(3)`.  Hence no ell1--minus sum or difference
can reach another angularly allowed minus shell.  A separate exact audit of
all ell1--ell1, ell1--control and constant-twist--ell1 frequencies has nonzero
residual against the only possible `L=2,3` minus targets.

The nonzero ellipse `d` pivot therefore remains isolated and forces every
required minus coefficient to vanish.  The bounded smooth uniformly
almost-periodic class is `OBSTRUCTED`.  The remaining possible rescue sector
starts at generic `ell>=2` Einstein-plus and extra-primary oscillators.
