# Zero homogeneous Einstein--Weyl solution cofiber

In the smooth polynomial generalized-zero class, a global homogeneous gauge
slice leaves exactly two invariants: `D=C-K` and `A_x`.  The complete target
equations are `D''''=0` and `A_x''=0`, so their quotient kernel has six
coordinates.

The Einstein--Maxwell coordinates map to the four metric-kernel coefficients
by `(d0,d1,d2,d3)=(c-a,d-b,a,b/3)`.  This map has determinant `1/3`; `Q_e`
and `W_x` map identically.  Hence the Einstein image exhausts the homogeneous
Weyl--Maxwell quotient and the solution cofiber is zero.

This does not make the identity map symplectic.  The action-derived forms are
related by `R=I+N`, `rank(N)=2`, `N^2=0`, with the certified unipotent
symplectomorphism `S=I+N/2`.

The theorem remains `ONSHELL_MAP_ONLY`.  Off-shell global chain maps, large
gauge transformations, final residual descent, bounded-time truncation and
causal/quantum interpretations remain open.
