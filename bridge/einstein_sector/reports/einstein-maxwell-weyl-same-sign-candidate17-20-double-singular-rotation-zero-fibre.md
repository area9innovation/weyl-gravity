# Candidate-17/20 connected double-singular rotation-zero hub

The common intersection of the two candidate-17/20 singular components is

```text
S_plus x S_minus.
```

Resolve each common-square cone by

```text
Tot(O_P2(-2) direct-sum O_P2(-2)) -> S.
```

For the product, fixing the total negative and positive occupations and
quotienting their two free phases gives

```text
P(L_plus direct-sum L_minus)_negative
  fibre-product over P2_plus x P2_minus
P(L_plus direct-sum L_minus)_positive.
```

This is a compact connected Kähler manifold of complex dimension six.  The
lifted diagonal `SO(3)` action is Hamiltonian and equivariant with the
resolution.  The explicit central-`m` positive-occupation witness makes its
zero fibre nonempty, and Kirwan connectedness makes that fibre connected.
Connected incidence fibres pass connectedness to the complete
double-singular target hub.

Thus candidates 17 and 20 each have one connected double-singular
rotation-zero hub at every fixed positive occupation.  The theorem does not
yet show that every point or connected component of either larger singular
component reaches this hub.
