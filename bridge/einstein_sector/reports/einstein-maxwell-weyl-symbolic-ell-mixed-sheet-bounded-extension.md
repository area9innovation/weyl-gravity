# Symbolic-ell mixed-sheet bounded extension

nonzero wave-only bounded second-order jet.  Choose equal opposite-momentum
q-minus coefficients with

```text
a_+/-=sigma*sqrt(ell*(ell+1)/2)*p_+/-,  sigma=+1 or -1,
```

and add equal `+k/-k` q-plus occupation to cancel the negative q-minus
Hamiltonian.  Axisymmetry kills rotations and equal signed occupations kill
compact momentum.

The complete standard-branch census proves that the only nonzero-frequency
collision is the q-minus `L=2*ell` p shell.  Both adjoint projections on that
shell vanish on either mixed sheet.  The complete finite-generic bounded
cokernel theorem then supplies a bounded finite-quasiperiodic correction.

This proves second-order survival for two explicit Einstein-sector families
at every generic angular degree.  It does not classify the full amplitude
cone, prove all-orders integration, join different absolute momenta, or
construct a causal/retarded, residual, observational or quantum map.
