# No single Einstein-minus mode rescues the exceptional ellipse

The bounded obstruction extends from the explicit axial `ell=2` endpoint to
the entire axisymmetric resonance ellipse.

Every ellipse point has `d!=0` and a strictly negative Hamiltonian moment
map.  A single real axisymmetric Einstein-minus mode at any `ell>=2`, in
either parity, can be scaled to cancel that charge without introducing
momentum or rotation charge.  However, the exact generic-`lambda` cross
theorem gives a nonzero `d` pivot in every physical harmonic.  Its triangular
shell ideal forces `d=0` whenever the dressing amplitude is nonzero.

Thus every single-mode Einstein-minus dressing is obstructed for bounded or
finite-quasiperiodic corrections, while the same stabilizer-balanced tangent
has a certified smooth exponential-polynomial correction with a secular
term.

This theorem does not cover simultaneous multiple minus modes or additional
carriers.  Such enlarged dressings could in principle place more than one
quadratic pair on the same shell functional and require a separate joint
source calculation.
