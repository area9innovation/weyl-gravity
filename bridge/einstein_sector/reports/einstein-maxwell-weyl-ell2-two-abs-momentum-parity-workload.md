# The 21 isolated rows require 164 reduced source coefficients

Exact parity and angular selection removes half of the naive input/output
parity assignments but does not delete any of the 21 all-`m` shell rows.
Same-parity inputs land in polar even-`L` or axial odd-`L` targets; cross-parity
inputs land in axial even-`L` or polar odd-`L` targets.

After retaining the two generic `p`-primary multiplicities, the dynamical
workload is 164 action-derived scalar adjoint coefficients: 108 at `L=4`, 44
at `L=3`, and 12 at exceptional `L=1`.  The 56 odd-`L` coefficients cannot be
tested with an axisymmetric `m_1=m_2=0` fixture.  No source zero, extension or
obstruction is inferred by this combinatorial reduction.
