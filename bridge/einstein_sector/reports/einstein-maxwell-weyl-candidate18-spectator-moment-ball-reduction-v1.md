# Candidate-18 spectator moment-ball reduction

The exact candidate-18 gate contains ten complex spectator amplitudes: two
complete spin-two multiplets.  They are not discarded.  Instead, their
fixed-norm lifted-rotation moment problem can be solved exactly.

If \(H_s=|s_1|^2+|s_2|^2\), then

\[
 \left\{M_s:\ |s|^2=H_s\right\}
 =
 \left\{\nu\in\mathbb R^3:\ |\nu|\leq 2H_s\right\}.
\]

The operator-norm bound of the spin-two generators proves necessity.  A
superposition of the \(m=0\) and \(m=2\) weight vectors realizes every radius
on one axis, and rotation covariance realizes every direction.  The fixed
moment fibres are connected by the connected-fibre theorem for compact
Hamiltonian \(SO(3)\) actions on \(\mathbb{CP}^9\), together with the connected
Hopf fibre.

Consequently, with

\[
 H_s=6N_+-H_f,
\]

the complete 60-real-coordinate fibre projects exactly to the
40-real-coordinate active rank-one carrier cut out by

\[
 H_s\geq0,\qquad |M_f|^2\leq4H_s^2.
\]

Every active point satisfying these inequalities has a nonempty connected
spectator fibre, and every full point projects to this set.  The positive-node
phase and lifted rotations remain in an equivariant associated-fibre
reconstruction.

This is a strict reduction of the unresolved gate, not its final
classification.  The active real radical, stabilizer strata, associated
compact-group quotient and global bridge-incidence question remain open.
