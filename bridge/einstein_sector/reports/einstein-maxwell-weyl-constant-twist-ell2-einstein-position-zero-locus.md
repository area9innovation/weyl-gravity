# Constant-twist position kernels on the ell=2 Einstein shells

Direct four-dimensional Weyl--Maxwell expansion gives the same projected
parity-incidence matrix on the Einstein minus and plus shells:

```text
Q_minus = Q_plus = [[0,216/5],[432/5,0]],
det(Q_plus/minus)=-93312/25.
```

The plus result is the exact `sqrt(3) -> -sqrt(3)` involution of the direct
minus-shell rows.  Since the matrix is invertible, the only kernel comes from
the angular factor in the unique `V_1 tensor V_2 -> V_2` intertwiner.  For a
nonzero twist rotated to the `z` axis, `A_hat dot J_2` vanishes only at `m=0`.
Thus each Einstein shell retains precisely its axial and polar axisymmetric
coefficients: two complex dimensions out of ten.  The combined plus/minus
Einstein kernel has complex dimension four out of twenty.

This is a shellwise bounded-resonance theorem.  It does not yet intersect the
Einstein kernel with the twelve-dimensional extra-shell kernel and the
`H,J_i` moment cone, add wave self-sources, or prove a complete second-order
extension.
