# Global bounded cone for finite generic rest-frame waves

The exact finite-harmonic wave theorem already proves that arbitrary finite
cross-`ell` sums at `k=0` extend at second order precisely when their total
stabilizer moment maps vanish.  The generic-`lambda` global-cross theorem
adds a shell-separated obstruction: every nonzero common-zero wave sum has
an Einstein-minus coefficient, and its unique `(ell,m,parity,omega_minus)`
channel carries a nonzero triangular `(a,b,d)` pivot.

Consequently, two bounded strata are certified:

- the static branch `(c,d,W_x,A)`, with `a=b=Q_e=B=0`; and
- any nonzero finite generic `k=0` wave on the total `H,J_i` zero cone,
  with `a=b=d=Q_e=0`, `A=B=0`, and arbitrary `(c,W_x)`.

Cross-`ell` wave products add no new obstruction and cannot cancel the
global-times-minus pivot.  Circumference and electric mixed columns have
certified bounded range corrections at `k=0`, while the Wilson mixed source
vanishes.  Constant twist is not a spectator: its off-axis extra-primary
column has the exact nonzero adjoint pairing `24*sqrt(3)`.  The electric tangent is
excluded independently by its homogeneous `E11=Q_e^2/2` source after the
total Hamiltonian moment map has cancelled the wave contribution.

The complete nonzero-`A` wave zero locus remains open, as do infinite
harmonic completions, nonzero compact momentum, exceptional `ell=1` wave
inputs, causal/retarded corrections, all-orders integration, residual descent
and quantum claims.
