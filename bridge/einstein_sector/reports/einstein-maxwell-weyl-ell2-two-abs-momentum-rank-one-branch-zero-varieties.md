# Multiplicity-two source L4 zero varieties

Candidates 8 and 12 are the two remaining `L=4` fibres with one
two-dimensional `p_extra` source multiplicity and a scalar target cokernel.
Exact algebraic-number reduction shows that the doubled rows have rank one.
Their negative proportionality factors have squared magnitudes `3/40` and
`120`; rational isolating intervals select the negative roots.

An internal change of basis separates one active and one spectator binary
quartic in each parity.  The active system is the scalar binary-quartic
multiplication problem.  Its invariants are respectively `r^2=1/3` and
`r^2=1/40`, so each active variety has two one-fibre-zero planes and two real
mixed proportionality sheets.  The free ten-dimensional spectator factor
makes all four components complex dimension 20 inside ambient dimension 30.

Together with the preceding certificates, only candidates 7, 11, 13 and 19
remain unclassified at cross-fibre level.  Same-fibre sources, Taub
intersections, bounded and smooth-secular correction classes, residual
observables, causal maps and quantum interpretations remain fail-closed.
