# Asymptotic Bach Schouten corner zero-mode obstruction

## Result

The minimal bulk Schouten pair is not yet a memory-inclusive null-boundary
phase space.

In the tracefree radiative principal Bondi jet, the auxiliary equation gives

\[
S_{AB}=\frac{\alpha_B}{2}\partial_u Q_{AB},
\]

where \(Q_{AB}\) is the leading \(p=0\) metric source and \(S_{AB}\) is the
leading \(r^{-1}\) coefficient of the auxiliary Schouten tensor.

Consequently:

\[
Q_{AB}(u)=Q^-_{AB}
+\frac{2}{\alpha_B}\int_{-\infty}^u S_{AB}(v)\,dv
\]

and

\[
Q_{AB}(u)=Q^+_{AB}
-\frac{2}{\alpha_B}\int_u^{+\infty}S_{AB}(v)\,dv
\]

are different reconstructions unless the endpoint matching condition is
supplied.  The transformation

\[
Q_{AB}\mapsto Q_{AB}+q_{AB}(x)
\]

with \(q_{AB}\) tracefree and \(u\)-independent leaves \(S_{AB}\) unchanged.
Thus choosing a retarded or advanced inverse silently fixes a corner; it is
not an intrinsic inverse of the boundary differential.
