remaining zero-frequency `L=2,4` polar block is invertible, so the complete
zero-frequency source has a bounded correction, with zero homogeneous
correction.

actual nonzero-frequency polynomial source, particularly products involving

alone does not exclude polynomial growth in its inverse.
