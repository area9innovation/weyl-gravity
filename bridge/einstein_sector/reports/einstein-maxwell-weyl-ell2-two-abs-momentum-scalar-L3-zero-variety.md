# Candidate-2 scalar L3 zero variety

Candidate 2 is the first odd-`L` fibre whose complete resonance ideal is now
classified, rather than merely supplied with a mixed zero witness.  Its two
parity equations form an exact `2 x 2` bilinear pencil.  An invertible change
of variables diagonalizes that pencil with two nonzero real eigenvalues.

The `V_2 x V_2 -> V_3` projection is the unique first transvectant of binary
quartics.  In characteristic zero, its vanishing says that the two quartics
are proportional, including the zero cases through the rank formulation.
The transformed zero variety is therefore the Cartesian product of two
rank-at-most-one `5 x 2` determinantal varieties.  It is irreducible of
complex dimension `12` in the ambient dimension `20` and is defined by twenty
quadratic minors, ten in each parity eigenchannel.

This closes one additional fibrewise ideal.  It does not classify the other
fifteen ideals, same-fibre sources, Taub intersections, bounded or
smooth-secular corrections, residual descent, or causal and quantum maps.
