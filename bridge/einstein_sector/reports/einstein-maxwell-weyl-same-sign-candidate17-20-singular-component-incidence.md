# Candidate-17/20 singular-component incidence

The two algebraic singular components of the candidate-17/20
third-transvectant product are

```text
Sigma_plus  = S_plus x K_minus,
Sigma_minus = K_plus x S_minus.
```

They meet in `S_plus x S_minus`, of complex dimension eight.  This
intersection remains physically present on every positive fixed-occupation
level: occupy one parity factor with

```text
f = sqrt(6*N_minus)*e_0,
g = sqrt(96*N_plus)*e_0,
```

and set the other factor to zero.  The occupied factor is a common-square
singular pair, the zero factor belongs to the singular cone, both occupations
are the prescribed positive values, and all three lifted rotation moments
vanish.  The two total node norms are positive, so both node-phase actions
remain free.

Consequently the images of the two singular components meet in the
node-phase and lifted-rotation zero quotient.  This is the sharp contrast
with candidate 18, whose component intersection is excluded by positive
negative-node occupation.

The theorem does not show that either candidate-17/20 singular component is
connected after the physical constraints, nor that the complete singular
rotation-zero quotient is connected.
