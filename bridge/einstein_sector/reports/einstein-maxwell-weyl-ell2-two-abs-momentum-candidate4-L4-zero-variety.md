# Candidate-4 target-doublet L4 amplitude zero variety

Candidate 4 has one input copy per parity on each momentum fibre but two
target-adjoint components per output parity.  Exact reduction of those target
components leaves two independent binary-quartic multiplication equations:

`A_a B_p - A_p B_a = 0`,

`A_a B_a - 3 A_p B_p = 0`.

Their sum and difference after multiplication by `sqrt(3)` factor into

`(A_a-sqrt(3)A_p)(B_a+sqrt(3)B_p)=0`,

`(A_a+sqrt(3)A_p)(B_a-sqrt(3)B_p)=0`.

Because `C[x,y]` has no zero divisors, the complete all-`m` zero variety has
exactly four ten-complex-dimensional linear components: the two one-fibre-zero
planes and the two real mixed sheets with equal parity ratios `+sqrt(3)` or
`-sqrt(3)` on both momentum fibres.

Thus the second target-adjoint component does not turn the branch-basis
obstruction into an all-amplitude no-go.  This certificate classifies one of
the twenty-one parent amplitude fibres; aggregate progress across independent
certificates is recorded only in the generated atlas.  Same-fibre sources,
Taub intersection, complete tangent cone, smooth-secular and causal correction
classes remain fail-closed.
