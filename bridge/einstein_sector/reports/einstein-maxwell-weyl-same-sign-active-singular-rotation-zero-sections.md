# Singular rotation-zero sections at every positive occupation

The complex singular strata of candidates 17, 18 and 20 survive both physical
tests that might otherwise have removed them: strictly positive active-node
norms and the three lifted rotational constraints.

For candidates 17 and 20, let `e_0=(0,0,1,0,0)` be the `m=0` quartic vector.
It is the common-square vector `v([0:1:0])/2`.  For arbitrary
`N_minus,N_plus>0`, put in one parity factor

```text
f = sqrt(6 N_minus) e_0,
g = sqrt(96 N_plus) e_0,
```

and set the other parity factor to zero.  The two absolute-current norms are
exactly `N_minus,N_plus`; the point lies in the double singular locus and is
axisymmetric.

For candidate 18, retain its positive internal weight `alpha>0` and negative
absolute weight `beta>0`.  Put in one rank-one parity factor

```text
f = sqrt(6 N_plus/alpha) e_0,
g = sqrt(6 N_minus/beta) e_0,
```

set the other factor to its vertex, and set all ten spectators to zero.  This
again realizes every positive occupation pair on a singular axisymmetric
point.

Both total node norms are positive, so the two common node-phase actions are
free at these sections.  Thus singular reduction is unavoidable in a complete
global theorem.  The present result is an existence theorem, not a component
decomposition or quotient-topology theorem.
