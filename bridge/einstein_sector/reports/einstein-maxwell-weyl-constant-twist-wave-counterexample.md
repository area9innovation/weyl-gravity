# Constant twist is not an arbitrary wave spectator

The pure constant-twist position is an exact flat-holonomy modulus. That fact
does not provide a bounded mixed transport for every oscillator in the fixed
periodic carrier.

Choose the rotationally neutral axial `ell=2,m=0` Einstein-minus/extra
balance and a perpendicular constant twist. The already-certified
twist-position adjoint matrix sends the axial-extra `e1` column to

```text
(0,24*sqrt(3),0,0).
```

This source lies at `omega_extra=4/sqrt(3)`. The twist-times-Einstein-minus
source lies at a different frequency, and the wave self-sums and differences
do not occupy `omega_extra`. Hence the nonzero adjoint pairing cannot cancel.

This point belonged to the previously stated wave branch with arbitrary
constant twist `A`. That portion of the global axial `ell=2`, full `ell=2`,
fixed-`ell`, and finite-harmonic global cone claims must therefore be
withdrawn. The wave-free constant-twist modulus and the `A=0` wave subcones
remain certified. The complete nonzero-`A` wave zero locus is open.
