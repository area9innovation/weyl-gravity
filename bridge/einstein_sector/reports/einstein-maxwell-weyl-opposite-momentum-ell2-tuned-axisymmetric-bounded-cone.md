
```text

```

travelling planes meet the five-moment-map cone only at the origin because
`r=omega_-/omega_+` satisfies `0<r<1`.

```text
(1-r)/(1+r) <= t <= (1+r)/(1-r).
```

then fixed by the Hamiltonian and compact-momentum equations, up to their
phase and multiplicity factorization.  The exact collision census proves
that the displayed resonance rows are the only further bounded conditions.
