classified in the smooth exponential-polynomial correction category on the
compactified Plebański--Hacyan background.  Inputs may contain any finite set

```text

```



for the complete declared finite generic carrier, including opposite momenta,
multiple absolute-momentum fibres and all relative phases.
