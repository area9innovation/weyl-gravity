# Candidate 18 singular-component separation

Candidate 18 has two determinantal singular components,

```text
Sigma_plus  = C^10 x {0} x R_minus,
Sigma_minus = C^10 x R_plus x {0},
```

meeting in `C^10 x {0} x {0}`.  The negative-current active-node norm is
carried only by the two rank-one factors, not by the ten positive spectators.
Their intersection therefore has negative-node occupation zero and misses
every strictly positive level.

Consequently, the singular locus on every positive active-node occupation
level is a disjoint union of two nonempty clopen pieces.  Both node phases and
lifted diagonal `SO(3)` preserve the labelled vanishing-factor conditions and
cannot identify the pieces.  Each piece contains the certified central
`m=0` rotation-zero section.  Hence the singular rotation-zero quotient has
at least two connected components.

This does not prove that either piece is connected, nor that the complete
smooth-plus-singular rotation-zero fibre is disconnected: smooth strata may
still connect the two singular pieces.
