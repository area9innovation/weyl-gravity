# Exceptional axial ell=1 target operator

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `REDUCED-MODE`.

At `ell=1,k=0`, the generic four-coefficient axial operator retains a
polynomial gauge column and a polynomial Noether row.  On the nonzero
frequency quotient its exact determinant is

```text
omega^2 (omega^2-4) (3 omega^2-4).
```

The three primary fibres are:

* `omega^2=0`: the global axial twist;
* `omega^2=4`: the standard Einstein--Maxwell physical oscillator;
* `omega^2=4/3`: a previously unclassified fourth-order Weyl--Maxwell mode,
  represented at `k=0` by `(H_t,H_x,Q_t,Q_x)=(0,1,0,-3)`.

At zero frequency the raw left cokernel is two-dimensional.  One direction
is the universal Noether identity; the other is the physical twist-adjoint
direction.  Its three `SO(3)` copies are precisely the rotation/Taub
constraints needed in the non-axisymmetric quadratic problem.

The Lee--Wald sign of the new exceptional mode and the polar exceptional
fourth-order quotient remain open.
