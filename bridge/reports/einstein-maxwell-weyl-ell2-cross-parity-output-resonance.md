# Axial-polar ell=2 cross-output solvability

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `REDUCED-MODE`.

An axial `ell=2` mode times a polar `ell=2` mode has odd total parity.  Its
outputs are polar `L=1,3` and axial `L=2,4`; axial `L=0` is absent because the
constant scalar harmonic generates no axial harmonic.

At zero frequency all four present quotient blocks are invertible.  At every
one of the nine nonzero sum/difference-frequency types, exact algebraic
witnesses miss both target shells in every block.  In particular, the new
polar `L=1` theorem excludes its `omega^2=4/3` and `omega^2=4` shells.

Therefore no axial-polar cross-source coefficient can create a new
second-order obstruction at `ell=2,k=0`.  Natural Noether identities complete
the raw equation solve from the invertible local-gauge quotient.  The only
remaining obstructions for a combined tangent are the already known pure
sector `H` and `J_i` projections.
