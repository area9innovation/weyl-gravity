# Exceptional polar ell=1,k=0 operator

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `REDUCED-MODE`.

At `ell=1` the tracefree polar tensor harmonic vanishes, so the scalar
diffeomorphism remains as a residual gauge parameter after the usual
`h_A=0`, Weyl `K=0` slice.  Its field column on `(A_t,B,C_t,U)` is

```text
(2(omega^2-1), 0, 2, -1).
```

It is an exact left and right null vector of the specialized action Hessian.
Because its `U` component is a unit, `U=0` is a complete polynomial slice.
The reduced determinant is

```text
(omega^2-4)(3 omega^2-4)/2.
```

Thus the quotient has a fourth-order shell at `omega^2=4/3`, the standard
shell at `omega^2=4`, and no physical zero-frequency cokernel.  The latter is
the important cross-cone result: polar `L=1` introduces no new static Taub
condition.
