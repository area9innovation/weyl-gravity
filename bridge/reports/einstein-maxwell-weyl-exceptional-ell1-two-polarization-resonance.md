# Axisymmetric exceptional `ell=1` two-polarization no-go

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `REDUCED-MODE`.

Let `a_x,a_p` be the axial and polar exceptional `ell=1,m=0,k=0`
positive-frequency amplitudes at `omega_e^2=4/3`. A collinear twist velocity
can cancel their total constant-lapse moment map at

```text
B^2=(8/3)|a_x|^2+(1/2)|a_p|^2.
```

The polar self-source at `2omega_e` has polar `L=2` adjoint pairings

```text
(1/8,-1/4).
```

These are exactly `-3/16` times the axial self-source pairings
`(-2/3,4/3)`. Thus the even-parity self-obstructions would cancel at

```text
|a_p|^2=(16/3)|a_x|^2.
```

But the same choice necessarily has `a_x*a_p != 0`. The direct axial--polar
cross-source is an axial `L=2` resonant source

```text
(2/3,0,0,4,0,0),
```

and its exact axial adjoint pairing is

```text
-8*sqrt(3)/9.
```

Hence the cross channel obstructs every interior point. On either boundary,
the surviving individual self-source is already obstructed. The only
axisymmetric exceptional amplitude compatible with the complete
`2omega_e` equation is therefore `a_x=a_p=0`, even after twist balance.

This closes the full `m=0` two-polarization exceptional cone at second order.
Interference among distinct `m` remains a separate representation-theoretic
question.
