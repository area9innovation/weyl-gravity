# Same-parity ell=2 output ledger

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `REDUCED-MODE`.

The angular selection and nonzero-frequency resonance calculations are common
to axial--axial and polar--polar `ell=2,k=0` quadratic inputs.  Both products
have even total parity, hence polar outputs at even `L=0,2,4` and axial
outputs at odd `L=1,3`.

All nine sum/difference-frequency types miss the exceptional axial `L=1`
twist, fourth-order, and standard shells and both generic axial `L=3` shells.
The polar `L=2,4` nonresonance ledger is inherited unchanged.  Only the
homogeneous `L=0` source matrix remains input-sector specific.

This removes the artificial dependency of the polar cone theorem on the
axial cone theorem.  Axial--polar cross terms have odd total parity and remain
outside this certificate.
