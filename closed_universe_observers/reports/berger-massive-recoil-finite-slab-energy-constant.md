standard retarded energy estimate.  The exact longitudinal sector is instead
algebraic with multiplier `1/m_b^2` and must not be assigned high-mode
smoothing.

- `integral h_b dt=4/3`;

`integral ||delta(h_b K_b)|| dt <= (3 H_b^2/m_b^2 + 8 H_b/(3m_b)) E_A`.

integral and exact switch radii.  The downstream Maxwell-to-detector dual norm
