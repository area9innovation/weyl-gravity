# Clock-integrated detector form coefficients

The fixed scalar detector profile has now been multiplied by the actual,
detector-dependent one-form polarization.  In detector-centred quaternion
coordinates the exact spatial gradients are

```text
dR0_1 = a(-c y2 theta1 + c y1 theta2 + y0 theta3)
dR1_2 = a( y0 theta1 - y3 theta2 + c^-1 y2 theta3).
```

Validated rational intervals enclose every real and imaginary Peter--Weyl
matrix entry through `two_j=4`.  The required clock factors are odd secant
powers; they are rigorously bracketed by the already certified adjacent even
secant moments.  Applying the exact finite-block adjoint de Rham matrix also
exports the spatial coderivative component.  The spin-zero coderivative
vanishes and nonconstant blocks are nonzero, providing direct regression
rails.

This is not yet the four-dimensional Maxwell source.  Its spatial one-form
part contains a time derivative, and that derivative must be integrated
against the retarded/advanced mode kernel rather than discarded by an
unweighted clock integral.  The infinite spectral tail, full Green images,
and recoil coefficient therefore remain open.
