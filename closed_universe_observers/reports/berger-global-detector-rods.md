# Global Berger detector rods

## Result

The two detector charts now have an explicit global compact realization.  A
separate triplet of massless scalar rods is assigned to each detector.  This
is necessary because the two identity Jacobians are prescribed as Cauchy data
at different times; one shared triplet would impose an additional, previously
unproved two-time interpolation constraint.

On the unit-quaternion model of the Berger three-sphere, the displayed linear
profiles have scalar Laplacian eigenvalue `29/18`.  Multiplication by
`cos(sqrt(58)/6 (t-t_a))` therefore gives six exact global wave solutions.
At detector `a`, its triplet has rod center `(s_a,0,0)`, zero normal
derivative, and identity relational Jacobian in direction order
`((4/3)e0,e3,e1,e2)`.

Their conserved quadratic stress is now a fully determined global compact
source.  Its spatial support is confined to `j=0` and `j=1`, and its temporal
frequencies are `0` and `+-sqrt(58)/3`.  This finite sector is the exact input
for the next compact Taub calculation.

## Carrier correction and boundary

Detector indexing changes the prospective apparatus extension from three to
six rod fields.  With two memories and two multipliers, the extension has ten
new degree-zero rows and ten paired degree-one rows, so the proposed carrier
has **84 rows**, not 78.

This export does not evaluate the compact adjoint-kernel projector, prove a
second-order backreacted branch, construct the interacting 84-row BV complex,
or certify a Lorentzian Green homotopy for it.
