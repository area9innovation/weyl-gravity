# Berger response-specific streaming preflight

Materializing the first sub-unit moving-profile rail through `two_j=3835`
would require 44,140,852 supported detector-coordinate entries, 117,703,824
scalar recurrence applications, and 662,112,780 legacy clock-power intervals.
Those counts grow to billions for tighter detector-`L2` tolerances.

The required output is a fixed detector or recoil scalar, not a reusable dense
coefficient artifact.  The certified replacement is therefore a shell stream:
contract each exact charge block immediately into the fixed scalar functional
and stop when its interval plus the dual-norm-weighted tail proves the desired
width, sign, or nonzero statement.

The fixed massive-chain dual norms and complete scalar integrand are not yet
certified, so the Maxwell tail currently has `NO_CERTIFIED_MAP` to a numerical
recoil interval.  That analytic map is the next gate.
