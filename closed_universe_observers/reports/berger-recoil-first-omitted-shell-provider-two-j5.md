imported direct `two_j<=4` detector, D1-on-h0 and Maxwell/massive kernel

remainder, and the Maxwell degree-0/1 plus massive degree-0/1/2 exact operator

from the certified detector-support upper endpoints and switch-support lower
endpoints, rather than accepted as free constants.

This is a direct-carrier one-shell extension.  It is not an identification
with the separate hashed exact-`T` stream through `two_j=138`, and it does not
yet evaluate any `I_abc[5,k]`.  All-shell coverage, physical masses, the tail
stopping rule, quotient descent, tangent-cone restriction, Bridge 3 and
