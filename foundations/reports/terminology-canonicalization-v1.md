# Canonical terminology and standalone-word admission

The published global index contains 3,776 candidates, down from 6,205 after the
frequency-only filter. A versioned editorial policy defines canonical notation
aliases and retained single-word terms. Dictionary or external-vocabulary matches
also admit standalone words. Capitalization alone does not admit a word.
Thus absence, absent, absolute and the reported typo absemt do not become
standalone entries; longer phrases such as absolute continuity remain eligible.
This is an explicit publication policy, not an automatic judgment of meaning.

ACA0, ACA_0, ACA_{0}, ACA₀ and a trailing-period extraction variant map to ACA₀;
RCA, WKL and WWKL subscript-zero forms follow the same rule. Bare ACA and RCA
are deliberately distinct from their subscript-zero systems. No stemming, fuzzy
spelling correction, or merger by shared dictionary link is performed. Additional
aliases require an explicit policy edit and contextual review.

Merging precedes the five-occurrence gate and source-scope slicing. Identical
source spans count once. Each canonical candidate retains observed aliases,
original candidate IDs, methods, dictionary/vocabulary links and all contexts.
Search accepts aliases; expanded entries show alternate spellings. Canonical IDs
are derived from kind and canonical phrase. Frequency sorting uses the combined
count. The raw NLP extraction remains unchanged and content-addressed.

Limits: the project list is an initial editorial selection, not an exhaustive
technical vocabulary. Rare or unlisted technical words may be omitted. Multiword
noise and ambiguous vocabulary matches remain possible. No definitions, semantic
certificates, scientific claims or lifecycle states are promoted.

Validation covers notation merging, span deduplication before the frequency
boundary, keeping ACA distinct, lexical exclusions and technical exceptions, all
seven scope shards, alias search, original contexts and existing dictionary
behavior. An initial test exposed that approved subscripted canonical forms also
needed admission as technical terms; fixed and rerun. A verifier running across
that edit detected manifest drift; the final build and verifier were rerun.
Exact commands, elapsed times, results and hashes are recorded in the
[receipt](../receipts/terminology-canonicalization-v1.json). Tier 3 is unnecessary:
this is an editorial website change with no altered mathematical inputs.
