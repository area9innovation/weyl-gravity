# Finite field, finite mode, finite Hilbert dimension, and finitism

## Result

`FOUNDATIONAL_FINITE_FIELD_FINITE_MODE_NON_EQUIVALENCE_V1` proves a typed
non-equivalence ledger for four constructions that are often called “finite”:

| Object | What is finite? | Scalars | Infinite parent? |
|---|---|---|---|
| finite-field phase space `F_q^2` | phase-space points | finite field coordinates; associated quantum carrier is `C^q` | not for fixed `q` |
| Weyl finite-mode cutoff `I_<=N` | retained energy coordinates | real/complex | yes, explicit countable tower |
| finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space `C^d` | basis dimension | complex | scalar continuum remains |
| foundational finitism | admissible ontology/codes | must be reconstructed | actual infinity rejected or unavailable |

The relation is `NOT_EQUIVALENT_BY_TYPE`.  Equal or similar cardinalities do
not erase the different scalar, carrier, regulator, and foundational axes.

## Finite-field kinematics

Gibbons, Hoffman and Wootters use `F_q^2` for prime-power `q`.  It has `q^2`
points, `q(q+1)` affine lines and `q+1` striations.  The quantum state space
in their construction is nevertheless `q`-dimensional over the complex
numbers.  Finite phase-space coordinates therefore do not mean that complex
amplitudes have been replaced by finite-field amplitudes.

## Weyl mode truncation

The repository cutoff retains the energy modes up to `N` from a fixed
countable `E/A/L` tower.  It leaves the real/complex coefficient field
unchanged and comes with canonical inclusions into larger cutoffs and the
completed `ell^2` carrier.  Through energy 12 it contains exactly 3,740
coordinates.  This is a regulator of a specified infinite parent, not a
finite-field phase space and not foundational finitism.

## Six non-equivalences

The machine ledger supplies a witness for every pair among the four objects.
The sharpest are:

- `F_q^2` and `C^q` occur simultaneously in the finite-field construction;
- a cutoff parameter bounds energy, whereas `q` selects a field and state
  dimension;
- abstract `C^d` carries no canonical inclusion into a continuum theory;
- ordinary finite-dimensional complex quantum mechanics still uses the
  infinite scalar continuum; and
- a finite regulator inside ZF or ZFC does not reject actual infinity.

## Missing bridges

A finite-field continuum proposal needs a directed refinement family,
comparison maps, observable/state transport, a topology, dynamics and error
bounds.  A Weyl cutoff limit needs norm or distributional convergence,
uniform domain estimates, regulator independence, states and renormalization.
A finitist physics needs finite scalar/probability codes, replacements for
completion and limits, and empirical error semantics.

None of these bridges currently exists in the repository.  This is not a
no-go theorem for finite-field physics or finitism.  It prevents a regulator,
a finite carrier, and rejection of actual infinity from being substituted for
one another without the missing mathematics.  It establishes nothing
`LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL`.
