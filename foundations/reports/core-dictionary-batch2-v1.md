## Editorial and scientific boundary

These are AI-authored, editorially reviewed explanations, not independent expert
review or new scientific results. Local source hashes are retained in the dictionary.
The wave reversal, trace repair, reduced Hadamard cutoff and observable-extension
reports supply project context. Formal-system terminology was checked against
[Simpson's introduction](https://sgslogic.net/t20/sosoa/chapter1.pdf), and quantum
terminology against [Fewster and Rejzner](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.04051) and
[Rejzner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14272).

The accounts distinguish regularity from state positivity, reduced calculations
from a full Lorentzian construction, and centered vertex classes from particle
states. Partial trace repair does not certify the full classical import. No
quantum lifecycle, dependency boundary, theorem or certificate is promoted.

## Validation

The companion [receipt](../receipts/core-dictionary-batch2-v1.json) records exact
commands, elapsed times, source/output hashes and failed attempts as well as final
passes. The 31 scoped unit tests check account coverage, aliases, links, extraction
and publication. Independent site and proposal verifiers check source spans,
content hashes, deterministic rendering and claim boundaries. Chromium checks
cover hover/focus/tap, comparison, Escape, deep links, no JavaScript, mobile bounds,
vertical column order and editorial inventory controls. Desktop and mobile images
were visually inspected. A browser regression caught the comparison layout
revealing every entry; the final CSS keeps non-target entries hidden in every
perspective combination.

Tier 0 covers syntax, structured data and scoped whitespace/diff review. Tiers 1
and 2 cover the changed editorial package and generated site/inventory/proposal
consumers. Tier 3 was not run: no mathematical input, shared algebra, scientific
lifecycle or release changed. These checks cannot certify explanatory semantics.
