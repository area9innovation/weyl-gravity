# Explicit free-mode Krein and C*-dynamics in ZF

## Result

`FOUNDATIONAL_EXPLICIT_MODE_DYNAMICS_ZF_V1` turns the previously static
coordinate energy calculus into an actual dynamics.  On the explicit
one-particle carrier `H_1=ell^2(I)`, define

```text
(U_t x)_i = exp(-i t energy(i)) x_i.
```

This is a strongly continuous Hilbert-unitary and `J`-unitary group in ZF.
Its generator is the already-certified diagonal energy `D`, it preserves
`Dom(D)`, and its bosonic lift is generated by `dGamma(D)`.

On the unitized compact-operator algebra

```text
A = K(H_1)^~,
alpha_t(A) = U_t A U_t^dagger_0
```

is a point-norm continuous one-parameter group of C*-automorphisms.  This is a
genuine free reduced-mode dynamics, not merely a static functional calculus.
The result carries exactly `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC` and `REDUCED-MODE`.

## Direct coordinate proof

The group and unitarity equations are coordinate identities:

```text
U_0=1,
U_t U_s=U_(t+s),
U_t^dagger_0 U_t=1.
```

The energy and fundamental symmetry are diagonal in the same named basis, so
`U_t J=J U_t` and therefore

```text
U_t^dagger_0 J U_t=J.
```

Thus the group preserves both the companion Hilbert product and the indefinite
Krein product.  Since phases have modulus one, the weighted sum defining
`Dom(D)` is unchanged.

Strong continuity also has a direct proof.  Given `x` and `epsilon`, split its
explicitly convergent coordinate sum into a finite head and a small tail.  The
tail of `(U_t-1)x` is bounded by twice the original tail norm.  The head has
only finitely many exponential phases, so ordinary finite-dimensional
continuity applies.  No simultaneous selection from a family of tails or
eigenvectors occurs.

The derivative on `Dom(D)` is `-iD`.  Stone's theorem is not being invoked to
produce a group from an abstract operator: the group is already the displayed
coordinate formula.

## The induced C*-dynamics

For a coordinate matrix unit `E_ij`,

```text
alpha_t(E_ij)
  = exp(-i t (energy(i)-energy(j))) E_ij.
```

The energy difference is an integer Laurent degree.  Addition of degrees
proves multiplicativity on composable matrix units, negation proves
compatibility with the Hilbert adjoint, and conjugation by a unitary proves
isometry.  Compact operators stay compact and the adjoined identity is fixed.

Point-norm continuity uses the defining finite-rank approximation of a compact
operator.  For finite-rank `F` near `K`,

```text
||alpha_t(K)-K||
  <= 2 ||K-F|| + ||alpha_t(F)-F||.
```

The final term is a finite matrix calculation and tends to zero.  On the
finite-rank core, the generator derivation is

```text
delta(E_ij) = -i (energy(i)-energy(j)) E_ij
            = -i [D,E_ij].
```

Energy-block-diagonal compact operators are fixed.  The action is nontrivial:
a matrix unit joining energies 2 and 3 has degree `-1`.

## Foundation and theorem avoidance

ZF suffices because the carrier, coordinate basis, integer energy function,
and complex phases are explicit.  No Axiom of Choice or Countable Choice is
added.  The construction avoids using the abstract spectral theorem, Stone's
theorem as an existence theorem, an eigenbasis theorem, or Zorn's lemma.

This is a sufficiency result, not a weakest-base theorem.  A coded
reverse-mathematics or constructive treatment of the real exponential and
uniform continuity could lower or refine the base.

## Boundary

This certificate does not exponentiate the other unbounded conformal
generators, construct the full conformal group, add interactions, produce
causal Green propagation, select a state, prove a KMS condition, or establish
a generalized Born rule.  It says nothing about thermodynamic implementability
in an inequivalent representation.

In particular, this is not `LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL` evidence for an off-shell BV
propagator, Hadamard state, time-ordered products, or a Lorentzian QME.
