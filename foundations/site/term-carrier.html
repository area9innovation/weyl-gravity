Carrier

Project vocabulary for the mathematical setting supporting a construction; not a material medium.

General

The kind of mathematical structure in which a model is built. A finite collection of matrices, a space of waves, and an algebra of quantum quantities are different carriers, with different questions that must be checked.

Why the choice matters

A finite table of numbers lets you perform exact finite calculations. A whole wave may require infinitely many approximations plus a rule explaining their limit. Both can model aspects of the same problem, but success with one does not automatically settle the other.

Reading a matrix cell

The carrier tells you what mathematical objects that cell is discussing. The other dimensions ask what assumptions are allowed and which job—such as defining states or proving evolution—has been completed.

Avoid a common confusion

This word does not mean the substance that carries a physical wave. It describes the mathematical setting used to investigate it.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

Two models can use the same underlying numbers while measuring closeness or probability differently. Ask whether the proposed connection preserves the detector reading, not just the names of the variables.

Physics

The specified mathematical realization carrying the model’s variables, operations and states: for example finite matrices, a completed function space or a local algebraic construction. Changing carrier may change which analytic obligations are meaningful.

Finite and continuum settings

A finite mode truncation supplies matrices and finite sums. Passing to a completed field space can add convergence, domain, positivity and causal-support obligations. A successful truncation is evidence about that truncation unless a transfer theorem is given.

What to inspect

Look for the space or algebra, the chosen topology, admissible states, domains of the dynamics, and maps connecting it to another realization. These determine whether two calculations address the same quantity.

Boundary

The atlas tracks different carriers explicitly so their established results cannot silently be combined into an unproved complete theory.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

A useful carrier change should specify the preserved dynamics, observables, pairing and causal support relevant to its intended use. Preservation of one property cannot stand for all of them.

Mathematics

The chosen structured ambient object, including any topology, pairing, operator domains and completion data needed for a result. The matrix uses carrier as an organizational dimension rather than as one universally defined category.

More than an underlying set

An identical vector space with two different topologies or pairings may support different continuity or positivity claims. A useful carrier specification therefore includes the structures used in the theorem.

Transfer question

To carry a result to another realization, specify maps and prove the required compatibility—such as continuity, intertwining of differentials, or preservation of the pairing. The necessary conditions depend on the claimed result.

Project usage

Carrier is an editorial organizing term here, not a claim that all entries form one category with interchangeable morphisms.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

An isomorphism of vector spaces need not be a homeomorphism, a chain map or an isometry. Each extra preservation condition changes the interface statement.

Specialist

The typed support of a construction in the atlas. Cross-carrier transfer requires a declared interface preserving the relevant operations and hypotheses; matching labels or finite fixtures do not establish equivalence.

Interface obligations

State the source and target kinds and the exact property transported. A chain map, an SDR, a cyclic pairing identification and a causal operator identification answer different questions; one cannot substitute for the others.

Why it matters to the programme

Reduced-mode certificates and local algebraic checks retain their dependency tags when compared with full Lorentzian targets. A positive finite test does not close the missing analytic transfer gates.

Boundary

Read each interface certificate and its excluded claims. A coverage mark is neither a functorial construction nor proof of physical equivalence.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

Finite-mode, Hilbert and local field-theoretic results require explicit joins. No full-import or causal-state claim follows solely from placing their certificates in neighboring atlas cells.
