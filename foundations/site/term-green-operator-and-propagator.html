Green operator and propagator

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A Green operator turns a source into a solution of a linear equation. “Propagator” is a related physics word whose exact meaning depends on whether it describes classical response, a commutator or a quantum correlation.

Example and distinction

An inverse formula does not yet tell us where the response occurs. Causal support and suitable domains must be checked, and a response function is not automatically a state assigning physical probabilities.

Physics

For a linear field operator, advanced and retarded Green operators solve source equations with designated support. Quantum propagator conventions can instead involve time ordering and a choice of two-point structure.

Use in the model

State which object is meant. A Euclidean inverse or reduced-mode kernel does not establish a full Lorentzian off-shell propagator compatible with all fields and gauge identities.

Mathematics

A Green operator is a map between specified source and solution spaces satisfying the required left/right inverse identities and support conditions. Its distributional kernel is one representation of that map.

Conditions and consequences

The kernel’s existence, continuity and singularity structure are analytic claims. A formal inverse in a mode basis is insufficient without convergence and the intended domain and boundary conditions.

Specialist

A complete Lorentzian off-shell BV propagator for the full metric complex remains unestablished absent its certificate. Keep scalar witnesses and reduced kernels separate from that obligation.

Project role and claim boundary

The failed full-export consistency prerequisite blocks the advertised dependent transfer. Neither a formal causal expression nor a local algebraic quantum calculation supplies the missing compatible full operator.
