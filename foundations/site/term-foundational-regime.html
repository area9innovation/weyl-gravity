Foundational regime

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

A foundational regime is a chosen approach to which mathematical objects and reasoning principles are allowed. It is one of the organizing axes of the atlas.

Example and distinction

It is not a ladder of reader intelligence or a score for how true a physical theory is. The same physical task can be asked under several regimes to reveal which existence assumptions its answer uses.

Physics

A foundational regime records the mathematical permission structure under which a physical obligation is studied. It is distinct from the carrier family and from the physical postulates.

Use in the model

A finite exact construction, a constructive realization and a set-theoretic existence argument may address similar physics while providing different guarantees. Compare the actual assumptions and outputs rather than only their regime labels.

Mathematics

The atlas groups foundational environments for comparison, but those labels are not themselves formal theories with automatically established implications. The theorem must still identify its exact base and coding.

Conditions and consequences

Reverse-mathematical systems such as RCA₀ and ACA₀ provide precise calibrations for particular assertions. Broader constructive or set-theoretic categories require additional specification before a strength comparison is meaningful.

Specialist

A regime coordinate records scope; it is not proof that a cell holds for every object or axiom system informally associated with that regime. Cell evidence remains witness- and obligation-specific.

Project role and claim boundary

Composition requires compatible realizations and assumption transport. Neither neighboring coordinates nor a shared theory profile certify those interfaces or establish a global ordering of all regimes.
