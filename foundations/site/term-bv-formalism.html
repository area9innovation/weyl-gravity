BV formalism

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

Stands for: Batalin–Vilkovisky

General

BV stands for Batalin–Vilkovisky. It is a way to organize a gauge theory’s fields, ghosts and additional variables called antifields so that their consistency is expressed by a master equation.

Example and distinction

Antifields are part of this mathematical construction, not a declaration of new physical particles. A consistent classical organization is only one ingredient of a complete quantum theory.

Physics

The Batalin–Vilkovisky (BV) formalism extends the field space and uses an odd bracket to encode gauge structure and variational consistency. The master equation is central to the associated differential.

Use in the model

Its precise implementation depends on the classical action, gauge algebra and conventions. Quantization introduces further renormalization and anomaly questions; a classical BV complex is not already a positive causal quantum model.

Mathematics

Batalin–Vilkovisky (BV) data include graded variables, an odd bracket and an action satisfying the classical master identity in the stated setting. The induced differential organizes the corresponding cohomological problem.

Conditions and consequences

Local functionals and integrated boundary quotients require care. The finite-dimensional formalism’s operations cannot be imported naively as unregularized infinite-dimensional operators without establishing their meaning.

Specialist

Batalin–Vilkovisky (BV) data for quantum work must come from the authoritative classical import. Do not reconstruct a competing complex or infer a complete freeze from a partial snapshot.

Project role and claim boundary

Classify local counterterms and anomalies before coefficients, restore the local QME before residual transfer, and keep Lorentzian certification separate. The existing partial repair does not close the full import gate.
