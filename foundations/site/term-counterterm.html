Counterterm

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A counterterm is an allowed additional term used to adjust a quantum calculation while respecting specified rules. It can cancel a problematic contribution or implement a chosen normalization.

Example and distinction

It is not permission to add anything that makes an answer look desirable. The available terms must first be classified, and the corrected result must then be checked against the relevant identities.

Physics

Counterterms encode local renormalization freedom subject to dimensional, symmetry, grading and boundary restrictions. Which terms are allowed is a classification question separate from their coefficients.

Use in the model

A candidate counterterm can be admissible without its coefficient being known. A coefficient can be computed without proving that the complete local quantum identity has been restored.

Mathematics

The counterterm space is an ansatz of allowed functionals modulo the declared equivalences. The relevant BRST differential and quotient by total derivatives or boundary terms must be fixed.

Conditions and consequences

A local density and its integrated functional need not have the same classification problem. Exact canonical reduction should retain witnesses rather than assuming a desired basis in advance.

Specialist

The programme requires counterterm and anomaly classification before coefficient computation. Restoring the local quantum master equation precedes any residual transfer of the correction.

Project role and claim boundary

These LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC steps do not construct Lorentzian causal products or a physical state. Literature coefficients, spectral determinants and local BV breakings are distinct inputs requiring an explicit comparison if combined.
