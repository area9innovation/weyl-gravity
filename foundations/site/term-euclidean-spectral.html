Euclidean-spectral

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL marks a calculation using the Euclidean setting and spectral properties of specified operators. It identifies the setting of the evidence, not a guarantee about physical propagation through time.

Example and distinction

Such a calculation can reveal useful coefficients or test a proposed estimate. Connecting it to Lorentzian causal physics requires additional hypotheses and constructions.

Physics

EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL results concern the declared Euclidean operator, domain and spectral procedure. Determinants or related spectral quantities have that dependency when calculated in this setting.

Use in the model

They are not automatically BV master-equation breakings, positive Lorentzian states or causal propagators. The relevant identification or reconstruction must be provided rather than assumed from a matching coefficient.

Mathematics

The operator realization, boundary conditions and regularization specify the spectral problem. A formal expression or a finite eigenvalue sample does not establish all of its analytic properties.

Conditions and consequences

A Euclidean estimate and a Lorentzian support theorem control different objects. Changing signature does not transport domains, positivity and distributional singularities without an argument.

Specialist

EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL cannot be promoted to LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL by terminology. A successful determinant calculation does not certify renormalized Lorentzian products or the full metric quantum master equation.

Project role and claim boundary

The programme’s Euclidean stability counterfamily is similarly scoped: it excludes a proposed estimate under its hypotheses, not every possible reconstruction or the entire physical programme.
