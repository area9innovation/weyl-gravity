Formal proof and computational check

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

A formal proof gives a derivation in explicit rules that a proof system can check. A computational check runs a specified test on encoded data. Both can be valuable, but they answer the question they were actually given.

Example and distinction

Checking many finite examples is not a proof about all infinite cases. A checked derivation also does not decide whether its assumptions describe nature or whether the explanation accurately states its theorem.

Physics

A computational certificate can verify exact algebra, numerical behavior or a finite witness. A formal proof checks a derivation relative to a formal statement and trusted logical environment.

Use in the model

Neither independently validates the physical modeling assumptions. The project’s wave reversal combines a written infinite argument with finite exact checks; it is not presented as a completed proof-assistant derivation.

Mathematics

Formalization specifies the language, axioms, definitions and derivation of the claimed statement. A program test can instead establish a finite predicate on inputs without encoding the surrounding infinite theorem.

Conditions and consequences

The link between mathematical objects and their machine representations is a separate correctness obligation. Independent verification should expose which decisive assumptions are shared with the producer.

Specialist

Receipts distinguish written proof, exact witness verification, numerical diagnostics, reproduction and independent checking. Do not promote one evidence type by changing its description.

Project role and claim boundary

The wave theorem’s infinite argument remains unformalized in a proof assistant, and the full classical import is not certified by partial matrix checks. A timeout or skip is never a passing proof obligation.
