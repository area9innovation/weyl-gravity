Finite propagation speed

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

Finite propagation speed means that a disturbance cannot affect arbitrarily distant places immediately. Its response stays inside a region determined by the allowed travel speed and elapsed time.

Example and distinction

This is stronger than simply having a formula that solves an equation. A mode extending across all space is not a localized disturbance, so a finite list of modes does not by itself demonstrate how support travels.

Physics

A finite-propagation theorem controls the support of solutions from localized data using the operator and causal geometry. The source, data class and time interval must be specified.

Use in the model

Mode truncations can obscure or destroy exact localization. A scalar hyperbolic example does not automatically establish finite propagation for the full constrained metric complex or its proposed Green operator.

Mathematics

The statement is a support inclusion, often bounding solution support by the causal development of initial or source support. It requires more than the algebraic equation Pu=f.

Conditions and consequences

Energy estimates or domain-of-dependence arguments can establish it under suitable hypotheses. The theorem must retain the geometry, regularity and boundary conditions on which those arguments rely.

Specialist

The finite chiral ladder explicitly separates exact wave residuals from causal support. Its finite arithmetic certificate does not discharge a finite-propagation theorem for the completed carrier.

Project role and claim boundary

Likewise, the tested hyperbolic witnesses have limited architecture scope. A failed witness is not a universal absence of finite propagation, and a successful reduced example is not a full Lorentzian certificate.
