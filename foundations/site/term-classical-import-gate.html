Classical import gate

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

The classical import gate is the set of checks required before the quantum programme can rely on a supplied classical construction. It verifies that the imported pieces satisfy their promised relations.

Example and distinction

A label saying “classical data” is not enough. If one required object or check is missing, a partial snapshot may still be useful, but it cannot be treated as the complete accepted input.

Physics

The gate requires the classical snapshot’s nilpotency, contraction, chain-map, cyclicity, residual-cohomology and pairing checks at the declared scope. The authoritative classical complex is the source for quantum work.

Use in the model

Source version, content hashes, assumptions and missing objects must travel with the import. A reduced reference passing direct checks does not certify every transitive premise of the full metric construction.

Mathematics

Import acceptance is a conjunction of required predicates on a specified serialized object. A content hash establishes identity of bytes, not truth of those predicates.

Conditions and consequences

Independent checking must address the actual maps, degrees and coefficient domains. Successful subblocks do not imply the complete operator identities, and a missing check cannot be assigned a passing value.

Specialist

The gate is fail-closed and prohibits a competing reconstruction of classical data in the quantum tree. A partial snapshot cannot claim the classical freeze certificate.

Project role and claim boundary

The full export currently fails its square-zero prerequisite; the trace/ghost candidate is not a fully accepted repair. Quantum conclusions depending on that full input remain blocked regardless of separate LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC progress.
