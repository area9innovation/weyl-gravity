Finite chiral fixture

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

A finite chiral fixture is one explicitly specified finite collection of wave modes and coefficients used for a controlled test. “Fixture” means the exact test object, not the whole continuum theory.

Example and distinction

Its finite sums can be checked without rounding. Solving its wave equation and obtaining positive finite energy still does not say that it is spatially localized or that every infinite extension works.

Physics

The ladder starts from a labelled finite family of rational-complex chiral modes in the declared normalization. Integer differentiation weights and decidable rational equality support exact residual and energy checks.

Use in the model

PRA is the stated base for each fixed fixture. Completion, causal support and spatial localization are explicitly additional obligations, not consequences of having finitely many modes.

Mathematics

A fixture fixes its finite labels, coefficients and operations before the identity checks. Rational-complex means rational real and imaginary parts, not a finite scalar field.

Conditions and consequences

The certificate includes finite wave residuals, nesting and positive finite energy under the declared assumptions. A family of such checks must not be silently identified with a uniform infinite reconstruction theorem.

Specialist

Retain the exact fixture, arithmetic domain and quantifiers when citing the ladder’s initial rung. Its dependency is a finite algebraic construction, not a complete physical-state realization.

Project role and claim boundary

Nested Galerkin spaces do not certify a completed carrier or support-preserving propagation. Those transitions require their own representations, topology and independent interface checks.
