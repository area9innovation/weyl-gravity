Algebraic quantum field theory

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

Stands for: Algebraic Quantum Field Theory

General

AQFT stands for Algebraic Quantum Field Theory. It starts with algebras of observables associated with regions of spacetime and asks how those algebras relate. States then supply expectation values and probabilities.

Example and distinction

This keeps the rules for observables distinct from a particular state-vector description. The perturbative form, pAQFT, develops interactions order by order and still needs precise causal and renormalization conditions.

Physics

Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT) organizes observables, locality and dynamics through algebraic relations. Its perturbative variant, pAQFT, constructs interacting observables as formal perturbative objects under specified hypotheses.

Use in the model

An algebraic formulation does not remove the state problem. The relevant positive representations, singularity control and gauge consistency must still be established for the model under study.

Mathematics

Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT) assigns compatible observable algebras to spacetime regions, with axioms expressing properties such as isotony and causal compatibility. States are positive normalized functionals in the relevant setting.

Conditions and consequences

The precise category and axioms vary by formulation. A locally covariant or perturbative construction is an additional specification, not something automatically attached to any algebra of finite matrices.

Specialist

Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT), including perturbative AQFT, names a target framework rather than a completed result of this programme. A causal perturbative construction for the full metric complex remains uncertified.

Project role and claim boundary

Local BV calculations and reduced-mode state tests contribute separately scoped evidence. They cannot certify full Lorentzian causal products, compatible states or the quantum master equation by adopting the AQFT label.
