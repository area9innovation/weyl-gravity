WKL₀

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

Stands for: Weak König’s Lemma

General

WKL stands for Weak König’s Lemma. WKL₀ adds an infinite-path existence rule to RCA₀: a binary branching tree with arbitrarily long allowed paths has an infinite path.

Example and distinction

Picture successive yes-or-no choices whose allowed finite histories keep extending. The added principle guarantees a whole endless choice history. Its role here is a mathematical benchmark, not a level of physical accuracy.

Physics

Weak König’s Lemma gives the additional axiom in WKL₀. The atlas uses this system to distinguish some compactness-based existence arguments from reconstruction already possible in RCA₀.

Use in the model

This does not make WKL₀ the stated solution of the rate-free wave task: that assertion is calibrated against ACA₀. The exact theorem, data representation and base must decide which principle is relevant.

Mathematics

WKL₀ is RCA₀ together with Weak König’s Lemma: every infinite subtree of the binary tree of finite zero-one strings has an infinite path.

Conditions and consequences

The binary-tree restriction is essential. Neither an arbitrary branching-tree theorem nor an algorithm for choosing the path is the stated axiom. Keep WKL₀ distinct from stronger systems and from bare notational abbreviations.

Specialist

Weak König’s Lemma is a named reference principle in the atlas, not evidence that a particular field construction has been carried out in WKL₀.

Project role and claim boundary

Preserve the code for the tree or compactness problem and its connection to the physical obligation. The project’s WR reversal remains an ACA₀ equivalence over RCA₀; introducing this glossary entry does not alter that bound.
