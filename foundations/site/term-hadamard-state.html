Hadamard state

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A Hadamard state is a quantum state with a prescribed short-distance correlation structure suited to the spacetime field problem. The condition controls how its idealized correlations become singular at very small separations.

Example and distinction

It does not mean the correlations are smooth everywhere. Nor does a candidate with the right singularity pattern automatically have nonnegative probabilities; without positivity, it has not yet earned the full state interpretation.

Physics

The Hadamard requirement fixes the local vacuum-like singularity class of two-point data for the declared linear field theory. It supports the definition of local renormalized quantities under the appropriate framework.

Use in the model

A positive state must also satisfy the field relations and normalization; gauge theories add compatibility requirements. Checking the singularity class alone does not construct a BRST-compatible physical state for the full metric complex.

Mathematics

The Hadamard condition has a microlocal formulation restricting the two-point distribution’s wavefront set to specified null-related covectors with an orientation condition. Its exact version depends on the field operator and conventions.

Conditions and consequences

Smooth changes do not alter that singularity structure, but they can affect positivity. A theorem about candidate distributions should not call them states before the positive-functional conditions have been established.

Specialist

The reduced cutoff theorem fixes a reference singularity and commutator and excludes an admissible smooth positive repair on its full declared mode family. Its dependency is REDUCED-MODE.

Project role and claim boundary

This is not a theorem that the full metric BRST-compatible Hadamard state has been constructed, nor a universal obstruction for every changed carrier. The classical import and full observable comparison remain separate gates.
