RCA₀ and ACA₀

Reverse mathematics; RCA₀ and ACA₀ are distinct axiom systems described together here.

General

Two systems of mathematical axioms. The example compares what can be proved using the weaker system and a stronger one.

Physics

RCA₀ is the weak arithmetic base used for coded constructions. ACA₀ adds arithmetic comprehension, allowing sets defined by arithmetic conditions.

Mathematics

Subsystems of second-order arithmetic. RCA₀ provides the recursive-comprehension base; ACA₀ extends it by arithmetic comprehension.

Specialist

The stated equivalence is over RCA₀ and concerns the exact second-order WR representation, not a uniform higher-type operator.
