ACA₀

A stronger axiom system used in the wave reconstruction equivalence.

Stands for: Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom

General

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. ACA₀ extends the starting rulebook RCA₀ with permission to collect numbers satisfying an arithmetic condition into a set, even when no general decision procedure is supplied.

Why an existence rule helps

An approximation sequence may eventually settle at every accuracy without telling you where it settles. The proof can use the stronger rulebook to collect enough information to specify a reliable limiting answer.

What “equivalent” means

For this precisely stated mathematical problem, ACA₀ is enough to obtain the answer, and the ability to solve every allowed input is enough to recover the extra rule. Both directions are needed.

What this does not mean

The result does not say that a laboratory needs an axiom system to function. Supplying an error schedule changes the mathematical input and avoids this extra requirement in the comparison.

Names that must stay distinct

The subscript zero is part of this system’s name. Bare ACA can denote a different system, so this dictionary no longer automatically treats the two spellings as interchangeable.

Physics

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. ACA₀ adds arithmetic comprehension to the base theory. In this wave problem it supplies missing tail-selection information needed to turn a rate-free Cauchy input into the required named limit.

Location of the extra assumption

The upper-bound proof forms the condition that all approximants beyond N are close at a requested tolerance, then obtains witnesses for those tails. Propagation and detector integration are handled separately by explicit estimates.

Why the converse is interesting

The detector can encode an arbitrary limiting scalar amplitude even at time zero. Recovering the requested output therefore cannot be justified solely by pointing to the simplicity of the wave dynamics.

Reading the result correctly

This is a logical calibration of a representation and completion task. It is not a new physical interaction, a numerical complexity estimate, or a general obstruction to finite measurements.

Names that must stay distinct

An ACA₀ requirement concerns existence for the specified rate-free input and fast output. It is neither a bound on laboratory resources nor an additional force acting on the wave.

Mathematics

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. The extension of RCA₀ by comprehension for arithmetical formulas: number quantifiers are allowed, with set parameters, but no bound set variables. The wave statement WR is equivalent to this extension over RCA₀.

Comprehension versus an algorithm

Existence of a set defined by an arithmetical condition need not supply a computable procedure deciding membership. Number quantifiers can express noncomputable conditions even when the underlying data have effective codes.

Standard characterization used

Over RCA₀, existence of ranges of injections characterizes ACA₀. The reduction encodes such a range in a limiting amplitude and decodes it from the fast output name.

Boundary of the comparison

This does not assert all possible comprehension principles. The theorem names ACA₀ specifically and relies on the exact coding of its input promise and output function.

Names that must stay distinct

The base and language determine the comparison: number quantifiers with set parameters are permitted in arithmetical comprehension. This is not comprehension over arbitrary predicates quantifying over sets.

Specialist

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. The exact strength of WR over RCA₀ for the declared rate-free representation. The upper bound selects Cauchy-tail witnesses; the lower bound recovers range existence through one scalar amplitude at t=0.

Upper and lower bounds

The tail predicate is arithmetical in the input code, so comprehension supplies a witness-selection construction. Conversely, a fixed profile and detector recover the amplitude code needed for range existence.

Contribution and standard background

The scalar completeness reversal is standard. The project realizes it within its fixed detector benchmark and retains a supplied-rate comparison and exact finite reduction witnesses.

No automatic promotion

The result is not a higher-type uniform operator theorem or a theorem about the full metric BV complex. Computational replay of finite witnesses does not certify the entire infinite reversal.

Names that must stay distinct

The statement stays an equivalence over RCA₀ for WR. Retain the original coding, fixed detector and infinite-proof boundary when citing the result.

Action and Lagrangian

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

An action assigns a number to a possible history of a system. A Lagrangian supplies the local ingredients used to build that number. Requiring the action not to change to first order under allowed variations produces equations of motion.

Example and distinction

This is a compact way to specify dynamics, but it does not solve the equations or tell us which states and measurements are physically acceptable. Boundary conditions also affect which variations are allowed.

Physics

An action functional is usually an integral of a Lagrangian density, together with any boundary terms. Its stationary variations produce Euler–Lagrange equations under stated boundary conditions.

Use in the model

Gauge symmetry and higher derivatives can introduce constraints and additional boundary data. A formally invariant action does not establish causal well-posedness, positivity or quantum consistency.

Mathematics

A Lagrangian density depends on fields and specified derivatives; integration gives a functional whose first variation defines the equations and boundary contributions. The function space and variation class matter.

Conditions and consequences

Formal integration by parts requires the appropriate boundary or support assumptions. Local densities, integrated functionals modulo boundary terms and residual classes are distinct mathematical objects in the programme.

Specialist

The pure-Weyl action is the starting point of a multi-stage construction, not its completion. Keep local variational identities, the encoded classical complex and quantum counterterm classification in their own result kinds.

Project role and claim boundary

No quantum coefficient or determinant should be substituted for a BV master-equation breaking. The classical import gate remains authoritative for any subsequent quantum use of transferred data.

Algebra

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

General

An algebra is a mathematical collection whose elements can be added and multiplied, with specified rules. For observables, multiplication helps describe combinations of quantities being studied.

Example and distinction

The rules matter: multiplying in one order may give a different result from multiplying in the reverse order. An algebra’s existence does not alone tell us which of its elements represent measurements or how probabilities are assigned.

Physics

An observable algebra packages combinations of observables, often with an involution representing adjoints. States then assign compatible expectation values on that declared algebra.

Use in the model

A finite matrix algebra can model noncommuting observables exactly. Extending it to a continuum or local field algebra requires additional structure and does not follow simply by increasing the number of matrix entries.

Mathematics

An algebra over a scalar field is a vector space with a bilinear multiplication satisfying the specified axioms. Associativity, a unit, commutativity and an involution are additional choices.

Conditions and consequences

Topological or operator algebras carry further analytic conditions. An algebraic identity in a finite carrier need not extend continuously to a completion unless the multiplication and relevant domains are controlled.

Specialist

Keep finite exact algebras, local functional algebras and represented quantum observable algebras distinct. Their states, ideals and quotients answer different obligations.

Project role and claim boundary

A local algebraic quantum calculation does not provide renormalized Lorentzian products or a positive representation of the full theory. The result type and dependency tag must identify which algebra was actually constructed.

Algebraic quantum field theory

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

Stands for: Algebraic Quantum Field Theory

General

AQFT stands for Algebraic Quantum Field Theory. It starts with algebras of observables associated with regions of spacetime and asks how those algebras relate. States then supply expectation values and probabilities.

Example and distinction

This keeps the rules for observables distinct from a particular state-vector description. The perturbative form, pAQFT, develops interactions order by order and still needs precise causal and renormalization conditions.

Physics

Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT) organizes observables, locality and dynamics through algebraic relations. Its perturbative variant, pAQFT, constructs interacting observables as formal perturbative objects under specified hypotheses.

Use in the model

An algebraic formulation does not remove the state problem. The relevant positive representations, singularity control and gauge consistency must still be established for the model under study.

Mathematics

Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT) assigns compatible observable algebras to spacetime regions, with axioms expressing properties such as isotony and causal compatibility. States are positive normalized functionals in the relevant setting.

Conditions and consequences

The precise category and axioms vary by formulation. A locally covariant or perturbative construction is an additional specification, not something automatically attached to any algebra of finite matrices.

Specialist

Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (AQFT), including perturbative AQFT, names a target framework rather than a completed result of this programme. A causal perturbative construction for the full metric complex remains uncertified.

Project role and claim boundary

Local BV calculations and reduced-mode state tests contribute separately scoped evidence. They cannot certify full Lorentzian causal products, compatible states or the quantum master equation by adopting the AQFT label.

Anomaly

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

An anomaly is a failure of a symmetry or consistency identity when a classical description is carried into the relevant quantum construction. The precise identity matters.

Example and distinction

Some apparent failures can be removed by an allowed adjustment; others represent an obstruction within the stated rules. The word anomaly should therefore name what failed and whether its removal has actually been shown.

Physics

An anomaly can refer to different quantum effects, including current or trace identities and BV master-equation breakings. These objects are related only through specified arguments, not by terminology alone.

Use in the model

Classifying a possible anomaly is not computing its coefficient. Computing a nonzero expression is not yet proving it is unremovable by an admissible local counterterm.

Mathematics

A local anomaly problem often imposes a consistency condition and identifies breakings modulo variations of allowed counterterms. The resulting cohomology depends on degree, locality and the quotient convention.

Conditions and consequences

A cocycle can be trivial in one space but nontrivial in a restricted one. Keep pointwise densities, integrated functionals and residual classes separate when formulating the obstruction.

Specialist

The programme separates CLASSIFIED, COEFFICIENT_COMPUTED and QME_RESTORED results. A local cancellation is a LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC conclusion, not a LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL construction.

Project role and claim boundary

A background trace anomaly or beta function must not be used as a BV breaking without its explicit identification. No residual or full quantum lifecycle state is promoted merely by publishing an anomaly explanation.

Atlas cell and evidence grade

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

An atlas cell is one question formed by choosing mathematical assumptions, a kind of mathematical setting and a job for a physical theory. Its evidence grade describes the kind of support recorded for that question.

Example and distinction

The 576 cells are not 576 universes. A colored cell may concern a limited example or a scoped result, and many successful cells may use incompatible constructions. Read the evidence and limits behind the color.

Physics

The atlas crosses six foundational regimes, six carrier families and sixteen obligations. Cell evidence identifies what has been constructed, bounded, ruled out or left conditional at that coordinate.

Use in the model

Coverage is not composition. A theory profile can collect results from different realizations; a theory journey must show that the states, dynamics and observables belong to one compatible route.

Mathematics

A cell is an organizational record, not a universally quantified theorem about every object matching its row and column labels. The underlying statement, witness and assumptions determine its logical force.

Conditions and consequences

The table’s geometry does not supply implications between adjacent cells. Claims about transport require explicit maps and preservation conditions, independently of the grade assigned to either endpoint.

Specialist

Treat grades as evidence classifications with scoped provenance, not confidence scores or lifecycle promotions. Inspect dependency tags and does-not-establish fields before reusing a result.

Project role and claim boundary

No grid coverage count certifies the full classical import gate or a Lorentzian quantum theory. A failed prerequisite must block dependent claims even if nearby records remain valid.

Axiom and axiom system

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

An axiom is a rule accepted as a starting point for an argument. An axiom system is a collection of such rules. A theorem is a conclusion proved from them.

Example and distinction

Think of agreeing on the rules of a game before asking which moves are possible. In mathematics, a different rulebook can change which infinite objects can be proved to exist. This is separate from deciding which physical assumptions describe nature.

Physics

An axiom system specifies the mathematical rules available to a model’s proofs. It is distinct from a physical postulate such as locality or a symmetry assumption.

Use in the model

The same field equation can be studied with different existence principles. When comparing constructions, keep both the physical premises and the mathematical rulebook fixed unless the comparison explicitly changes one of them.

Mathematics

A theory consists of axioms in a specified formal language together with their deductive consequences. The language and underlying logic are part of the specification.

Conditions and consequences

In reverse mathematics the relevant theories concern numbers and sets of numbers. A statement’s provability is relative to a base theory; mathematical truth in an intended model and derivability in a formal system are different notions.

Specialist

Axioms identify the deductive environment of a scoped result. In the atlas, logical principles must remain distinct from carrier structure and encoded physical premises.

Project role and claim boundary

A claimed implication should expose which assumptions are discharged and which remain parameters. The atlas’s organizational notation is not evidence that every represented claim has been formalized in a proof assistant.

Base theory

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A base theory is the shared mathematical starting point used to compare two claims. It tells us which reasoning is already allowed before asking what an extra assumption adds.

Example and distinction

If both sides can already use ordinary finite calculations, those calculations are not the extra cost being measured. In the wave example, the question is what is additionally needed to reconstruct a limit when no error schedule is supplied.

Physics

The base theory fixes the mathematical resources allowed in a strength comparison. It plays a different role from a background spacetime or a reference physical model.

Use in the model

The supplied-rate wave reconstruction is proved in RCA₀. Comparing it with the rate-free assertion measures additional existence assumptions under that base, rather than extra dynamics, experimental precision or computing time.

Mathematics

A base theory is the formal theory over which an implication or equivalence is proved. Its coding conventions and induction and comprehension axioms affect the statement.

Conditions and consequences

To write T equivalent to ACA₀ over RCA₀ is to assert that RCA₀ proves the required directions. Changing the base can alter the strength comparison, so the base cannot be dropped from the theorem’s formulation.

Specialist

The base is a dependency of the reverse implication, not an informal label for an easy proof. Different finite fixtures and infinite assertions may legitimately use different bases.

Project role and claim boundary

The ladder’s fixed-fixture PRA claims and the wave reconstruction over RCA₀ must retain their quantifier and representation boundaries. A family of finite checks is not automatically a uniform theorem in the advertised base.

Born rule

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

The Born rule is the quantum rule connecting a state to probabilities of measurement outcomes. In a simple state-vector description, probabilities come from squared amplitudes.

Example and distinction

Squaring an amplitude is only meaningful after specifying the state, measurement and normalization. A model with equations but no acceptable probability assignment has not yet supplied the predictions this rule is meant to describe.

Physics

For a normalized state and a projective measurement, the Born rule assigns an outcome probability from the corresponding projection’s expectation value. More general measurements use positive effects summing to the identity.

Use in the model

Positivity and normalization are essential, and the observable representation must be specified. Real frequencies or positive classical energy alone do not supply those ingredients.

Mathematics

In a Hilbert representation, a normalized vector ψ assigns a projection E the value ⟨ψ,Eψ⟩. A density operator ρ gives Tr(ρE); algebraic states evaluate admissible measurement effects.

Conditions and consequences

The Hilbert inner product or positive functional is structural data, not notation that can be attached to an arbitrary indefinite pairing. The model must also identify which effects represent the intended measurements.

Specialist

The probability obligation is distinct from spectral reality, gauge-complex consistency and local quantum identity restoration. A residual class does not already define a Born-rule state.

Project role and claim boundary

When comparing carriers, verify that the operational identification and positive normalization are preserved. An algebraic correspondence alone cannot justify transporting empirical probabilities.

Bound and estimate

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

General

A bound limits how large or small a specified quantity can be. An estimate gives a controlled comparison. We need to know what is bounded, under which conditions and with which constants.

Example and distinction

A bound on detector error is not a bound on computing time. The project also uses “upper bound” for sufficient mathematical axioms, which is a different use from a numerical inequality.

Physics

An analytic estimate controls quantities such as energy, response or truncation error. Its dependence on data, cutoff and background determines what physical conclusion it can support.

Use in the model

A sufficient stability estimate can fail without disproving every stable construction. Keep uncertainty-weighted observational scores, analytic norms and logical strength bounds separate even when all are summarized numerically or by an ordering.

Mathematics

An estimate states an inequality on a declared domain, including parameter dependence and constants. A logical upper bound instead proves an assertion from a specified principle; a lower bound derives a principle from the assertion.

Conditions and consequences

Uniformity and effective availability of constants can change the theorem. A bare existence assertion for an estimate does not necessarily supply the modulus needed by a reconstruction procedure.

Specialist

State the controlled object, quantifiers and dependency tags before interpreting a bound. The wave reversal’s lower bound concerns named reconstruction, while the reduced cutoff result concerns full regularity of corrections.

Project role and claim boundary

Neither is a runtime theorem or an empirical accuracy estimate. A failed universal Euclidean estimate targets that sufficient route, not every possible realization of the proposed physics.

Boundary condition

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A boundary condition specifies how a field behaves at the edge of its domain. It helps determine which solutions an equation allows.

Example and distinction

A string fixed at both ends vibrates differently from one with a free end, even if the same local wave equation describes its interior. Conditions at infinity or periodic identifications play related roles in other models.

Physics

Boundary conditions constrain fields, variations or fluxes at a physical, artificial or asymptotic boundary. They affect allowed modes, conserved quantities and operator domains.

Use in the model

They must be compatible with the intended gauge structure and variational principle. Choosing convenient mode conditions does not automatically preserve the full boundary data needed by a classical or quantum construction.

Mathematics

Boundary data select a domain for a differential operator or a trace condition for solutions. Different choices can produce different self-adjoint extensions, spectra and uniqueness properties.

Conditions and consequences

Initial conditions and boundary conditions answer different parts of an evolution problem. Formal integration by parts only discards boundary terms when the chosen support or boundary assumptions justify doing so.

Specialist

Record boundary assumptions with the action, operator and import certificate. Local identities modulo boundary terms are not identities of pointwise densities or automatically boundary-compatible transfers.

Project role and claim boundary

The periodic wave benchmark and a full metric theory with boundary have different obligations. No boundary construction should be inferred merely from the existence of its bulk complex or finite mode spectrum.

BRST formalism

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

Stands for: Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin

General

BRST stands for Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin. The formalism adds graded bookkeeping variables called ghosts to organize gauge redundancy through an operation that is required to square to zero.

Example and distinction

Passing that consistency test lets one ask which objects survive after trivial changes are removed. It does not automatically show that the survivors are particles with positive probabilities.

Physics

Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin (BRST) methods encode gauge structure using a graded differential. Depending on the gauge algebra and formulation, closure and the required field content need explicit treatment.

Use in the model

Cohomology can describe observables or other classes, but the complex, degree and domains determine the result. A BRST-compatible state also needs its positivity and analytic conditions; nilpotency alone does not supply them.

Mathematics

Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin (BRST) structure is expressed by a degree-one differential on a graded space or algebra. The square-zero identity makes closed elements modulo exact ones meaningful.

Conditions and consequences

The differential may encode more than a linear gauge generator. Local functionals, integrated quotients and state spaces provide different domains, and the associated cohomologies should not be identified without a map.

Specialist

Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin (BRST) identities must be checked on the actual imported operator. A formal expression bearing the name does not certify the current serialized full complex.

Project role and claim boundary

The programme separates local BRST classification, residual deformation classes and positive physical states. Its failed full-export prerequisite blocks dependent transfer while leaving separately certified scoped calculations intact.

BV formalism

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

Stands for: Batalin–Vilkovisky

General

Convergence rate (modulus)

Cauchy convergence in the wave input; “modulus” has other mathematical meanings.

General

An error schedule: for each requested accuracy, it tells you how far along an approximation sequence you must go so that all later approximations agree to that accuracy.

A practical comparison

“The readings will eventually agree within one thousandth” gives you no stopping point. “From reading 500 onward, every pair differs by at most one thousandth” gives you one usable guarantee. A modulus provides such a guarantee for every requested accuracy.

Why it matters here

A computer can produce better approximations without knowing when they are good enough. The wave comparison separates these two kinds of information: an eventual-agreement promise and a supplied error schedule.

A limit on the analogy

These are mathematical guarantees for an ideal sequence. A few stable readings do not certify that every future reading will remain close.

Use the schedule at the right interface

An error schedule is useful only when it describes the error you actually care about. A schedule for the input wave must be connected by an estimate to the detector’s output accuracy.

Physics

A tolerance-to-index function controlling all later differences between input approximants in a specified norm. Supplying this function is stronger input information than promising that the sequence eventually settles.

The norm is part of the claim

A mode cutoff or numerical resolution is useful only together with an error bound in the topology required by the output. In this benchmark the input metric controls the smeared observable uniformly in time.

Using the supplied rate

Given tolerance 2⁻ᵏ, select an input far enough along using μ, then spend the remaining error budget on interpolation of the detector response. The finite computations are unchanged when μ is supplied rather than obtained by a stronger existence principle.

Boundary

A convergence modulus is not a physical frequency, a decay constant, or an empirical fit to a few errors. It certifies an entire tail of the specified approximation sequence.

Use the schedule at the right interface

The detector continuity estimate transports input error control to output control. The supplied modulus provides tail information that the continuity estimate alone does not create.

Mathematics

A function μ such that m,n≥μ(k) implies d(pₘ,pₙ)≤2⁻ᵏ. The Cauchy statement asserts existence of a suitable index for each k; a supplied modulus gives those indices as one input function.

Quantifier distinction

The promise has form ∀k ∃N ∀m,n≥N d(pₘ,pₙ)≤2⁻ᵏ. It does not include a function collecting witnesses N. In a complete ambient space the tail estimate also bounds distance to the limit, but assuming that limit as already named would change the input problem.

An elementary example

For pₙ=2⁻ⁿ in the rationals with the usual metric, μ(k)=k suffices: any two terms with indices at least k differ by at most 2⁻ᵏ. A general Cauchy promise does not present such a formula.

Related notions

A modulus of continuity controls changes in the output as the argument changes. A convergence modulus controls the tail of a sequence. The wave output representation supplies continuity information separately.

A modulus for a Cauchy sequence, a modulus of continuity and a bound on truncation error are different functions. Each must state its domain and the quantified condition it witnesses.

General

Gauge fixing chooses representatives among descriptions intended to represent the same physical situation. Constraints restrict which data or configurations are admissible. The two operations are related but not identical.

Example and distinction

Removing a variable because it is inconvenient is not enough. One must show that the choice preserves the intended physical possibilities and that the remaining data still satisfy the relevant restrictions.

Physics

Gauge conditions address redundant descriptions; constraints encode relations that admissible data or solutions must satisfy. Their compatibility, propagation and possible residual gauge freedom depend on the formulation.

Use in the model

A gauge-fixed operator may be easier to invert, but that inverse must still fit the gauge complex. A discarded classical direction is not proven unphysical merely because one convenient gauge omits it.

Mathematics

Gauge fixing can be formulated as a slice, a choice of additional equations or an algebraic procedure on an extended complex. Constraints define a subspace or submanifold only under suitable assumptions.

Conditions and consequences

Existence and uniqueness of representatives can fail globally. Quotienting, imposing equations and choosing coordinates are different mathematical operations and need explicit comparison maps.

Specialist

Retain gauge conditions, constraint class and allowed field content in propagation certificates. Cyclicity or principal-symbol success in a selected ansatz is not certification of every gauge condition.

Gauge symmetry

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Gauge symmetry describes different mathematical descriptions of the same physical situation. It tells us that some changes in the variables should not count as changes in what is physically observed.

Example and distinction

A coordinate description can contain information that no detector distinguishes. Removing that redundancy requires a rule; simply deleting an unwanted solution is not enough to show that it was redundant.

Physics

Gauge symmetry organizes redundancies of field descriptions. Gauge fixing, constraints and BRST methods address that redundancy, but their precise admissible spaces matter.

Use in the model

An extra solution direction must be tested for gauge triviality rather than assumed unphysical. Even a direction surviving that test still needs nonlinear and quantum interpretation checks before it is called a physical particle.

Mathematics

Gauge reduction identifies configurations under a specified equivalence, often encoded infinitesimally by a complex. The quotient and any cohomological replacement depend on the actual maps and domains.

The square-zero condition is required before the usual cohomology is defined. Compatibility with pairings, support and boundary structures is additional data, not a consequence of taking a formal quotient.

Hilbert space

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A Hilbert space is a space with an inner product in which every Cauchy sequence has a limit. It combines a way to measure length with a guarantee that appropriate approximations stay within the space.

Example and distinction

Quantum theory often uses such spaces for state vectors. Merely arranging finite wave modes into vectors does not construct the completed space or show that its vectors represent acceptable physical states.

Physics

A Hilbert space is a complete positive inner-product space. It provides a familiar state-vector setting, but a physical model still needs operators, domains and a probability interpretation.

Use in the model

Gauge variables may initially live in a larger indefinite setting. Passing to a positive physical Hilbert space requires a separate construction; reality of a spectrum or positivity at finite cutoff is not a substitute.

Mathematics

A Hilbert space is an inner-product space complete in its induced norm. Its topology and completeness are part of the structure, not properties of an underlying vector space alone.

Conditions and consequences

A completion of a pre-Hilbert space is defined relative to its positive inner product. In weak foundations, coding that completion and asserting existence of particular limits must be distinguished explicitly.

Specialist

A Hilbert realization is one carrier choice in the atlas, not a universal destination already certified by the classical complex. Positivity and operator-domain conditions remain separate dependencies.

Indefinite inner product

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

An indefinite pairing can give either sign when an object is paired with itself. Unlike a positive inner product, it does not directly define an ordinary squared length or a probability.

Example and distinction

Such pairings can be useful in a redundant gauge description. A separate argument is needed to obtain the physical state space and show that its probability assignments are nonnegative.

Physics

Indefinite inner-product formalisms allow negative-norm and possibly null directions in an enlarged description. Their physical treatment depends on constraints, gauge reduction and the observable representation.

Use in the model

Preservation of an indefinite form is not the same as unitary evolution on a positive Hilbert space. Positive energy and real frequencies are also separate properties.

Mathematics

An indefinite Hermitian form lacks positive definiteness. It therefore does not induce a norm by taking the square root of the self-pairing, even when it is nondegenerate.

Conditions and consequences

A nonzero null vector need not lie in the radical of the form. Quotienting a null subspace requires the correct compatibility conditions; it is not automatic removal of every vector with zero self-pairing.

Specialist

General

Example and distinction

Physics

An independent check targets failure modes not inherited from the producer, for example by using a distinct reduction or verifying witnesses against original operators. Reproducibility is a separate property.

Finite independent checks do not convert a written infinite argument into a machine-checked proof. Conversely, a formal proof still depends on the correctness and relevance of its formal statement and trusted foundation.

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A pairing turns two mathematical objects into a number. An inner product is a special pairing with positivity properties that support a notion of length and angle.

Example and distinction

Physics

A pairing can couple fields, dual variables or graded objects. A positive inner product provides the Hilbert-space geometry used for ordinary quantum probabilities.

Use in the model

Mathematics

An inner product is a positive-definite symmetric bilinear form over the reals, or Hermitian sesquilinear form over the complexes. A general pairing may be degenerate, indefinite or between distinct spaces.

Pairing compatibility is an independent import and transfer obligation. Nilpotency and chain-map identities alone do not certify cyclicity, nondegeneracy or positive state interpretation.

Kernel and image

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

General

The kernel of a linear map contains the inputs it sends to zero. Its image contains the outputs it can actually produce. These two collections reveal what the map loses and what it reaches.

Example and distinction

If a map only reads one component of a vector, changes in the other components are invisible to it. Cohomology compares two related collections of this kind; “kernel” elsewhere can also mean an integral kernel, a different object.

Physics

For a linear operator, the kernel solves the homogeneous equation and the image describes reachable sources or outputs. Both depend on the domain and boundary conditions.

Use in the model

Kernel vectors can represent solutions, redundancies or residual directions according to the operator. Their existence alone does not give a particle interpretation, and finite rank calculations need not settle infinite-dimensional range questions.

Mathematics

For T:V→W, ker T={v:T(v)=0} and im T={T(v):v∈V}. In a complex, d²=0 implies im d is contained in the next kernel.

Conditions and consequences

The algebraic image and its topological closure can differ. Reduced cohomology sometimes uses that closure, so the quotient convention must be specified rather than inferred from finite-dimensional intuition.

Specialist

Cocycle and trivialization calculations must use the actual operator and domain. An expected residual basis cannot be hard-coded as the kernel computation’s answer.

General

Uniformity means that one rule or bound works across the whole stated collection of cases. Without it, each case may need its own adjustment, with no control over how those adjustments grow.

Example and distinction

You might repair every finite mode model separately while needing larger and larger corrections. That does not give one controlled repair of the unlimited model. The set of cases and the quantity controlled must both be named.

Physics

A uniform estimate bounds a specified quantity independently of the parameter being varied. The parameter might be a cutoff, time range, background or input family.

Use in the model

Mathematics

Specify the index set, norm or seminorm and remaining parameter dependence before claiming uniform control. Uniform operator existence is also distinct from an instance-by-instance theorem in weak foundations.

Physics

Use in the model

Mathematics

General

Example and distinction

Physics

Use in the model
