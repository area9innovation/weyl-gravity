ACA₀

A stronger axiom system used in the wave reconstruction equivalence.

Stands for: Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom

General

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. ACA₀ extends the starting rulebook RCA₀ with permission to collect numbers satisfying an arithmetic condition into a set, even when no general decision procedure is supplied.

Why an existence rule helps

An approximation sequence may eventually settle at every accuracy without telling you where it settles. The proof can use the stronger rulebook to collect enough information to specify a reliable limiting answer.

What “equivalent” means

For this precisely stated mathematical problem, ACA₀ is enough to obtain the answer, and the ability to solve every allowed input is enough to recover the extra rule. Both directions are needed.

What this does not mean

The result does not say that a laboratory needs an axiom system to function. Supplying an error schedule changes the mathematical input and avoids this extra requirement in the comparison.

Physics

ACA₀ adds arithmetic comprehension to the base theory. In this wave problem it supplies missing tail-selection information needed to turn a rate-free Cauchy input into the required named limit.

Location of the extra assumption

The upper-bound proof forms the condition that all approximants beyond N are close at a requested tolerance, then obtains witnesses for those tails. Propagation and detector integration are handled separately by explicit estimates.

Why the converse is interesting

The detector can encode an arbitrary limiting scalar amplitude even at time zero. Recovering the requested output therefore cannot be justified solely by pointing to the simplicity of the wave dynamics.

Reading the result correctly

This is a logical calibration of a representation and completion task. It is not a new physical interaction, a numerical complexity estimate, or a general obstruction to finite measurements.

Mathematics

The extension of RCA₀ by comprehension for arithmetical formulas: number quantifiers are allowed, with set parameters, but no bound set variables. The wave statement WR is equivalent to this extension over RCA₀.

Comprehension versus an algorithm

Existence of a set defined by an arithmetical condition need not supply a computable procedure deciding membership. Number quantifiers can express noncomputable conditions even when the underlying data have effective codes.

Standard characterization used

Over RCA₀, existence of ranges of injections characterizes ACA₀. The reduction encodes such a range in a limiting amplitude and decodes it from the fast output name.

Boundary of the comparison

This does not assert all possible comprehension principles. The theorem names ACA₀ specifically and relies on the exact coding of its input promise and output function.

Specialist

The exact strength of WR over RCA₀ for the declared rate-free representation. The upper bound selects Cauchy-tail witnesses; the lower bound recovers range existence through one scalar amplitude at t=0.

Upper and lower bounds

The tail predicate is arithmetical in the input code, so comprehension supplies a witness-selection construction. Conversely, a fixed profile and detector recover the amplitude code needed for range existence.

Contribution and standard background

The scalar completeness reversal is standard. The project realizes it within its fixed detector benchmark and retains a supplied-rate comparison and exact finite reduction witnesses.

No automatic promotion

The result is not a higher-type uniform operator theorem or a theorem about the full metric BV complex. Computational replay of finite witnesses does not certify the entire infinite reversal.

Carrier

Project vocabulary for the mathematical setting supporting a construction; not a material medium.

General

The kind of mathematical structure in which a model is built. A finite collection of matrices, a space of waves, and an algebra of quantum quantities are different carriers, with different questions that must be checked.

Why the choice matters

A finite table of numbers lets you perform exact finite calculations. A whole wave may require infinitely many approximations plus a rule explaining their limit. Both can model aspects of the same problem, but success with one does not automatically settle the other.

Reading a matrix cell

The carrier tells you what mathematical objects that cell is discussing. The other dimensions ask what assumptions are allowed and which job—such as defining states or proving evolution—has been completed.

Avoid a common confusion

This word does not mean the substance that carries a physical wave. It describes the mathematical setting used to investigate it.

Physics

The specified mathematical realization carrying the model’s variables, operations and states: for example finite matrices, a completed function space or a local algebraic construction. Changing carrier may change which analytic obligations are meaningful.

Finite and continuum settings

A finite mode truncation supplies matrices and finite sums. Passing to a completed field space can add convergence, domain, positivity and causal-support obligations. A successful truncation is evidence about that truncation unless a transfer theorem is given.

What to inspect

Look for the space or algebra, the chosen topology, admissible states, domains of the dynamics, and maps connecting it to another realization. These determine whether two calculations address the same quantity.

Boundary

The atlas tracks different carriers explicitly so their established results cannot silently be combined into an unproved complete theory.

Mathematics

The chosen structured ambient object, including any topology, pairing, operator domains and completion data needed for a result. The matrix uses carrier as an organizational dimension rather than as one universally defined category.

More than an underlying set

An identical vector space with two different topologies or pairings may support different continuity or positivity claims. A useful carrier specification therefore includes the structures used in the theorem.

Transfer question

To carry a result to another realization, specify maps and prove the required compatibility—such as continuity, intertwining of differentials, or preservation of the pairing. The necessary conditions depend on the claimed result.

Project usage

Carrier is an editorial organizing term here, not a claim that all entries form one category with interchangeable morphisms.

Specialist

The typed support of a construction in the atlas. Cross-carrier transfer requires a declared interface preserving the relevant operations and hypotheses; matching labels or finite fixtures do not establish equivalence.

Interface obligations

State the source and target kinds and the exact property transported. A chain map, an SDR, a cyclic pairing identification and a causal operator identification answer different questions; one cannot substitute for the others.

Why it matters to the programme

Reduced-mode certificates and local algebraic checks retain their dependency tags when compared with full Lorentzian targets. A positive finite test does not close the missing analytic transfer gates.

Boundary

Read each interface certificate and its excluded claims. A coverage mark is neither a functorial construction nor proof of physical equivalence.

Convergence rate (modulus)

Cauchy convergence in the wave input; “modulus” has other mathematical meanings.

General

An error schedule: for each requested accuracy, it tells you how far along an approximation sequence you must go so that all later approximations agree to that accuracy.

A practical comparison

“The readings will eventually agree within one thousandth” gives you no stopping point. “From reading 500 onward, every pair differs by at most one thousandth” gives you one usable guarantee. A modulus provides such a guarantee for every requested accuracy.

Why it matters here

A computer can produce better approximations without knowing when they are good enough. The wave comparison separates these two kinds of information: an eventual-agreement promise and a supplied error schedule.

A limit on the analogy

These are mathematical guarantees for an ideal sequence. A few stable readings do not certify that every future reading will remain close.

Physics

A tolerance-to-index function controlling all later differences between input approximants in a specified norm. Supplying this function is stronger input information than promising that the sequence eventually settles.

The norm is part of the claim

A mode cutoff or numerical resolution is useful only together with an error bound in the topology required by the output. In this benchmark the input metric controls the smeared observable uniformly in time.

Using the supplied rate

Given tolerance 2⁻ᵏ, select an input far enough along using μ, then spend the remaining error budget on interpolation of the detector response. The finite computations are unchanged when μ is supplied rather than obtained by a stronger existence principle.

Boundary

A convergence modulus is not a physical frequency, a decay constant, or an empirical fit to a few errors. It certifies an entire tail of the specified approximation sequence.

Mathematics

A function μ such that m,n≥μ(k) implies d(pₘ,pₙ)≤2⁻ᵏ. The Cauchy statement asserts existence of a suitable index for each k; a supplied modulus gives those indices as one input function.

Quantifier distinction

The promise has form ∀k ∃N ∀m,n≥N d(pₘ,pₙ)≤2⁻ᵏ. It does not include a function collecting witnesses N. In a complete ambient space the tail estimate also bounds distance to the limit, but assuming that limit as already named would change the input problem.

An elementary example

For pₙ=2⁻ⁿ in the rationals with the usual metric, μ(k)=k suffices: any two terms with indices at least k differ by at most 2⁻ᵏ. A general Cauchy promise does not present such a formula.

Related notions

A modulus of continuity controls changes in the output as the argument changes. A convergence modulus controls the tail of a sequence. The wave output representation supplies continuity information separately.

Specialist

An input function witnessing the ordinary-Cauchy promise in the exact second-order representation. WR omits it; WRμ includes it. This changes the completion obligation while preserving the detector and dynamics.

Representation contract

The input consists of rational step-pair codes with a unit bound. Its ordinary-Cauchy promise is arithmetic in those codes. WRμ additionally supplies μ; replacing the input with an already completed state name would hide this extra information.

Where the upper bound spends strength

ACA₀ forms the arithmetic tail predicate and collects suitable witnesses. With μ supplied, monotone majorization and finite interpolation suffice over RCA₀. The stronger principle is used for completion advice, not for the translation dynamics.

Boundary

The equivalence concerns ∀ input ∃ output in second-order arithmetic. It is not an asserted uniform higher-type solution operator, nor a statement that every physically given source lacks a usable rate.

Observable

General concept with distinct classical, quantum and wave-example realizations.

General

A quantity a model connects to a possible measurement, such as position, energy, or an average wave amplitude. The observable tells us what we are asking about; the state supplies the information used to predict it.

How to think about it

A description of a wave contains much more information than one detector reports. To predict that report, the model needs a rule that turns the wave description into a number. That rule is the observable. Choosing a different rule asks a different measurement question.

Example here

The wave example averages the wave over space, giving some locations more weight than others. Small errors in the wave data then produce controlled errors in this average. The project asks what information is needed to obtain a reliable limiting prediction.

What it does not tell you

Predicting this average does not reconstruct the entire wave, and the mathematical averaging rule is not itself a calibrated laboratory instrument.

Physics

A specified quantity evaluated on a classical state, or represented by an appropriate operator or algebra element in quantum theory. Its mathematical definition and domain must be fixed before discussing predictions.

Classical and quantum uses

Classically an observable is often a function on phase space. Quantum mechanically a state supplies expectation values for observables; self-adjoint operators model real-valued sharp quantities, with domain conditions for unbounded operators. A specified expectation value is not an individual measurement outcome.

The wave calculation

For chiral data p=(a,b), the fixed detector evaluates Oₚ(t)=∫₀¹h(x)[a(x−t)+b(x+t)]dx. The tent weight h is fixed rather than optimized with the input. The energy-norm bound controls the propagated error uniformly in time.

Why the distinction matters

The reconstruction theorem concerns this functional and its coding. It does not by itself supply detector calibration, statistical noise modeling, or observables for the full metric BV theory.

Mathematics

A map extracting a quantity from a state, with the required structure depending on the model. In the wave example it is a bounded linear functional; quantum observables instead belong to an operator or algebraic framework.

Mathematical structure

At each fixed time, Oₚ(t) is linear in the pair p. Its continuity is quantified by |Oₚ(t)−Oₚ′(t)|≤d(p,p′), where d is the stated L² product metric. This estimate lets finite input approximations control the output.

What must be represented

WR requests a fast sequence of rational polygonal functions representing the time-dependent observable. Existence of an abstract value and existence of an output in this representation are different statements over a weak base.

Scope

A bounded linear functional is the construction used here, not the definition of every observable in mathematics or physics. No full state reconstruction follows from this single functional.

Specialist

The observable notion is carrier-dependent. The WR benchmark fixes a bounded linear detector functional on chiral energy data; it makes no identification with the full gauge-invariant Weyl observable algebra.

Exact role in WR

The detector and translated chiral data are fixed by the reconstruction source. The uniform operator bound factors error control through the input metric; interpolation produces the prescribed fast polygonal output.

Lower-bound mechanism

On the fixed profile p(q)=(qg,0), evaluation at time zero recovers a nonzero rational multiple of q. Thus the reversal can encode scalar completion without using propagation or an unbounded family of detector modes.

Claim boundary

Do not infer gauge invariance, a BRST observable algebra, a measurement model, or the accepted full-complex transfer from this reduced functional. Each requires its own construction.

RCA₀

A base axiom system for reverse mathematics; the old #rca link remains valid.

Stands for: Recursive Comprehension Axiom

General

RCA stands for Recursive Comprehension Axiom. RCA₀ is the modest mathematical rulebook used as the starting point for this comparison. It lets the project distinguish what can already be proved from what needs an additional existence assumption.

What a rulebook does

Mathematics starts with assumptions and asks what follows from them. Some familiar arguments use an assumption that an infinite object exists. Starting with fewer permissions makes it possible to see when that extra assumption enters.

What happens in this project

If the wave data come with an error schedule, the project can prove the stated reconstruction within RCA₀. That is a claim about the proof and the information supplied, not about how intelligent a reader must be.

Reading the notation

The small zero is part of the conventional name. You do not need to manipulate the formal axioms to follow the physical example; watch which objects the input already supplies.

Physics

RCA₀ is a weak base theory for reasoning about coded numbers, sequences and countable mathematical objects. It is the bookkeeping framework for the wave comparison, not an extra law governing the wave.

Why a physicist encounters it

A derivation may pass from increasingly accurate finite data to a completed quantity. Reverse mathematics makes that step explicit. RCA₀ provides a restrained setting in which routine coded calculations can be separated from stronger completion assertions.

Application to the detector

With μ supplied, one selects a sufficiently accurate rational step pair and interpolates its response. The report proves the required estimates in the base. Removing μ changes the existence problem, even though the wave equation is unchanged.

Avoid a misleading interpretation

Calling this a weak base does not mean the physical model is inaccurate. Logical strength measures assumptions used in proofs, not experimental precision or runtime.

Mathematics

A subsystem of second-order arithmetic with elementary arithmetic, Σ⁰₁ induction and Δ⁰₁ comprehension, allowing set parameters. It serves as the base over which the stated reconstruction equivalence is proved.

What second order means here

The language has natural-number variables and variables for sets of natural numbers. Countable sequences and mathematical objects are encoded using these. The exact code for a real or a continuous function matters to the theorem being formalized.

Relative computability

The comprehension scheme permits sets computable relative to available set parameters. It is misleading to conclude that every set appearing in every model of RCA₀ must be computable without parameters.

Use in a reversal

To calibrate a theorem T over this base, prove the stronger system implies T and prove RCA₀ plus T recovers that system. A proof of only the first direction supplies an upper bound, not an equivalence.

Specialist

The base for WR↔ACA₀ and WRμ. The input/output coding and all reductions must be available over RCA₀; importing an already completed input would invalidate the stated comparison.

Base-theory audit

Keep the ordinary-Cauchy promise separate from a selected tail witness function, completed energy input, or convergence oracle. The reduction must establish the required promise before invoking WR.

What is certified

The report supplies an infinite mathematical argument and independent exact finite reduction checks. Those checks do not constitute a proof-assistant verification of RCA₀ formalizability.

Scope of strength

The claim is an equivalence over the declared base and representation. A different output object or input name may have a different calibration; stronger ambient mathematics can conceal this distinction.

Reverse mathematics

Logical analysis of a precisely represented theorem over a stated base system.

General

A way of asking which mathematical assumptions a theorem really needs. First show that certain assumptions prove the theorem; then reason backwards from the theorem to see whether it recovers those assumptions.

The backwards step

Ordinarily we begin with assumptions and prove a conclusion. The reverse step temporarily treats the conclusion as a rule we may use, then asks whether that rule lets us recover the original assumptions. This tests necessity as well as sufficiency.

The wave example

The project compares two requests for the same detector prediction. One includes an error schedule; the other merely promises eventual agreement. The difference helps locate which information the proof needs.

What is being compared

The comparison is between mathematical statements with specified inputs. It does not rank readers, and it does not decide which laws nature follows.

Physics

An analysis of the axiom strength of a mathematical assertion after its inputs, outputs and representations have been fixed. It can separate the cost of taking limits from the physical evolution being modeled.

Start with the physical question

Fix the quantity to predict and what preparation data are supplied. Next specify how an approximation or limiting value is represented. Only then is there a definite assertion whose logical strength can be compared.

The informative separation

In the wave benchmark the lower bound already occurs at one time and one profile. That locates the extra logical requirement in amplitude completion rather than in wave propagation.

Scope

A sufficient set of axioms is only an upper bound. Proving that a particular algorithm fails is not a reversal: necessity requires a reduction from the target principle.

Mathematics

The study of implications and reversals over a specified base theory. An equivalence B⊢(T↔S) calibrates T against the additional principles S, with B held fixed.

Formal reading

B is the background theory, T the represented theorem, and S the principle used for calibration. Both implications must be proved over B. Changing B or changing T’s coding can change the answer.

Example

The rate-free wave statement asks for a fast polygonal name from an ordinary-Cauchy promise. Adding a function witnessing that promise yields a different statement and lowers the strength required in the report.

Distinction

Reverse mathematics is related to computability theory, but axiom strength, effective uniform solvability and numerical complexity are separate questions.

Specialist

An equivalence or implication analysis whose base, representation and quantifier form are part of the theorem. Here the focal comparison is RCA₀⊢WRμ versus RCA₀⊢(WR↔ACA₀).

Audit checklist

State the base, code spaces, promises, output representation, and whether the assertion is pointwise existence or uniform selection. Check that the reduction creates valid inputs without using the very comprehension principle it is meant to recover.

Result here

The fixed-detector reduction realizes a standard scalar completion equivalence. Its scientific value lies in identifying the representation dependency of this benchmark, not in relabeling the background reversal as a new theorem.

Boundary

No general claim about foundational necessity for physical reality follows from one represented mathematical model.

State

Multiple related uses: classical configurations, quantum expectation rules and state cohomology are not interchangeable.

General

The mathematical information a model uses to make predictions about a system. What counts as a state depends on the model: a moving particle may need position and velocity, while a quantum model uses a different kind of description.

A familiar example

Knowing a ball’s position alone is not enough to predict where it will go next: its velocity also matters. A state supplies the information required by that particular model. It is a mathematical description, not necessarily everything one could know about the real object.

Quantum use

A quantum state determines probabilities or averages for the measurements the model describes. It generally does not assign a definite pre-existing value to every quantity at once.

Why this project is careful

Constructing a set of mathematical solutions does not by itself show that they give sensible probabilities. Entries in the matrix separate the underlying objects, existence of states, and positivity requirements.

Physics

A classical phase-space point or field initial datum, or a quantum density operator or normalized positive functional on an observable algebra. The carrier and positivity requirements must be stated; a cohomology class is not automatically a physical state.

Different realizations

Finite-dimensional quantum mechanics can use a positive density matrix of trace one. Algebraic formulations define a state through expectation values, avoiding an assumed Hilbert-space realization at the outset. Field theories add analytic and domain requirements.

The relevant obstruction

A negative expectation for A* A rules out a positive state on an algebra containing A. A finite-mode positivity construction does not establish positivity for the continuum or for a larger BRST complex.

Project boundary

The residual classes [W₊²] and [W₋²] are deformation or vertex classes in this programme, not identified one-particle graviton states.

Mathematics

A model-dependent object on which predictions depend. Examples include a phase-space point and a normalized positive linear functional ω on a unital *-algebra, satisfying ω(A* A)≥0 and ω(1)=1.

Algebraic definition

For a unital *-algebra, linearity, normalization and positivity specify the algebraic notion of state. Topological algebras may require continuity, and unbounded field realizations require attention to the common domain.

Cohomology is a different construction

A quotient ker Q / im Q identifies closed vectors modulo exact vectors when Q²=0. Calling such a space state cohomology does not equip it with a positive functional or prove a probabilistic interpretation.

Read the carrier first

When a matrix cell says state existence, inspect the underlying algebra or state space and the hypotheses. There is no single state set shared by every carrier.

Specialist

Keep classical data, algebraic positive states, BRST state cohomology, and residual deformation classes separate. The existence or computation of one does not establish the others or an accepted full Lorentzian state construction.

Type discipline

Record whether the object is initial data, an algebraic functional, a density operator, a BRST class, or a residual vertex. Transport between these kinds needs explicit maps and compatible analytic structures.

Reduced versus full

The reduced TT discussion concerns a specified commutator and positivity problem. Its conclusion neither supplies a full BV Hadamard state nor licenses identification of residual vertex classes with particle states.

Current boundary

The current full serialized complex fails the export audit. A glossary definition is not evidence that the missing full state construction exists.
