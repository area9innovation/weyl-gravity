ACA₀

A stronger axiom system used in the wave reconstruction equivalence.

Stands for: Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom

General

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. ACA₀ extends the starting rulebook RCA₀ with permission to collect numbers satisfying an arithmetic condition into a set, even when no general decision procedure is supplied.

Why an existence rule helps

An approximation sequence may eventually settle at every accuracy without telling you where it settles. The proof can use the stronger rulebook to collect enough information to specify a reliable limiting answer.

What “equivalent” means

For this precisely stated mathematical problem, ACA₀ is enough to obtain the answer, and the ability to solve every allowed input is enough to recover the extra rule. Both directions are needed.

What this does not mean

The result does not say that a laboratory needs an axiom system to function. Supplying an error schedule changes the mathematical input and avoids this extra requirement in the comparison.

Names that must stay distinct

The subscript zero is part of this system’s name. Bare ACA can denote a different system, so this dictionary no longer automatically treats the two spellings as interchangeable.

Physics

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. ACA₀ adds arithmetic comprehension to the base theory. In this wave problem it supplies missing tail-selection information needed to turn a rate-free Cauchy input into the required named limit.

Location of the extra assumption

The upper-bound proof forms the condition that all approximants beyond N are close at a requested tolerance, then obtains witnesses for those tails. Propagation and detector integration are handled separately by explicit estimates.

Why the converse is interesting

The detector can encode an arbitrary limiting scalar amplitude even at time zero. Recovering the requested output therefore cannot be justified solely by pointing to the simplicity of the wave dynamics.

Reading the result correctly

This is a logical calibration of a representation and completion task. It is not a new physical interaction, a numerical complexity estimate, or a general obstruction to finite measurements.

Names that must stay distinct

An ACA₀ requirement concerns existence for the specified rate-free input and fast output. It is neither a bound on laboratory resources nor an additional force acting on the wave.

Mathematics

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. The extension of RCA₀ by comprehension for arithmetical formulas: number quantifiers are allowed, with set parameters, but no bound set variables. The wave statement WR is equivalent to this extension over RCA₀.

Comprehension versus an algorithm

Existence of a set defined by an arithmetical condition need not supply a computable procedure deciding membership. Number quantifiers can express noncomputable conditions even when the underlying data have effective codes.

Standard characterization used

Over RCA₀, existence of ranges of injections characterizes ACA₀. The reduction encodes such a range in a limiting amplitude and decodes it from the fast output name.

Boundary of the comparison

This does not assert all possible comprehension principles. The theorem names ACA₀ specifically and relies on the exact coding of its input promise and output function.

Names that must stay distinct

The base and language determine the comparison: number quantifiers with set parameters are permitted in arithmetical comprehension. This is not comprehension over arbitrary predicates quantifying over sets.

Specialist

ACA stands for Arithmetical Comprehension Axiom. The exact strength of WR over RCA₀ for the declared rate-free representation. The upper bound selects Cauchy-tail witnesses; the lower bound recovers range existence through one scalar amplitude at t=0.

Upper and lower bounds

The tail predicate is arithmetical in the input code, so comprehension supplies a witness-selection construction. Conversely, a fixed profile and detector recover the amplitude code needed for range existence.

Contribution and standard background

The scalar completeness reversal is standard. The project realizes it within its fixed detector benchmark and retains a supplied-rate comparison and exact finite reduction witnesses.

No automatic promotion

The result is not a higher-type uniform operator theorem or a theorem about the full metric BV complex. Computational replay of finite witnesses does not certify the entire infinite reversal.

Names that must stay distinct

The statement stays an equivalence over RCA₀ for WR. Retain the original coding, fixed detector and infinite-proof boundary when citing the result.

Action and Lagrangian

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

An action assigns a number to a possible history of a system. A Lagrangian supplies the local ingredients used to build that number. Requiring the action not to change to first order under allowed variations produces equations of motion.

Example and distinction

This is a compact way to specify dynamics, but it does not solve the equations or tell us which states and measurements are physically acceptable. Boundary conditions also affect which variations are allowed.

Physics

An action functional is usually an integral of a Lagrangian density, together with any boundary terms. Its stationary variations produce Euler–Lagrange equations under stated boundary conditions.

Use in the model

Gauge symmetry and higher derivatives can introduce constraints and additional boundary data. A formally invariant action does not establish causal well-posedness, positivity or quantum consistency.

Mathematics

A Lagrangian density depends on fields and specified derivatives; integration gives a functional whose first variation defines the equations and boundary contributions. The function space and variation class matter.

Conditions and consequences

Formal integration by parts requires the appropriate boundary or support assumptions. Local densities, integrated functionals modulo boundary terms and residual classes are distinct mathematical objects in the programme.

Specialist

The pure-Weyl action is the starting point of a multi-stage construction, not its completion. Keep local variational identities, the encoded classical complex and quantum counterterm classification in their own result kinds.

Project role and claim boundary

No quantum coefficient or determinant should be substituted for a BV master-equation breaking. The classical import gate remains authoritative for any subsequent quantum use of transferred data.

Atlas cell and evidence grade

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

An atlas cell is one question formed by choosing mathematical assumptions, a kind of mathematical setting and a job for a physical theory. Its evidence grade describes the kind of support recorded for that question.

Example and distinction

The 576 cells are not 576 universes. A colored cell may concern a limited example or a scoped result, and many successful cells may use incompatible constructions. Read the evidence and limits behind the color.

Physics

The atlas crosses six foundational regimes, six carrier families and sixteen obligations. Cell evidence identifies what has been constructed, bounded, ruled out or left conditional at that coordinate.

Use in the model

Coverage is not composition. A theory profile can collect results from different realizations; a theory journey must show that the states, dynamics and observables belong to one compatible route.

Mathematics

A cell is an organizational record, not a universally quantified theorem about every object matching its row and column labels. The underlying statement, witness and assumptions determine its logical force.

Conditions and consequences

The table’s geometry does not supply implications between adjacent cells. Claims about transport require explicit maps and preservation conditions, independently of the grade assigned to either endpoint.

Specialist

Treat grades as evidence classifications with scoped provenance, not confidence scores or lifecycle promotions. Inspect dependency tags and does-not-establish fields before reusing a result.

Project role and claim boundary

No grid coverage count certifies the full classical import gate or a Lorentzian quantum theory. A failed prerequisite must block dependent claims even if nearby records remain valid.

Axiom and axiom system

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

An axiom is a rule accepted as a starting point for an argument. An axiom system is a collection of such rules. A theorem is a conclusion proved from them.

Example and distinction

Think of agreeing on the rules of a game before asking which moves are possible. In mathematics, a different rulebook can change which infinite objects can be proved to exist. This is separate from deciding which physical assumptions describe nature.

Physics

An axiom system specifies the mathematical rules available to a model’s proofs. It is distinct from a physical postulate such as locality or a symmetry assumption.

Use in the model

The same field equation can be studied with different existence principles. When comparing constructions, keep both the physical premises and the mathematical rulebook fixed unless the comparison explicitly changes one of them.

Mathematics

A theory consists of axioms in a specified formal language together with their deductive consequences. The language and underlying logic are part of the specification.

Conditions and consequences

In reverse mathematics the relevant theories concern numbers and sets of numbers. A statement’s provability is relative to a base theory; mathematical truth in an intended model and derivability in a formal system are different notions.

Specialist

Axioms identify the deductive environment of a scoped result. In the atlas, logical principles must remain distinct from carrier structure and encoded physical premises.

Project role and claim boundary

A claimed implication should expose which assumptions are discharged and which remain parameters. The atlas’s organizational notation is not evidence that every represented claim has been formalized in a proof assistant.

Base theory

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A base theory is the shared mathematical starting point used to compare two claims. It tells us which reasoning is already allowed before asking what an extra assumption adds.

Example and distinction

If both sides can already use ordinary finite calculations, those calculations are not the extra cost being measured. In the wave example, the question is what is additionally needed to reconstruct a limit when no error schedule is supplied.

Physics

The base theory fixes the mathematical resources allowed in a strength comparison. It plays a different role from a background spacetime or a reference physical model.

Use in the model

The supplied-rate wave reconstruction is proved in RCA₀. Comparing it with the rate-free assertion measures additional existence assumptions under that base, rather than extra dynamics, experimental precision or computing time.

Mathematics

A base theory is the formal theory over which an implication or equivalence is proved. Its coding conventions and induction and comprehension axioms affect the statement.

Conditions and consequences

To write T equivalent to ACA₀ over RCA₀ is to assert that RCA₀ proves the required directions. Changing the base can alter the strength comparison, so the base cannot be dropped from the theorem’s formulation.

Specialist

The base is a dependency of the reverse implication, not an informal label for an easy proof. Different finite fixtures and infinite assertions may legitimately use different bases.

Project role and claim boundary

The ladder’s fixed-fixture PRA claims and the wave reconstruction over RCA₀ must retain their quantifier and representation boundaries. A family of finite checks is not automatically a uniform theorem in the advertised base.

Born rule

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

The Born rule is the quantum rule connecting a state to probabilities of measurement outcomes. In a simple state-vector description, probabilities come from squared amplitudes.

Example and distinction

Squaring an amplitude is only meaningful after specifying the state, measurement and normalization. A model with equations but no acceptable probability assignment has not yet supplied the predictions this rule is meant to describe.

Physics

For a normalized state and a projective measurement, the Born rule assigns an outcome probability from the corresponding projection’s expectation value. More general measurements use positive effects summing to the identity.

Use in the model

Positivity and normalization are essential, and the observable representation must be specified. Real frequencies or positive classical energy alone do not supply those ingredients.

Mathematics

In a Hilbert representation, a normalized vector ψ assigns a projection E the value ⟨ψ,Eψ⟩. A density operator ρ gives Tr(ρE); algebraic states evaluate admissible measurement effects.

Conditions and consequences

The Hilbert inner product or positive functional is structural data, not notation that can be attached to an arbitrary indefinite pairing. The model must also identify which effects represent the intended measurements.

Specialist

The probability obligation is distinct from spectral reality, gauge-complex consistency and local quantum identity restoration. A residual class does not already define a Born-rule state.

Project role and claim boundary

When comparing carriers, verify that the operational identification and positive normalization are preserved. An algebraic correspondence alone cannot justify transporting empirical probabilities.

Carrier

Project vocabulary for the mathematical setting supporting a construction; not a material medium.

General

The kind of mathematical structure in which a model is built. A finite collection of matrices, a space of waves, and an algebra of quantum quantities are different carriers, with different questions that must be checked.

Why the choice matters

A finite table of numbers lets you perform exact finite calculations. A whole wave may require infinitely many approximations plus a rule explaining their limit. Both can model aspects of the same problem, but success with one does not automatically settle the other.

Reading a matrix cell

The carrier tells you what mathematical objects that cell is discussing. The other dimensions ask what assumptions are allowed and which job—such as defining states or proving evolution—has been completed.

Avoid a common confusion

This word does not mean the substance that carries a physical wave. It describes the mathematical setting used to investigate it.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

Two models can use the same underlying numbers while measuring closeness or probability differently. Ask whether the proposed connection preserves the detector reading, not just the names of the variables.

Physics

The specified mathematical realization carrying the model’s variables, operations and states: for example finite matrices, a completed function space or a local algebraic construction. Changing carrier may change which analytic obligations are meaningful.

Finite and continuum settings

A finite mode truncation supplies matrices and finite sums. Passing to a completed field space can add convergence, domain, positivity and causal-support obligations. A successful truncation is evidence about that truncation unless a transfer theorem is given.

What to inspect

Look for the space or algebra, the chosen topology, admissible states, domains of the dynamics, and maps connecting it to another realization. These determine whether two calculations address the same quantity.

Boundary

The atlas tracks different carriers explicitly so their established results cannot silently be combined into an unproved complete theory.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

A useful carrier change should specify the preserved dynamics, observables, pairing and causal support relevant to its intended use. Preservation of one property cannot stand for all of them.

Mathematics

The chosen structured ambient object, including any topology, pairing, operator domains and completion data needed for a result. The matrix uses carrier as an organizational dimension rather than as one universally defined category.

More than an underlying set

An identical vector space with two different topologies or pairings may support different continuity or positivity claims. A useful carrier specification therefore includes the structures used in the theorem.

Transfer question

To carry a result to another realization, specify maps and prove the required compatibility—such as continuity, intertwining of differentials, or preservation of the pairing. The necessary conditions depend on the claimed result.

Project usage

Carrier is an editorial organizing term here, not a claim that all entries form one category with interchangeable morphisms.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

An isomorphism of vector spaces need not be a homeomorphism, a chain map or an isometry. Each extra preservation condition changes the interface statement.

Specialist

The typed support of a construction in the atlas. Cross-carrier transfer requires a declared interface preserving the relevant operations and hypotheses; matching labels or finite fixtures do not establish equivalence.

Interface obligations

State the source and target kinds and the exact property transported. A chain map, an SDR, a cyclic pairing identification and a causal operator identification answer different questions; one cannot substitute for the others.

Why it matters to the programme

Reduced-mode certificates and local algebraic checks retain their dependency tags when compared with full Lorentzian targets. A positive finite test does not close the missing analytic transfer gates.

Boundary

Read each interface certificate and its excluded claims. A coverage mark is neither a functorial construction nor proof of physical equivalence.

What a carrier comparison must preserve

Finite-mode, Hilbert and local field-theoretic results require explicit joins. No full-import or causal-state claim follows solely from placing their certificates in neighboring atlas cells.

Cauchy sequence

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A Cauchy sequence is a list whose later entries become arbitrarily close to one another. This describes how the list settles, even before a limiting object has been constructed.

Example and distinction

Approximations might eventually agree to a millionth, but the promise alone need not tell you which entry starts that level of agreement. A modulus supplies that missing schedule. Whether a limit belongs to the chosen space is a separate completeness question.

Physics

A sequence is Cauchy when its tail differences become arbitrarily small in the declared metric or norm. The relevant notion of error depends on the carrier.

Use in the model

A field sequence may be Cauchy in one norm and fail in a stronger one. In the benchmark, distinguish the rate-free tail promise from a supplied bound on when each tolerance is met; the latter is additional input information.

Mathematics

In a metric space, a sequence is Cauchy if for every positive ε there is N such that d(x_m,x_n) is below ε whenever m,n are at least N.

Conditions and consequences

Completeness asserts that such sequences converge within the space. A modulus chooses suitable N as a function of requested accuracy. In weak systems, the existence of those witnesses and a supplied witness function are different statements.

Specialist

The rate-free input in WR carries a Cauchy promise without the schedule supplied in WRμ. The target still requires an appropriately fast name for the observable.

Project role and claim boundary

Keep the promise, the code and the output requirement explicit. A change from ordinary Cauchy data to a fast representation is not a harmless normalization when proving the reversal over RCA₀.

Causality and causal support

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Causality restricts which events can influence which others. Causal support says that a response occurs only in the region allowed by the chosen spacetime’s causal geometry.

Example and distinction

A retarded response to a source must not appear before the source can affect it. Solving an equation algebraically does not prove this restriction; the solution’s location in space and time must also be checked.

Physics

Causal support is a support inclusion determined by the Lorentzian geometry, such as a retarded response lying in the causal future of its source. The precise domain and global assumptions matter.

Use in the model

A Euclidean inverse, a finite-mode propagator or an algebraic identity does not establish this property for the full metric complex. The programme records Lorentzian causal construction as an independent obligation.

Mathematics

Causal constructions combine differential equations with geometric support conditions. A right or left inverse of an operator is not automatically its advanced or retarded Green operator.

Conditions and consequences

A theorem must specify the source and target spaces, operator identities and causal support inclusion. Chain-map identities and continuity may be additional requirements; none should be silently substituted for the others.

Specialist

LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL is a dependency tag, not automatic certification. The full off-shell metric propagator and renormalized time-ordered products remain unestablished absent their explicit certificates.

Project role and claim boundary

The scoped hyperbolicity witnesses rule out tested architectures rather than every possible causal construction. Local algebraic or reduced-mode progress cannot be promoted across this boundary by using causal terminology.

Certificate and provenance

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

A certificate records evidence that a specified mathematical check succeeded. Provenance records where its inputs came from and which versions were used.

Example and distinction

A checksum can show that a file has not changed; it cannot show that the file describes nature correctly. The useful question is what was checked, against which assumptions, and what kinds of mistakes the check could detect.

Physics

A computational certificate should expose the object, arithmetic, assumptions and verifier behind the result. Provenance identifies the imported source and generated inputs, usually with version and content hashes.

Use in the model

Exact finite algebra, numerical agreement and a written infinite proof provide different evidence. A certificate for one of them cannot silently stand in for the others or for empirical validation.

Mathematics

A certificate is a finite witness or structured record checked against a declared predicate. A content hash identifies bytes; it does not certify the predicate’s mathematical adequacy.

Conditions and consequences

The checker’s relationship to the producer matters. A successful check proves only its encoded conditions on its encoded objects, while completeness of the specification and any infinite theorem require additional justification.

Specialist

The programme records source commits, schemas, hashes, assumptions, missing objects and exact commands. Classical imports remain fail-closed until every required identity gate passes.

Project role and claim boundary

Replay establishes reproduction; an independent rail addresses different failure modes. Neither can promote a local algebraic result to a Lorentzian state theorem, and a partial certificate must retain its unresolved fields.

Claim scope and assumptions

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

A claim’s scope says exactly which objects and situations it covers. Its assumptions say what must already hold for its conclusion to follow.

Example and distinction

“This finite model works” and “every full model works” are very different claims. A precise explanation names the restrictions early so the reader does not accidentally turn a useful limited result into a statement about all of physics.

Physics

Scope includes background, sector, data class, approximation order and target property. Assumptions include both physical premises and mathematical requirements needed by the construction.

Use in the model

A positive reduced-mode example does not certify full-metric positivity. A failure of one hyperbolic witness family likewise does not refute every causal formulation. Preserve the quantifiers and architecture in either direction.

Mathematics

A statement’s force is fixed by its quantifiers, domains, hypotheses and conclusion. Coding and output requirements can materially change that statement even when the informal physical object stays the same.

Conditions and consequences

The WR/WRμ comparison depends on this precision: a rate-free promise and a supplied modulus are different hypotheses. An abstract value and a fast name for it are different outputs.

Specialist

Attach dependency tags and lifecycle states to the exact result kind. A does-not-establish field is a boundary, not an additional theorem or evidence for its negation.

Project role and claim boundary

Missing assumptions cannot be filled by adjacent certificates or plausible prose. A skipped or timed-out gate remains unresolved, and current corrections override unsupported interpretations of historical summaries.

Cohomology

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

Cohomology records objects that satisfy a consistency rule after identifying changes counted as trivial by that rule. It helps separate surviving structure from removable descriptions.

Example and distinction

A surviving class is a mathematical result, but its meaning depends on the problem. In this project, some certified classes describe deformations or interactions. Calling them particles would require additional physical arguments.

Physics

For a square-zero differential, cohomology is the closed sector modulo exact contributions. It can classify gauge-invariant information, deformations or anomalies, depending on the complex and degree.

Use in the model

Local functionals, residual objects and physical state spaces are different targets. A nontrivial residual class does not by itself carry positive norm, a causal propagator or a one-particle interpretation.

Mathematics

For a cochain complex, Hⁿ is ker(d:Cⁿ→Cⁿ⁺¹) modulo im(d:Cⁿ⁻¹→Cⁿ). The quotient is defined because d²=0 makes the image a subspace of the kernel.

Conditions and consequences

Nontriviality requires a closed representative and proof that it is not exact. The answer depends on the actual differential, degree, coefficient domain and any quotient by boundary terms.

Specialist

The certified residual W₊² and W₋² classes are centered deformation/vertex classes. Local BRST cohomology, integrated functionals modulo boundary terms and residual state cohomology must remain separate result kinds.

Project role and claim boundary

The current export’s failed square-zero test blocks cohomology claims that rely on that specific object. Expected basis elements or a selected representative cannot substitute for cocycle and nontriviality witnesses.

Completion

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

Completion enlarges a space so that its intended Cauchy approximation sequences have limits inside it. It makes precise a step often hidden in the phrase “take finer and finer approximations.”

Example and distinction

The rational numbers have sequences approaching numbers such as the square root of two, which is not rational. Completing the rationals adds their real limits. For fields, the chosen measure of closeness determines which limits are added.

Physics

Completion passes from a preliminary space, such as finite mode combinations, to a space containing limits in a specified norm or topology. It is an additional construction, not a consequence of finite-mode success.

Use in the model

A completed carrier may support operations or states that need separate continuity, domain and positivity proofs. The ladder records these additional obligations rather than treating exact finite energy as a full field-theory construction.

Mathematics

A metric completion can be constructed from equivalence classes of Cauchy sequences, with sequences equivalent when their mutual distance tends to zero. The original space embeds densely.

Conditions and consequences

For normed spaces the completion extends the norm; an inner-product completion yields a Hilbert space. In weak foundations, distinguish constructing a coded completion from asserting limits for all rate-free sequences in a particular representation.

Specialist

Completion is a typed transition with both analytic and logical dependencies. The wave comparison isolates amplitude completion; the ladder distinguishes finite algebra, Cauchy, distributional and energy carriers.

Project role and claim boundary

Do not transport finite identities into the completion without checking continuity or the relevant domains. The reduced positivity counterexample also demonstrates why separate finite repairs need not assemble into an admissible smooth full correction.

Comprehension

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

Comprehension is a mathematical rule allowing a collection of numbers to exist as a set when membership is described by an allowed condition. Different systems allow different conditions.

Example and distinction

For example, describing a collection is not always the same as giving a method that decides membership. The distinction matters when a proof collects information about infinitely many approximations to produce a limiting answer.

Physics

Comprehension specifies which sets of numerical information may be asserted to exist. In the wave comparison, it supplies information about convergence tails rather than changing the wave equation.

Use in the model

An error schedule can provide useful tail information as input. Without that schedule, an existence proof may require a stronger comprehension principle. This is a statement about mathematical reconstruction, not a new physical interaction.

Mathematics

A comprehension scheme asserts set existence for predicates in a specified syntactic class. Parameters and quantifier restrictions are essential to identifying the scheme.

Conditions and consequences

Convergence rate (modulus)

General

An error schedule: for each requested accuracy, it tells you how far along an approximation sequence you must go so that all later approximations agree to that accuracy.

A computer can produce better approximations without knowing when they are good enough. The wave comparison separates these two kinds of information: an eventual-agreement promise and a supplied error schedule.

Vector space

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A vector space is a collection of objects that can be added and multiplied by numbers according to consistent rules. The objects can be arrows, lists of numbers or whole wave patterns.

Example and distinction

If two wave patterns belong to a linear space, their weighted sum also belongs to that space. This says how patterns combine; it does not specify distance, energy or probability, which require more structure.

Physics

A vector space supplies linear combinations of configurations or perturbations over a stated scalar field. Linearized solution spaces often use this structure.

Use in the model

The space itself does not fix a norm, pairing, topology or physical interpretation. In a nonlinear theory, adding two solutions need not give another solution, even though the ambient space of field variations is linear.

Mathematics

A vector space over a field has addition and scalar multiplication satisfying the vector-space axioms. Finite-dimensional spaces admit finite bases; infinite-dimensional settings require care about the intended notion of basis.

Conditions and consequences

An algebraic basis, a topological basis and an orthonormal expansion carry different requirements. Completing a finite-span space introduces analytic structure beyond its linear operations.

Specialist

Project role and claim boundary

Weyl gravity

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Weyl gravity is a proposed theory of gravity built from a part of spacetime curvature called the Weyl tensor. Its four-dimensional pure form has a local rescaling symmetry and equations involving higher derivatives.

Example and distinction

An elegant action does not settle whether the theory supplies sensible states, causal predictions and agreement with data. Those are separate questions in this project, and the current work does not claim a complete accepted theory of nature.

Physics

Four-dimensional pure Weyl gravity uses a Weyl-curvature-squared action, with conformal gauge symmetry and fourth-order metric equations. It is a specific realization within the wider discussion of conformal gravity.

Use in the model

Extra classical directions, gauge reduction, interactions and quantum probabilities require separate tests. The present full-export failure also means older affirmative statements about a complete BRST–Hadamard transfer must be read with the current correction.

The model is a metric variational theory based on a curvature-squared functional. Its gauge and differential structures require specifying the dimension, allowed metrics, boundary conditions and treatment of conformal transformations.
