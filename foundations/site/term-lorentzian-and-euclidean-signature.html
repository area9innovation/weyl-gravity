Lorentzian and Euclidean signature

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Lorentzian geometry distinguishes time directions from space directions and supports light cones. Euclidean geometry uses a positive metric with no corresponding distinction. Calculations in the two settings answer different questions.

Example and distinction

A Euclidean calculation may be a useful way to study a theory. It does not automatically describe how a physical disturbance travels through time. A justified connection to the Lorentzian problem is an additional step.

Physics

Lorentzian signature is indefinite with one time direction in the usual spacetime setting; Euclidean signature is positive definite. Their evolution, spectral and state constructions use different analytic structures.

Use in the model

Analytic continuation or reconstruction requires hypotheses and compatible data. A Euclidean determinant or stability estimate does not by itself construct a BRST-compatible Hadamard state or Lorentzian time-ordered products.

Mathematics

Signature records the numbers of positive and negative directions of a nondegenerate symmetric form. Lorentzian and positive-definite metrics induce different operator and geometric problems.

Conditions and consequences

Elliptic spectral control and hyperbolic causal control are distinct. A formal substitution of a time coordinate is not a proof that distributions, domains or positivity conditions transfer between the two settings.

Specialist

EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL and LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL mark different dependency classes. Neither label is a confidence score, and the former cannot certify the latter’s obligations.

Project role and claim boundary

The Euclidean stability counterfamily is scoped to its proposed estimate. Its failure does not refute every reconstruction programme, just as a successful spectral calculation would not establish the full Lorentzian quantum master equation.
