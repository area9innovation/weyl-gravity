Full-export consistency failure

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

The full-export consistency failure is a problem found in the particular serialized gravity construction used by the project: applying its proposed differential twice does not give zero where required.

Example and distinction

A candidate repair fixes exposed parts, but the complete repaired object has not passed all the necessary checks. This corrects older affirmative summaries without saying that every related classical calculation or physical theory is false.

Physics

Direct checks of the current serialized full complex expose nonzero differential-square terms and a projection compatibility defect. These are prerequisites of the advertised full BRST–Hadamard transfer.

Use in the model

The trace/ghost candidate repairs diagnosed blocks and selected contraction identities. Full nilpotency, compatible chain maps and cyclicity remain unverified for that candidate, so the full transfer is not accepted.

Mathematics

A complex needs d²=0 before its image lies in its kernel and the advertised cohomology can be used. A nonzero encoded coefficient violates that prerequisite for the supplied object.

Conditions and consequences

A right-inverse or contraction identity does not independently imply every missing chain-map and pairing identity. Repairs to subblocks must be checked as part of the complete new object before acceptance.

Specialist

Keep the authoritative classical programme, the concrete serialized export and the partial repair distinct. The current export fails its direct acceptance gate; no replacement operator has been accepted merely by the candidate experiment.

Project role and claim boundary

This LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC failure blocks dependent full-transfer claims. It does not erase separately certified reduced results, refute every BRST construction or determine the complete physical particle spectrum.
