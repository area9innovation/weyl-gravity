Showing each contraction separately nonvanishing does not show they are independent: a combination of nonvanishing objects can vanish, and that combination would be a symmetry. Conversely, a rank read at a base point is only a lower bound on the generic rank, so a rank deficit there is not a relation. What settles it is exhibiting the candidate combination --- by carrying an identity through the elimination, so a row whose left half reduces to zero returns its own coefficients --- and then testing that combination on the full jets, where a genuine relation must be the zero polynomial at every order.
