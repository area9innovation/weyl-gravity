Lorentzian-causal

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL marks a claim whose dependencies concern causal construction in Lorentzian spacetime. It tells the reader which kind of physics and mathematics must support the claim.

Example and distinction

The label is not a declaration that the full construction is finished. A result can concern one causal component while leaving the state, interactions or other compatibility conditions unresolved.

Physics

The tag identifies Lorentzian geometry together with the relevant causal operator or product structure. The specific source spaces, support properties and compatibility conditions remain essential.

Use in the model

A Euclidean inverse, local identity or reduced propagator cannot supply those full requirements by analogy. Certification must name the actual causal object and the checks it passed.

Mathematics

A LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL theorem combines operator or distributional identities with geometrically defined support and any further framework axioms. Its exact hypotheses determine its force.

Conditions and consequences

The tag has no independent deductive content. It must not be confused with the lifecycle state LORENTZIAN_CERTIFIED, which records acceptance of a declared construction after the requisite checks.

Specialist

The full off-shell BV propagator, BRST-compatible Hadamard state, renormalized time-ordered products, causal perturbative AQFT construction and Lorentzian QME remain unestablished absent explicit certificates.

Project role and claim boundary

Do not use LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC, REDUCED-MODE or EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL evidence to fill these gates silently. A certified narrower causal component would still need its scope recorded rather than being treated as the complete theory.
