Regularization and renormalization

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

Regularization introduces a controlled way to handle expressions that are otherwise singular or undefined. Renormalization specifies how to adjust or extend the calculation so that the intended quantities satisfy stated conditions.

Example and distinction

A temporary cutoff can be useful, but removing it is not simply erasing the cutoff symbol. One must show what remains well defined and which choices affect the final physical interpretation.

Physics

Regularization and renormalization are related but distinct steps. Renormalization can be formulated as choosing extensions of singular distributions or compatible local counterterms, without treating a regulator as a physical component.

Use in the model

The allowed freedom is constrained by locality, symmetry and normalization conditions. A computed divergence or counterterm coefficient does not alone establish a restored quantum master equation or causal interacting theory.

Mathematics

Renormalization may construct extensions of distributions from nonsingular configurations to coincident points, with controlled ambiguity. A formal subtraction algorithm and a theorem satisfying all required identities are different claims.

Conditions and consequences

Domains, scaling conditions and consistency under composition must be specified. In perturbation theory, the result can remain a formal series rather than a convergent nonperturbative object.

Specialist

Classify counterterms and anomalies before coefficients, then test local QME restoration before residual transfer. Local algebraic renormalization data remain distinct from renormalized Lorentzian time-ordered products.

Project role and claim boundary

Neither a determinant nor a beta function certifies the latter. Keep LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC, EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL and LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL dependencies visible rather than letting the word renormalized promote the claim.
