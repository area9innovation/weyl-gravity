Positive energy

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Positive energy means that the specified energy quantity is nonnegative, or strictly positive where the statement says so. It is a property of that energy formula and the objects to which it applies.

Example and distinction

This is different from saying that every possible measurement has a sensible probability. The project can verify positive energy for a finite wave fixture while still leaving the full quantum state problem open.

Physics

Positive energy is an energy-functional or spectral condition, depending on the claim. The allowed configurations and the treatment of zero modes must be stated.

Use in the model

A lower energy bound does not establish positivity of the state functional or absence of problematic gauge-sector directions. Conversely, a positive quantum state need not have a Hamiltonian bounded below. These tests answer different physical questions.

Mathematics

A positive quadratic energy may mean E(x)>0 for every nonzero admissible x; a nonnegative energy allows a kernel. A lower-bounded operator spectrum is a related but different formulation.

Conditions and consequences

Specify which definition is used, together with the domain and null directions. Finite-dimensional positivity of a matrix does not by itself prove a corresponding property on an infinite completed carrier.

Specialist

The finite chiral certificate’s positive energy does not discharge the quantum probability obligation. Retain its finite fixture and normalization rather than promoting it to a physical-state theorem.

Project role and claim boundary

When diagnosing higher-derivative modes, separate energy sign, inner-product sign, gauge triviality and nonlinear continuation. A finding in one of those tests does not decide the others.
