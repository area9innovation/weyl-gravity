Theory journey and interface

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

A theory journey follows one proposal from assumptions through states, change, measurement and comparison with data. An interface is a justified connection between two steps.

Example and distinction

Two well-built components may still fail to fit together. A state construction and a detector formula are useful only if the detector is defined on those states and retains the meaning claimed for its output.

Physics

A journey tracks a compatible sequence of realizations and obligations. Interfaces specify preserved data such as observables, pairing, causal support, normalization or empirical interpretation.

Use in the model

A profile showing separate successes need not supply those interfaces. In particular, a reduced positive block and a full gauge complex are not joined merely by sharing notation or belonging to the same theory family.

Mathematics

An interface is a specified map or comparison with required preservation properties. A chain map preserves differentials, but may fail to preserve pairings, topologies, support or positivity.

Conditions and consequences

Each composition theorem needs compatible domains and codomains. Abstract equivalence of underlying spaces is insufficient when the requested output includes a representation or quantitative control.

Specialist

Interface obligations make hidden changes of carrier or claim type visible. Record the exact source object and the properties carried across each map.

Project role and claim boundary

The full-export consistency failure blocks interfaces depending on that export. Valid local or reduced results survive at their own scope, but cannot be assembled into a full transfer by assuming the missing joins.
