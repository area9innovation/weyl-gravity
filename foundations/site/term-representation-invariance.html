Representation invariance

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

Representation invariance asks whether a claim remains valid when the same intended object is described in another form. The change must preserve what the object means and what the task asks us to recover.

Example and distinction

Two descriptions can hide different amounts of information. If one includes an error schedule and the other does not, calling them “the same wave” does not make their reconstruction tasks identical.

Physics

A representation comparison needs explicit translations preserving the intended observable and relevant physical structure. Equality of informal interpretation is not enough to preserve computational or logical resources.

Use in the model

The WR/WRμ example deliberately changes convergence information. Any proposed invariant reformulation must state whether the translation supplies that information or changes the output guarantee.

Mathematics

Representations assign objects to names. An invariance theorem must specify translations between names and the resources under which those translations and their preservation properties exist.

Conditions and consequences

An abstract carrier isomorphism need not be computable, continuous or available in the weak base. These are separate questions, and the chosen output representation can also change the assertion.

Specialist

Record the exact observable identification, input promises and translation assumptions in a representation comparison. Do not infer invariance from a shared physical label or matching finite examples.

Project role and claim boundary

The wave reversal locates a genuine distinction between its declared codings; it does not prove that every possible representation has the same logical strength or that no alternative carrier can avoid the requirement.
