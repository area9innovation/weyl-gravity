Energy

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Energy is a quantity a physical model uses to describe such things as motion, deformation and interactions. Its formula depends on the model. “Finite energy” says that this quantity does not diverge for the object being considered.

Example and distinction

A wave can have finite energy without being confined to a small region. Also, a finite number is not necessarily positive. The site keeps finite energy, positive energy and acceptable quantum probabilities as separate questions.

Physics

Energy is a model-dependent quantity associated, where applicable, with time evolution and time-translation symmetry. An energy functional may also define or control a useful norm.

Use in the model

A finite-mode computation can establish an exact positive finite energy for its fixture. That is not a theorem about the completed carrier, causal support or the positivity of a quantum state on a full observable algebra.

Mathematics

An energy is a specified functional on the admissible data or solution space. Finiteness, nonnegativity, conservation and coercivity are distinct properties that need their own hypotheses.

Conditions and consequences

A positive quadratic energy may induce a norm after handling null directions; an indefinite form does not. Extending the functional to a completion requires suitable control and cannot be inferred from its finite-dimensional formula alone.

Specialist

Read the ladder’s exact positive finite energy as a fixture-level certificate. Its boundary excludes completion and causal or spatial support unless separately established.

Project role and claim boundary

Do not substitute an energy bound for a positive state, a BRST-compatible pairing or a unitarity theorem. The reduced cutoff obstruction addresses a different acceptance condition even when both calculations use the same mode language.
