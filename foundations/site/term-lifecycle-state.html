Lifecycle state

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

A lifecycle state records which step of a declared research construction has actually been completed. It differs from both a reader’s perspective and the physical meaning of a quantum state.

Example and distinction

Knowing which corrections are allowed, calculating their coefficients and proving a corrected identity are different achievements. The status should name the completed step without suggesting the remaining ones have also passed.

Physics

CLASSIFIED identifies the allowed result space; COEFFICIENT_COMPUTED records evaluated coefficients; QME_RESTORED records the specified quantum master identity; RESIDUAL_TRANSFERRED records the validated transfer. LORENTZIAN_CERTIFIED requires its separate causal acceptance conditions.

Use in the model

These are not automatic promotions along a pipeline. A particular local identity and the full Lorentzian model have different dependencies and claim scopes.

Mathematics

Lifecycle labels summarize evidence for declared obligations. They are metadata, not proofs that prerequisite predicates hold or implications that stronger constructions exist.

Conditions and consequences

A transition needs the actual certificate and its hypotheses. Different result kinds may have different paths through these labels, so the names must not be interpreted as one universal theorem ordering.

Specialist

Use the exact programme labels and preserve independent dependency tags. Do not infer QME_RESTORED from a coefficient, RESIDUAL_TRANSFERRED from restoration alone, or LORENTZIAN_CERTIFIED from either.

Project role and claim boundary

A missing or failed gate leaves the dependent status unpromoted. The current full-export failure cannot be repaired administratively by assigning a later lifecycle label to a related local calculation.
