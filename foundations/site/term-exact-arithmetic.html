Exact arithmetic

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

General

Exact arithmetic keeps numbers such as fractions without rounding them to a limited number of decimal digits. This allows a finite identity to be checked as exactly zero rather than merely very small.

Example and distinction

For example, one third plus two thirds is exactly one in rational arithmetic. A tiny floating-point residual is a different kind of evidence and can hide cancellation or precision problems.

Physics

Exact rational or algebraic arithmetic verifies finite coefficient identities without a numerical tolerance. Rational-complex coefficients have rational real and imaginary parts.

Use in the model

It is appropriate for the programme’s finite mode residuals and algebraic complex checks. It does not itself control infinite sums, convergence, measurement error or empirical accuracy.

Mathematics

Exact arithmetic uses a specified effective representation of numbers and operations, with exact equality where supported. Rational arithmetic is a basic case; algebraic extensions require their own canonical representations.

Conditions and consequences

A symbolic expression is not automatically reduced to a form with decidable equality. Record the coefficient domain, normalization and equality procedure behind rank or cocycle computations.

Specialist

Canonical ranks, cocycle tests and trivialization witnesses must use exact arithmetic in this programme. Numerical agreement cannot certify those identities.

Project role and claim boundary

Conversely, an exact finite result cannot certify an infinite analytic theorem without its additional argument. Receipts must distinguish the arithmetic type and retain hashes of imported coefficients rather than silently regenerating a preferred answer.
