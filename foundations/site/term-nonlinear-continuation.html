Nonlinear continuation

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

Nonlinear continuation asks whether a small solution direction found in an approximation can be extended to a solution of the full equations. A first-order possibility can fail at the next order.

Example and distinction

A tangent direction can point along an apparent path that later runs into a constraint. The project therefore checks this step separately from finding a linear mode or removing redundant descriptions.

Physics

A linearized solution can source higher-order equations whose solvability imposes additional conditions. Nonlinear continuation tests those conditions in the declared background and perturbative class.

Use in the model

A nontrivial linear gauge class is not enough. Even successful formal continuation through several orders does not establish convergence of the expansion or a physical quantum state.

Mathematics

The continuation problem seeks a family solving the nonlinear equation with a specified initial tangent. At successive orders, obstruction classes or compatibility conditions may prevent solving the correction equations.

Conditions and consequences

Formal power-series solvability and existence of an actual analytic or smooth family are distinct. Regularity, gauge choices and boundary conditions are part of the relevant function-space theorem.

Specialist

Keep nonlinear admissibility distinct from the centered residual deformation classification and one-particle interpretation. A surviving class must be matched to the precise deformation problem before it supports a continuation claim.

Project role and claim boundary

A finite perturbative certificate covers its declared order and ansatz. It cannot silently establish an all-order or convergent interacting construction, much less full Lorentzian quantum consistency.
