Field, ghost and antifield complex

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

The field, ghost and antifield complex is the full mathematical collection used to organize the gauge construction. Physical field variables, redundancy bookkeeping and dual variational data play different roles within it.

Example and distinction

A count of its coordinates is not a count of physical particles. All required maps must fit together before the collection can support the claimed cohomological reduction.

Physics

The extended metric BV complex combines fields, gauge-related variables and antifields with declared degrees and differential maps. Batalin–Vilkovisky is the expansion of BV.

Use in the model

Truncating to a convenient field or tensor block changes the scope. Checks on that block do not establish the full set of gauge, pairing and propagation identities required by the entire construction.

Mathematics

The object is a graded carrier with maps encoding equations, symmetries and identities under specified conventions. Degree shifts, dualizations and pairings belong to the data.

Conditions and consequences

The required square-zero and chain-map relations involve all relevant components. An apparently correct subcomplex or a projection with a right inverse need not make the complete serialized object consistent.

Specialist

The 386-row full export is an encoded graded construction, not 386 one-particle modes. Direct audits expose differential and projection defects in that object.

Project role and claim boundary

The trace/ghost candidate repairs diagnosed blocks but has not supplied the full required nilpotency, chain-map and cyclicity checks. No full metric quantum propagator or state may be inferred from that partial repair.
