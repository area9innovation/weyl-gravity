Free and interacting theory

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

A free theory describes systems without the interactions under study; an interacting theory includes couplings that let components affect one another. Solving the free problem is often a starting point.

Example and distinction

Separate musical tones may be easy to describe, while a nonlinear coupling mixes them. A consistency test passed by isolated components need not remain true after coupling, so the project tests those stages separately.

Physics

A free theory is typically governed by a quadratic action, while interactions introduce higher-order couplings. The split depends on the chosen background and perturbative setup.

Use in the model

A free propagator or positive sector does not supply renormalized time-ordered products, an interacting state or a restored quantum identity. Each requires compatible additional construction.

Mathematics

The free/interacting distinction separates a linear or quadratic reference problem from nonlinear terms. Formal power-series solutions and convergent solutions are different mathematical claims.

Conditions and consequences

A perturbative coefficient can satisfy a local identity without establishing a global solution or an actual measure. The relevant algebra, topology, domains and order of approximation must remain explicit.

Specialist

Classify local counterterms and anomalies before computing coefficients, restore the local quantum master equation before residual transfer, and keep the Lorentzian gate independent.

Project role and claim boundary

A REDUCED-MODE or EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL calculation does not certify interacting causal products. Lifecycle states must record which step is complete rather than letting the word quantum imply the entire chain.
