\begin{abstract}
We show that an axial \(\ell=2\) Schwarzschild quasinormal mode becomes a
defective resonance in four-dimensional pure Weyl gravity.  The reduced
six-state Bach system has two spin-two Regge--Wheeler layers and one
spin-one Maxwell layer.  The repeated spin-two block is not a rational
direct sum: it is a non-split self-extension.  At a validated simple
Regge--Wheeler quasinormal frequency, its connection has Smith type
\((0,0,2)\), hence one length-two root chain.  The geometric root is
Einstein, while the generalized root has nonzero gauge-invariant
traceless-Ricci carrier.

The algebraic mechanism is an exact projective cocycle.  After Liouville
reduction,
\[
 [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
 =\frac{i\omega}{2}\left[1-\frac2r\right].
\]
The class in brackets is also the direction selected by the complete
coupled axial massive-spin-two system.  Transforming that system into its
massless Maxwell/Regge--Wheeler basis gives
\[
 [\mathcal I_{\rm phys}]=\frac13\left[1-\frac2r\right],
 \qquad
 [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
 =\frac{3i\omega}{2}[\mathcal I_{\rm phys}].
\]
Thus the isolated shifted-Regge--Wheeler target has the correct direction
but not the physical normalization.  The rational primitives reproduce
the differentiated moving massive phase, while the Coulomb exponent has
zero first mass derivative.  Matrix Frobenius and sectorial Volterra
constructions promote this comparison to the complete endpoint-normalized
Jost planes.  The certified selector therefore equals the velocity of the
complete massive axial QNM branch, up to the displayed normalization, and
that velocity is nonzero.

The complete radial pencil also admits a fixed-domain, two-ended
\(\pi/4\) exterior-complex-scaled realization that is Fredholm of index
zero.  Its inverse has a nonzero rank-one second-order pole, and its
compactly supported matrix elements agree with the exterior outgoing
cut-off inverse.  This outgoing inverse is the meromorphic continuation
of the Laplace transform of the mode-reduced retarded parent response, so
the pole belongs to an actual causal transfer operator.  Exact axial
reconstruction shows that the Einstein
range of the principal coefficient has nonzero Bondi shear.  A
complexified conserved, traceless, radially compact odd source has
nonzero adjoint overlap.  Thus the isolated local resonance contour
contains the expected \(t e^{i\omega_n t}\) Einstein profile.  We do not
claim a global retarded quasinormal expansion, a real causal source
construction, or standard asymptotic falloff for the time-independent
generalized component.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Fourth-order linear equations are often described by listing their scalar
factors.  That description is incomplete when a factor is repeated.  The
layers may form a direct sum, or they may form a non-semisimple extension.
Only the second possibility carries an intrinsic generalized state and can
turn a simple resonance of the repeated factor into a defective resonance
of the full system.
We use the standard Regge--Wheeler and Schwarzschild QNM conventions of
Refs.~\cite{reggewheeler1957,leaver1985}, translated to our
\(e^{+i\omega t}\) Fourier sign.

This distinction is realized by axial \(\ell=2\) Schwarzschild
perturbations of pure Weyl gravity.  In the factor-adapted radial variables,
the Bach equation
contains two spin-two Regge--Wheeler layers and one spin-one Maxwell layer,
but the two spin-two layers cannot be separated by a rational local field
redefinition.  The extension is also the critical first jet of the
massless and massive spin-two directions after exact reduction of the
complete coupled axial system.  At a
Schwarzschild quasinormal frequency, this confluence produces a
length-two root chain rather than two independent Einstein roots.

We use \(M=1\), the Fourier convention \(e^{+i\omega t}\), and the
negative-real-frequency representative of the reflected Schwarzschild
pair.  Thus damping means \(\operatorname{Im}\omega>0\), and the familiar
fundamental axial frequency is written near
\(-0.37367+0.08896i\).  The parameter \(m\) below is a signed
\emph{squared-mass coefficient}; it is not the positive square root usually
called the graviton mass.

The paper has five results.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The axial spin-two Bach block is a non-split rational
  Regge--Wheeler self-extension and an exact first parameter jet.
\item Its projective cocycle has the elementary normal form
  \[
    [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
    =\frac{i\omega}{2}\left[1-\frac2r\right],
  \]
  and the complete coupled massive axial tensor branch represents one
  third of the same class.
\item A validated QNM calculation selects the defective Smith type
  \((0,0,2)\).  The generalized root necessarily crosses the Einstein
  filtration.  The same certificate gives a nonzero velocity of the
  complete massive axial QNM branch.
\item A fixed-domain global exterior-complex-scaled radial pencil is
  Fredholm of index zero and has a genuine rank-one pole of order two.
  Its compactly supported matrix elements equal the exterior outgoing
  cut-off Green operator and meromorphically continue a mode-reduced
  retarded causal transfer.
\item Exact metric reconstruction and an explicit conserved, traceless odd
  source show that the range and source covectors of this pole can both be
  nonzero.
\end{enumerate}

These statements combine exact algebra with validated numerics.  The
module reduction, cocycle identities, mass comparison, Green reduction,
metric reconstruction, and source construction are exact.  The existence
and uniqueness of the enclosed QNM, nonvanishing of the projective
selector, and spin-one local-unit condition are interval-validated.

\section{The filtered axial Bach module}

\subsection{Differential-module conventions}

We work over
\[
 K=\C(r,\omega),\qquad
 D=f\partial_r,\qquad
 f=1-\frac2r,
\]
with \(\omega\ne0\) unless stated otherwise.  The spin-two and spin-one

\[
 L_2=D^2+U_2,\qquad
 U_2=\omega^2-f\left(\frac6{r^2}-\frac6{r^3}\right),
\]

 L_1=D^2+U_1,\qquad

\]
We use \(D(q)\) and \(D(U)\), rather than primes, for the radial

The Einstein submodule is the kernel of the gauge-invariant
traceless-Ricci, equivalently Schouten, carrier.  Thus ``non-Einstein''
means nonzero projection to this carrier quotient, not failure of a
coordinate gauge choice.

\subsection{Filtration and partial jet}

In the certified factor gauge, the six-state system is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:six-state}
\begin{aligned}
 DX&=AX+EY+CZ,\\
 DY&=AY+DZ,\\
 DZ&=A_1Z,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where \(X,Y,Z\in K^2\), while \(A\) and \(A_1\) are the companion
matrices of \(L_2\) and \(L_1\).  The rational reconstruction into the
axial metric and curvature variables has full row rank on the declared
domain.

\begin{theorem}[Filtered axial Bach module]\label{thm:filtration}
The reduced axial \(\ell=2\) Bach module has a filtration with successive
quotients
\[
 M_2,\qquad M_2,\qquad M_1.
\]
The middle four-state object is an extension
\[
 0\longrightarrow M_2\longrightarrow E_2
 \longrightarrow M_2\longrightarrow0,
\]
and the final quotient is the spin-one Maxwell module \(M_1\).
\end{theorem}

This theorem is an exact rational statement.  It does not rely on an
endpoint normalization or on the QNM calculation.

Introduce the four-state family
\[
 \mathcal B(\tau)=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 A+\tau E&D+\tau C\\
 0&A_1
 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
 \Phi_4(\tau)=
 \begin{pmatrix}P(\tau)&Q(\tau)\\0&R\end{pmatrix}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact partial jet]\label{thm:partial-jet}
The system \eqref{eq:six-state} is the first spin-two-row jet of
\(\mathcal B(\tau)\) at \(\tau=0\).  Its fundamental matrix is
\[
 \Phi_6=
 \begin{pmatrix}

 0&P&Q\\
 0&0&R
 \end{pmatrix}_{\tau=0},
\qquad
 \det\Phi_6=(\det P)^2\det R.

Although each scalar factor is second order, its chosen horizon or infinity

\subsection{Projective extension class}

Consider a scalar self-extension
\[
 Lx=(s_1D+s_0)y,\qquad Ly=0,\qquad L=D^2+U.
\]
Define
\[
 \mathcal I=s_0-\frac12D(s_1),\qquad
 \mathcal K_U=D^3+4UD+2D(U).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Triangular gauge]\label{lem:triangular-gauge}
For \(q\in K\), set
\[
 Q(q)=qD-\frac12D(q).
\]
On \(\ker L\),
\[
 [L,Q(q)]=-\frac12\mathcal K_U(q).
\]
Thus the lift change \(x\mapsto x+Q(q)y\) changes the scalar density by
\[
 \mathcal I\longmapsto
 \mathcal I-\frac12\mathcal K_U(q).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Expand the commutator by the Leibniz rule and use \(D^2y=-Uy\).
The coefficient of \(Dy\) cancels; the remaining coefficient is
\(-[D^3q+4UDq+2D(U)q]/2\).
\end{proof}

The projective extension class is therefore
\[
 [\mathcal I]\in K/\mathcal K_UK.
\]
The operator \(\mathcal K_U\) is the symmetric-square operator of \(L\).

\begin{theorem}[Mass-direction normal form]\label{thm:mass-normal}
For the axial \(\ell=2\) Bach extension,
\[
 \mathcal I_{\rm Bach}
 =
 \frac{i(r-2)(2r\omega^2+3\omega^2+12)}
 {5r^4\omega}.
\]
Let
\[
 q(r,\omega)=
 -\frac{i}{120\omega}
 \left(15r+13+\frac{12}{r}+\frac9{r^2}\right).
\]
Then, for \(\omega\ne0\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mass-normal-form}
 \mathcal I_{\rm Bach}
 =\mathcal K_{U_2}q+\frac{i\omega}{2}f,
\end{equation}
and hence
\[
 \boxed{\;
 [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
 =\frac{i\omega}{2}[f].
 \;}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Substitute \(D=f\partial_r\), \(U_2\), and the displayed \(q\) into
\(\mathcal K_{U_2}q\).  After clearing \(120r^4\omega\), the assertion is
a polynomial identity in \(r\) and \(\omega\).  Exact coefficient
comparison gives \eqref{eq:mass-normal-form}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Non-split physical-axis extension]\label{thm:nonsplit}
For every real \(\omega>0\), the extension \(E_2\) is non-split over

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Certificate-supported exact proof]

\subsection{The coupled physical system}

The covariant parent family contains the second-order tensor pencil
\[
 (\mathcal E+m\mathcal A)\phi=0,
\]
where \(m=\mu^2\) is the signed squared-mass coefficient.  For axial
\(\ell=2\) Schwarzschild perturbations, the complete massive tensor
equations of Refs.~\cite{britocardosopani2013,antoniou2025} are
\begin{align}
 D^2Q+
 \left[\omega^2-f\left(m+\frac{10}{r^2}-\frac{16}{r^3}\right)\right]Q
 &=\frac{8f(r-3)}{r^3}Z,
 \label{eq:massive-Q}\\
 D^2Z+
 \left[\omega^2-f\left(m+\frac4{r^2}+\frac2{r^3}\right)\right]Z
 &=\frac{2f}{r^2}Q.
 \label{eq:massive-Z}
\end{align}
This is a coupled tensor/vector system, not a single shifted
Regge--Wheeler equation.

At \(m=0\), introduce the tensor and vector master fields
\((\phi_2,\phi_1)\), satisfying \(L_2\phi_2=0\) and \(L_1\phi_1=0\).
The massless Berndtson transformation specializes to
\begin{align}
 Q={}&\frac1{\omega^2}\left(
 \frac{4f}{3r}\phi_2+\frac43D\phi_2
 -\frac{2f}{r}\phi_1+D\phi_1\right),\label{eq:berndtson-Q}\\
 Z={}&-\frac1{\omega^2}\left(
 \frac{7r-6}{3r^2}\phi_2+D\phi_2+\frac1r\phi_1\right).
\label{eq:berndtson-Z}
\end{align}
The induced \(4\times4\) first-order state map has determinant
\(\omega^{-4}\), and direct differentiation verifies its exact
intertwining with \eqref{eq:massive-Q}--\eqref{eq:massive-Z} at \(m=0\).

\begin{theorem}[Complete massive tensor first jet]\label{thm:mass-jet}
Transform the full \(m\)-derivative of
\eqref{eq:massive-Q}--\eqref{eq:massive-Z} through
\eqref{eq:berndtson-Q}--\eqref{eq:berndtson-Z}.  The reverse
tensor-to-vector source is rationally removable.  The tensor-led diagonal
tangent scalarizes to
\[
 L_2\dot\phi_2=(s_1D+s_0)\phi_2,
\]
where
\[
 s_1=-\frac{2(r-2)}{r^3\omega^2},\qquad
 s_0=\frac{(r-2)(3r^4\omega^2-14r+12)}
 {3r^5\omega^2}.
\]
Its projective density is
\[
 \mathcal I_{\rm phys}
 =\frac{(r-2)(3r^4\omega^2-20r+30)}
 {3r^5\omega^2},
\]
and obeys the exact normal form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:physical-mass-normal}
 \boxed{\;
 \mathcal I_{\rm phys}
 =\mathcal K_{U_2}\!\left(\frac{r}{6\omega^2}\right)
 +\frac13f.
 \;}
\end{equation}
Consequently,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:complete-mass-crosswalk}
 \boxed{\;
 [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
 =\frac{3i\omega}{2}[\mathcal I_{\rm phys}].
 \;}
\end{equation}

\begin{proof}
In the factor-state order
\((\phi_2,D\phi_2,\phi_1,D\phi_1)\), conjugating the full mass tangent

\eqref{eq:berndtson-Q}--\eqref{eq:berndtson-Z} gives four rational

\[
 L_1\dot\phi_1=
 \frac{32f}{9r^3\omega^2}\phi_2.
\]
Since
\[
 P=-\frac{16}{27\omega^2},\qquad
 L_1(P\phi_2)=\frac{32f}{9r^3\omega^2}\phi_2
 \quad\text{on }\ker L_2,
\]
the change \(\widehat\phi_1=\phi_1-mP\phi_2\) removes it.  Scalarizing
the tensor diagonal block gives the displayed \(s_1,s_0\).  Exact
substitution proves \eqref{eq:physical-mass-normal}; combining it with
Theorem~\ref{thm:mass-normal} proves
\eqref{eq:complete-mass-crosswalk}.
\end{proof}

The factor \(1/3\) is intrinsic.  If one tests the stronger but false

\[

\subsection{Endpoint-analytic massive Jost crosswalk}

Choose
\[

and the common massive infinity phase
\[
 e^{\sigma ikr}r^{\rho_\sigma(m)},\qquad

 \frac{\partial_m\phi_{2,\sigma}}{\phi_{2,\sigma}}

\begin{theorem}[Endpoint-compatible complete mass jet]
\label{thm:mass-jost}

system \eqref{eq:massive-Q}--\eqref{eq:massive-Z} has holomorphic
two-dimensional future-horizon-ingoing and infinity-outgoing Jost planes.

holomorphic same-sign changes of basis.  In particular, no opposite-Jost
component occurs.

Let \(a_{\rm phys}(\omega,m)\) be the spin-two Schur divisor after
eliminating the locally invertible spin-one channel.  Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:jost-mass-crosswalk}
 \boxed{\;
 b_{\rm B}(\omega)
 =\frac{3i\omega}{2}\,
   \partial_m a_{\rm phys}(\omega,0)
   +h(\omega)a_{\rm phys}(\omega,0),
 \;}
\end{equation}
where \(h\) is holomorphic.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write \(\Psi=(Q,Z)^{\mathsf T}\).  The complete equations have the form
\[
 D^2\Psi+\bigl[(\omega^2-mf)I_2+B(r)\bigr]\Psi=0,
 \qquad B(r)=O(r^{-2}),
\]
with
\[
 B=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 -f(10/r^2-16/r^3)&-8f(r-3)/r^3\\
 -2f/r^2&-f(4/r^2+2/r^3)
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
At infinity use the exact common phase
\[
 \Phi_\sigma=e^{\sigma ikx}r^{\sigma im/k},
 \qquad k^2=\omega^2-m,\qquad k(\omega,0)=\omega.
\]
Its logarithmic derivative is
\[
 D\log\Phi_\sigma
 =\sigma i\left(k+\frac{mf}{kr}\right),
\]
and direct substitution leaves the scalar residual
\[
 \frac{m}{k^2r^4}
 \left(
 4k^2r^2-\sigma ikr^2+6\sigma ikr-8\sigma ik
 {}-mr^2+4mr-4m

\]
On the certified \(\pi/4\) exterior-complex-scaled ray, the selected

The certified disk has nonzero real part and avoids every such resonance.

The differentiated Coulomb exponent vanishes, and the rational gauges

\end{proof}

\subsection{A complexified conserved, traceless source}

Let \(\ell\ge2\), \(\mu=(\ell-1)(\ell+2)\), and \(\omega\ne0\).  Use the
Martel--Poisson odd source projections and orientation
\(\epsilon_{tr}=1\), \(\epsilon^{tr}=-1\) \cite{martelpoisson2005}.

\begin{theorem}[Complexified conserved-source nonannihilation]
\label{thm:source}
For any
\[
 F\in C_c^\infty((2M,\infty)),
\]
define
\[
 P_t=0,\qquad
 P_r=\frac{\mu F}{2i\omega rf},\qquad
 P=\frac{\partial_r(rF)}{2i\omega}.
\]
Then
\[
 \nabla_aP^a+\frac2r r_aP^a-\frac{\mu}{r^2}P=0,
\]
and the Cunningham--Price--Moncrief source is
\[
 S_{\rm odd}
 =-\frac{2r}{\mu}\epsilon^{ab}\nabla_aP_b
 =\frac{F}{f}.
\]
The associated odd stress tensor
\[
 T^{aB}=\frac{P^a}{16\pi r^2}X^B,\qquad
 T^{AB}=\frac{P}{8\pi r^4}X^{AB},
\]
with all other harmonic components zero, is smooth and radially compact,
and satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\;
 \nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0,\qquad
 T^\mu{}_\mu=0.
 \;}
\]
The complexified conserved-traceless odd source map is therefore onto the
smooth compact reduced radial forcings.

Moreover, choose a nonnegative nonzero
\(\eta\in C_c^\infty((2M,\infty))\) supported where the adjoint state
\(\widetilde u_n\) is nonzero and set
\[
 F=\eta\,\overline{\widetilde u_n}.
\]
Then the augmented pairing has no endpoint term and
\[
 \boxed{\;
 \langle\widetilde u_n,F\rangle_{\rm aug}
 =\int\eta|\widetilde u_n|^2\,dr_*>0.
 \;}
\]
Thus the space of complexified frequency-domain conserved and traceless
odd sources does not annihilate the certified double pole.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since \(\sqrt{-\det g_{ab}}=1\),
\[
 P^r=fP_r=\frac{\mu F}{2i\omega r},
\qquad
 \nabla_aP^a=\partial_rP^r.
\]
Direct substitution proves the reduced conservation identity and
\(fS_{\rm odd}=F\).  The remaining spacetime conservation equations
vanish by the divergence-free odd vector harmonic, and tracelessness
follows from \(T^{ab}=0\) and
\(\Omega_{AB}X^{AB}=0\).  Compact support removes all endpoint terms from
the adjoint pairing, and the final integral is strictly positive.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What the source theorem does not say]
The choice \(F=\eta\overline{\widetilde u_n}\) depends on the complex QNM
frequency.  It is an existence proof in the complexified
frequency-domain source space, not a real, causal, temporally compact
matter source.  A real formal perturbation can be assembled by adjoining
the reflected frequency and harmonic sector, but causality and temporal
support still require an inverse-Laplace construction.  The theorem also
does not identify a specified material trajectory or an
energy-condition-satisfying model.  A standard massive point-particle
stress tensor is not trace-free and cannot be coupled directly to the
Bach equation without a conformal completion or compensator.
\end{remark}

the reduced Fredholm-domain issue without deciding whether a standard

\section{Conclusion}

The repeated Regge--Wheeler factor in axial Schwarzschild pure Weyl
gravity is not a direct sum.  It is a non-split self-extension with the
elementary projective class
\[
 [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
 =\frac{i\omega}{2}[1-2/r].
\]
Transforming the complete coupled massive axial system into its massless
Maxwell/Regge--Wheeler basis gives
\[
 [\mathcal I_{\rm phys}]=\frac13[1-2/r],
 \qquad
 [\mathcal I_{\rm Bach}]
 =\frac{3i\omega}{2}[\mathcal I_{\rm phys}].
\]
The corrected rational primitive matches the differentiated massive
phase, and the Coulomb exponent has vanishing first derivative.  The
matrix Frobenius/Volterra construction promotes the comparison to the
complete endpoint-normalized Jost planes and proves
\[
 \omega_n'(0)=\frac{2i}{3\omega_n}\kappa_n\ne0.
\]

At the certified damped Schwarzschild mode, the scalar QNM is simple but
the extension selector is nonzero.  The full connection is therefore
defective.  Its geometric root is Einstein; its generalized root has a
nonzero Ricci-carrier quotient.  The same intrinsic invariant controls the
nonzero rank-one
second-order pole of the exterior cut-off outgoing Green operator.

The leading pole survives an asymptotic observation test and a
complexified source-space test.  Its Einstein range has nonzero Bondi
shear, and a radially compact conserved, traceless odd source can have
strictly nonzero adjoint overlap.
The enhanced isolated-contour profile is therefore an ordinary outgoing
Einstein QNM multiplied by time, even though the constant generalized
component has weakened asymptotic falloff.

This gives a Schwarzschild counterpart, in asymptotically flat spacetime,
of the critical-gravity logarithmic-partner mechanism at the level of the
filtered Bach resonance.  The remaining work is genuinely global:
prove bounded causal metric/Bondi reconstruction and justify the retarded
contour deformation with threshold and high-frequency control.  The
fixed-domain complex-scaled Fredholm realization and the causal
Laplace-to-resonance bridge of the reduced radial response are complete.
