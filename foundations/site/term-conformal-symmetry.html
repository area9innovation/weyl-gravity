Conformal symmetry

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A conformal transformation rescales the metric while preserving angles and, in the Lorentzian setting, the light-cone directions. Conformal symmetry says the relevant theory respects an allowed version of this transformation.

Example and distinction

A rescaling can vary from point to point in a local conformal symmetry. This is not simply changing the numerical units in one formula; fields and the action must transform in the specified way.

Physics

Local Weyl transformations rescale the metric by a positive position-dependent factor, with suitable weights for other fields. Four-dimensional pure Weyl gravity has the corresponding classical conformal invariance.

Use in the model

The statement depends on dimension, action and boundary assumptions. Quantum preservation is a separate question; a classical symmetry does not itself rule out an anomaly or supply positive physical states.

Mathematics

A conformal class identifies metrics related by positive scalar rescaling. Conformal covariance of an operator or tensor requires a precise transformation law, which may involve weights.

Conditions and consequences

Invariance of a functional is not identical to pointwise invariance of every tensor component. Boundary terms, densities and the chosen variational class must be tracked when deriving symmetry identities.

Specialist

Conformal symmetry is a physical premise whose classical encoding is imported through the authoritative complex. Local quantum breakings must be classified and, where possible, canceled within the appropriate cohomological problem.

Project role and claim boundary

A trace-anomaly coefficient, a determinant and a BV master-equation breaking are distinct results. Their relationship must be proved, not inferred solely from the shared word conformal.
