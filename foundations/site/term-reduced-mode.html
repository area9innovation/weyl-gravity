REDUCED-MODE

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

REDUCED-MODE marks a result restricted to a specified mode sector or reduced model. The restriction can make a question tractable without answering it for the full theory.

Example and distinction

Reduced does not necessarily mean finite. A selected tensor sector can still contain infinitely many modes, so the difference between finite cutoffs and the whole selected family can remain crucial.

Physics

REDUCED-MODE identifies the chosen field sector, reduction or mode carrier supporting a calculation. Its normalization, commutator and state conventions define the result’s scope.

Use in the model

A positive finite block or an obstruction on the full reduced family does not automatically determine full metric-field positivity. A verified observable and pairing crosswalk is needed to relate those questions.

Mathematics

The reduced object must be specified together with the maps and properties that connect it to its source. Restriction, quotient and homotopy transfer are not interchangeable operations.

Conditions and consequences

Even an infinite-dimensional reduced carrier can omit decisive constraints or observables of the full complex. The tag alone does not certify its claimed embedding or all transitive import assumptions.

Specialist

The cutoff positivity obstruction is REDUCED-MODE and retains its reference and regularity hypotheses. The reconciled reduced certificate passes direct checks, not the complete full-metric import gate.

Project role and claim boundary

Do not identify its state representatives with W₊²/W₋² centered deformation classes. Neither reduced success nor reduced failure supplies an unqualified statement about the full Lorentzian physical spectrum.
