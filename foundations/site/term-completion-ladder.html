Completion ladder

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

The completion ladder organizes successive requirements beyond a finite wave calculation: limits, suitable function spaces, distributions, energy control and support properties. Each step asks what extra information or assumptions are needed.

Example and distinction

It is a map of obligations, not a promise that every higher step has been completed. A result at one rung should be read with its explicit additions and exclusions.

Physics

The ladder compares declared carriers and representations for the wave problem, beginning with exact finite modes and proceeding toward analytic and energy constructions. Different rungs discharge different properties.

Use in the model

Positive finite energy, completion and causal support are separate claims. A formal similarity of equations does not provide the maps or estimates needed to connect their carriers.

Mathematics

Each rung specifies an object, base, added assumptions, established statements and exclusions. The transitions can change topology, coding, quantifiers or test class, not merely increase dimension.

Conditions and consequences

Read the displayed arrows as claims requiring evidence, rather than inferring implications from their visual order. Representation invariance itself is an additional theorem with preservation conditions.

Specialist

The ladder is a typed evidence interface for scoped constructions. The finite PRA fixture and the RCA₀/ACA₀ reconstruction comparison must retain their distinct formulations.

Project role and claim boundary

Nothing in the ladder certifies the full metric BV propagator or a complete Lorentzian quantum theory. Its role is to expose missing joins and localize additional requirements in a controlled wave setting.
