Unitarity

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Unitary evolution preserves the inner-product structure used to compare quantum state vectors. In the usual positive Hilbert setting, this preserves their normalization and the total probability.

Example and distinction

The meaning depends on having the right state space. A calculation with real frequencies or a formally norm-preserving matrix is not by itself a complete argument that a gauge theory has sensible physical probabilities.

Physics

A unitary operator U on a Hilbert space satisfies U*U=UU*=1. Unitary dynamics or a unitary scattering operator must be established on the declared physical space.

Use in the model

An indefinite metric introduces a different preservation condition and does not automatically give ordinary probability conservation. Free-sector results also need not extend to interactions or the full gauge-reduced model.

Mathematics

Unitarity is an inner-product-preserving surjective linear isomorphism in the complex Hilbert setting. For continuous time evolution, generator and domain conditions enter the construction of a unitary group.

Conditions and consequences

A symmetric formal differential expression is not automatically self-adjoint, and spectral reality alone is not an existence theorem for the desired dynamics. The carrier and domains cannot be suppressed.

Specialist

The programme treats unitarity as a physical obligation independent of local BRST identities, finite positive blocks and Euclidean spectral calculations. Specify whether the claim concerns evolution or scattering.

Project role and claim boundary

No full interacting Lorentzian unitarity conclusion follows from the reduced cutoff analysis or certified residual vertex classes. A scoped failure of one stability shortcut likewise does not settle all possible unitarity constructions.
