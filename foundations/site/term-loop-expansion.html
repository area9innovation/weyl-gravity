Loop expansion

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A loop expansion organizes quantum corrections into successive orders. “One-loop” names one such order, not an assertion that the whole quantum problem has been solved.

Example and distinction

It resembles keeping a few stages of an approximation, although the expansion may be formal rather than convergent. A result at one order needs its order stated whenever it is used in a broader claim.

Physics

Loop order tracks powers of the quantum expansion parameter, conventionally ℏ, in perturbative contributions. Graph topology often records that order in a Feynman-diagram organization.

Use in the model

Field amplitude order and loop order are different. A one-loop coefficient can be useful without establishing higher-order consistency, a nonperturbative state or renormalized Lorentzian products for the full theory.

Mathematics

The construction may be a formal power series with algebraic coefficient identities at each order. Existence of those coefficients does not imply convergence at a finite physical value of the parameter.

Conditions and consequences

Recursive restoration can encounter new obstructions at later orders. The algebra, regularization conventions and allowed counterterms must be part of the statement at every step.

Specialist

Record both loop order and lifecycle state. A one-loop classified breaking, computed coefficient, canceled local identity and transferred residual term are four different achievements.

Project role and claim boundary

Do not infer all-order QME restoration or Lorentzian certification from a finite-order LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC or EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL calculation. The source operator and conventions must remain compatible with the classical import.
