PRA

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

Stands for: Primitive Recursive Arithmetic

General

PRA stands for Primitive Recursive Arithmetic. It is a mathematical rulebook for reasoning about explicitly built finite calculations and their repetitions.

Example and distinction

A fixed finite wave fixture can be checked by sums, products and other prescribed arithmetic operations. This does not turn every question about infinitely many approximations into a finite calculation; the limiting task needs its own assumptions.

Physics

Primitive Recursive Arithmetic (PRA) is the stated base for each fixed finite fixture in the ladder. It formalizes the arithmetic construction rather than adding physical dynamics.

Use in the model

The certificate concerns a declared finite family and its exact identities. A uniform claim about all inputs, or passage to a completed energy carrier, has different quantifiers and must be justified separately.

Mathematics

Primitive Recursive Arithmetic (PRA) is a first-order arithmetic theory with symbols for primitive recursive functions and quantifier-free induction in a standard presentation.

Conditions and consequences

Primitive recursion is a restricted way of defining total computations, not a synonym for every terminating algorithm. The formal language and the fixed-fixture quantifiers matter when comparing a PRA result with a second-order reconstruction assertion.

Specialist

Primitive Recursive Arithmetic (PRA) bounds the fixed-fixture arithmetic claims. It does not name a completed continuum field theory or supply the rate-free reconstruction principle WR.

Project role and claim boundary

Keep the finite encoding and exact verification separate from an assertion about arbitrary Cauchy data. A batch of successful fixture evaluations is reproduction of those cases, not automatically the theorem uniformly quantified over them.
