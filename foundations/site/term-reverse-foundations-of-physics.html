Reverse foundations of physics

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

Reverse foundations of physics starts with a job a theory must do—such as predicting a detector reading—and asks what physical ideas and mathematical assumptions make that job possible.

Example and distinction

This reverses the usual presentation that starts with an impressive equation and moves forward. It encourages a reader to ask where states, probabilities, limits and observations enter, and to notice when a necessary connection has not been supplied.

Physics

The programme decomposes a physical theory into postulates, carriers, logical existence assumptions, operational identifications and empirical protocols. It asks which combinations support a specified obligation.

Use in the model

This is broader than reverse mathematics, which calibrates logical strength over a base. A carrier comparison or empirical failure can be informative without being a reverse-mathematical equivalence.

Mathematics

Reverse foundations is an organizational programme comparing the structures and assumptions used to discharge physical obligations. It includes representation-sensitive existence questions alongside algebraic, analytic and empirical ones.

Conditions and consequences

The atlas is a coordinate system for those questions, not a single metatheorem that every cell has been mechanically proved. Interfaces between separately established results need explicit preservation conditions.

Specialist

The current synthesis organizes scoped implications without claiming a finished theory or universally formalized audit framework. The wave reversal and reduced positivity obstruction isolate different dependency failures.

Project role and claim boundary

Preserve the distinction between logical calibration, carrier construction, physical interpretation and empirical acceptance. A theory profile with many populated cells is not a compatible end-to-end realization of their claims.
