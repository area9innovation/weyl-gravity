AI editorial review

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

Stands for: Artificial intelligence

General

AI means Artificial intelligence. AI editorial review here means that an AI assistant drafted or checked an explanation against stated sources. It is not independent expert approval or proof that the explanation is correct.

Example and distinction

The useful questions are whether the terms are clear, the assumptions are visible and the evidence supports the stated conclusion. Fluent writing can still hide a mistake, so source links and corrections remain important.

Physics

Artificial intelligence (AI) can assist derivations, code, tests and exposition, but those activities produce different evidence. Editorial checking should preserve the physical quantity, mathematical hypotheses and scope across reader perspectives.

Use in the model

A rewritten explanation must not turn a local or reduced calculation into a full causal or empirical claim. The responsible research contact and the actual verification records remain part of the account.

Mathematics

Artificial intelligence (AI) editorial review is a provenance statement about how prose was produced and checked. It is distinct from a formal derivation, an independent computational verifier or peer review.

Conditions and consequences

Shared claim records can prevent some inconsistencies but cannot prove semantic equivalence of four explanations. The relation between prose, formal statement and encoded object still needs mathematical judgment.

Specialist

Artificial intelligence (AI) authorship does not alter dependency tags or acceptance gates. This dictionary’s accounts are AI-authored and AI-editorially reviewed, with pinned project sources and explicitly limited claim flags.

Project role and claim boundary

No scientific lifecycle is promoted by publication of definitions. Independent expert semantic review remains a separate activity, while mechanical tests cover structure, source freshness, links and reader interaction rather than physical truth.
