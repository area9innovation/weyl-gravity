Retarded and advanced solutions

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

A retarded solution responds only where a source can influence later events. An advanced solution uses the opposite temporal support direction and is a useful mathematical counterpart.

Example and distinction

These labels describe support relative to the chosen time orientation. Writing either word next to an inverse does not prove the support restriction; it must hold for the actual sources and geometry in the theorem.

Physics

Retarded and advanced Green operators impose future and past support, respectively, for compactly supported sources under the declared hypotheses. Their difference enters the classical causal response structure.

Use in the model

The sign convention for that difference should be fixed. It is not a positive quantum two-point function by itself, and specifying it does not choose a physical state.

Mathematics

Given a time-oriented spacetime, the support of a retarded response lies in the causal future of source support; the advanced version lies in its causal past. Operator identities and admissible spaces are additional requirements.

Conditions and consequences

Existence and uniqueness need hypotheses such as appropriate hyperbolicity and global geometry. A support inclusion alone does not supply all those analytic properties.

Specialist

Separate advanced/retarded identities, causal pairing and the state-dependent symmetric part of a two-point distribution. Matching a commutator does not establish positivity or Hadamard compatibility for the full gauge complex.

Project role and claim boundary

No Lorentzian certificate follows from a Euclidean spectral inverse. The imported geometry, field operator, domains and chain compatibility must be present at the scope of the claim.
