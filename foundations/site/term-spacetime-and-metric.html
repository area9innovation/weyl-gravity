Spacetime and metric

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Spacetime combines locations and times into one geometrical setting. A metric tells us how separations are measured; in relativity it also determines which directions light can travel.

Example and distinction

A diagram’s coordinates are labels, while the metric supplies geometrical information. A gravity model must specify how this geometry behaves. Changing the metric can change causal relationships, not just the appearance of a drawing.

Physics

A spacetime metric is a nondegenerate symmetric tensor fixing interval geometry and, in Lorentzian signature, null cones. The metric is dynamical in metric theories of gravity.

Use in the model

Causal support statements depend on this geometry. An analysis performed on a fixed background or reduced sector must not be read as a construction of the full dynamical metric theory.

Mathematics

A metric in this setting is a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form on each tangent space, varying with the base point. A Lorentzian metric is indefinite and is not a distance function in the metric-space sense.

Conditions and consequences

Additional global conditions enter evolution theorems. A local tensor identity alone does not supply the global causal or analytic hypotheses needed for a Green operator.

Specialist

Retain background geometry, signature and global assumptions as dependencies. Metric perturbations, scalar witness fields and induced mode pairings occupy different levels of the construction.

Project role and claim boundary

The programme’s failed full-export prerequisite is an algebraic consistency issue for a particular encoded object. It must not be confused with a general refutation of the underlying spacetime geometry or classical BRST architecture.
