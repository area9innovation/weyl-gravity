LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC marks a result about the specified local algebraic structures, such as identities or allowed correction terms. It is a way to say which part of the overall construction has been studied.

Example and distinction

Local here concerns the declared field expressions and their operations. It does not mean that a complete system of localized measurements or a causal physical state has already been built.

Physics

LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC includes local consistency and cohomological calculations in the declared field, derivative and functional setting. The coefficient domain and treatment of boundary terms are part of the result.

Use in the model

Classifying a counterterm or restoring a local quantum identity is important progress, but is distinct from Euclidean spectral evaluation or Lorentzian causal renormalization.

Mathematics

The tag identifies an algebraic dependency class rather than one fixed theorem. The actual differential, ansatz, quotient and exact witnesses determine what is proved.

Conditions and consequences

Pointwise local expressions, integrated functionals modulo boundary terms and residual objects must still be separated. Local algebraic manipulations do not supply topological convergence or existence of a positive representation.

Specialist

Use LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC for the relevant local result kind and record its lifecycle separately. CLASSIFIED, COEFFICIENT_COMPUTED and QME_RESTORED are not interchangeable labels.

Project role and claim boundary

A local restoration must precede residual transfer, and neither gives a LORENTZIAN_CERTIFIED full model. The authoritative classical import requirements remain in force even when a local calculation is independently valid.
