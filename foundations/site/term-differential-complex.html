Differential complex

Mathematical background and its role in the reconstruction problem.

General

A differential complex organizes objects and maps so that taking two successive steps gives zero. This rule makes it possible to distinguish meaningful structure from descriptions removed as redundant.

Example and distinction

If the two-step rule fails, the later classification called cohomology is not justified for that construction. The project’s current full export has such a consistency problem; fixing a few components does not establish the whole rule.

Physics

A complex packages fields, gauge data or related variables with maps satisfying the appropriate square-zero identity. Its differential is not necessarily an ordinary spacetime derivative.

Use in the model

This structure organizes redundancies, but its consistency does not by itself establish causal evolution or positive physical states. The full exported construction must pass its identities before claims depending on its transfer can be accepted.

Mathematics

A cochain complex consists of graded spaces Cⁿ and maps d:Cⁿ→Cⁿ⁺¹ with d∘d=0. A chain complex uses the opposite grading direction.

Conditions and consequences

The identity places images inside kernels, allowing cohomology or homology to be defined. An arbitrary sequence of maps, or a partially checked block matrix, is not yet a certified complex.

Specialist

The current serialized full object fails its differential-square prerequisite. The trace/ghost candidate repairs exposed blocks but has not established full nilpotency and all compatible import data.

Project role and claim boundary

Keep a verified classical construction, a particular serialized export and a partial repair distinct. A scoped export failure blocks its dependent claims without automatically invalidating every classical or reduced calculation.
