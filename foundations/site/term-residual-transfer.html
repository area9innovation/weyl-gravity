Residual transfer

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

Residual transfer moves specified structure from a larger complex to its smaller residual description using verified maps. It must show that the information relevant to the claim survives the move.

Example and distinction

The smaller description can be easier to study, but a successful calculation there is meaningful only when the connection to the original data is established at the required scope.

Physics

A quantum residual transfer transports an already justified correction through the declared classical reduction. Compatibility with differential, pairing and conventions must be retained.

Use in the model

The programme requires restoration of the local quantum master equation before transferring a correction. Transfer is not a substitute for restoration and does not itself produce a full physical state or causal propagator.

Mathematics

Transfer depends on contraction data and on the operations being transported, potentially including multilinear structures. Linear cohomology equivalence alone may not determine the transferred interactions.

Conditions and consequences

The identities and side conditions must hold for the actual source and target objects. Domain, topology or support preservation are further requirements when analytic or causal structures are involved.

Specialist

RESIDUAL_TRANSFERRED is distinct from QME_RESTORED and LORENTZIAN_CERTIFIED. Keep the exact imported classical snapshot, coefficient conventions and correction lifecycle in the receipt.

Project role and claim boundary

The current partial repair cannot authorize a full transfer relying on uncertified nilpotency or cyclicity. Local and reduced certificates remain valid only within their declared dependencies.
