Quotient

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

General

A quotient groups objects that a specified rule regards as equivalent and treats each group as one object. It removes distinctions that the rule says should not matter.

Example and distinction

For gauge descriptions, this can organize different descriptions of the same proposed physical situation. But a quantity is well defined on the quotient only if equivalent descriptions give the same quantity.

Physics

A quotient removes declared redundancies or null directions. Observables, dynamics and pairings descend only when compatible with the equivalence relation.

Use in the model

Deleting an inconvenient mode is not automatically a valid physical quotient. The programme requires showing that the removed directions are actually redundant and that the intended physical structures survive the reduction.

Mathematics

Given an equivalence relation, the quotient is the set of equivalence classes. Vector-space or algebra quotients require subspaces or ideals compatible with the relevant operations.

Conditions and consequences

A quotient topology or descended pairing brings further conditions. Cohomology is a particular quotient of closed by exact elements, defined only when the differential-square condition places the image inside the kernel.

Specialist

State the quotient relation and the structures proved to descend. Local functionals modulo boundary terms, gauge-state reduction and residual cohomology are different quotient constructions.

Project role and claim boundary

A certified class in one quotient is not automatically a physical state in another. The failed full export cannot authorize cohomological claims relying on its inconsistent differential, even if a formal quotient expression can be written.
