Curvature and Weyl tensor

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

Curvature measures how geometry differs from flat geometry. The Weyl tensor is a particular trace-free part of spacetime curvature, central to the action used in Weyl gravity.

Example and distinction

Curvature has several components with different roles. Saying that a theory uses the Weyl tensor does not mean all its solutions or physical predictions have already been determined from that one geometrical ingredient.

Physics

The Riemann curvature tensor decomposes into Ricci-dependent parts and the trace-free Weyl part in the relevant dimension. The pure-Weyl action uses a contraction quadratic in Weyl curvature.

Use in the model

Index placement affects conformal transformation formulas, and the four-dimensional action has its specific scaling property. Gauge reduction, boundary conditions and quantum interpretation remain additional requirements.

Mathematics

The Weyl tensor is the totally trace-free curvature component, obtained by subtracting the metric–Ricci contributions from the Riemann tensor. Its definition and possible vanishing depend on dimension.

Conditions and consequences

A tensor identity, its integrated quadratic functional and a cohomology class represented by that functional are different objects. An invariant notation does not identify their mathematical result types.

Specialist

The residual labels W₊² and W₋² refer to centered deformation/vertex classes in the certified programme. They must not be interpreted as one-particle graviton states.

Project role and claim boundary

Curvature-based local classifications inform quantum ansätze, but coefficients, master-equation restoration and full Lorentzian constructions require their own scoped evidence. The dictionary does not promote those lifecycle states.
