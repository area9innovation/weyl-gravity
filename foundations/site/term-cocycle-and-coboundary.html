Cocycle and coboundary

Mathematical structures, hypotheses and their role in the atlas.

General

A cocycle is an object that passes the complex’s consistency test. A coboundary is one produced by applying the differential to an earlier object, so it counts as trivial in cohomology.

Example and distinction

Every coboundary is a cocycle when the differential squares to zero. The interesting question is whether a given cocycle is also a coboundary; checking only that it passes the first test does not settle that.

Physics

A cocycle is closed under the declared gauge or cohomological differential; a coboundary is exact. Their quotient can classify observables, deformations or anomalies, depending on the problem.

Use in the model

A nonzero representative need not be a nontrivial class. One must check whether an allowed primitive removes it, with the correct degree, locality and boundary conventions.

Mathematics

For a differential d, a cocycle z satisfies dz=0 and a coboundary has the form db. Square-zero makes every coboundary closed, allowing the quotient H=ker d/im d.

Conditions and consequences

Nontriviality requires ruling out b in the specified domain. A primitive outside the allowed local or regularity class does not establish triviality in the original problem.

Specialist

Retain exact cocycle and trivialization witnesses together with the generated ansatz and canonical reduction. A basis printed by a producer is not an independent nontriviality check.

Project role and claim boundary

Local BRST classes, integrated functionals modulo boundary terms and residual classes use different domains. A certificate in one must not be cited as the answer for another without a verified comparison.
