Locality and spatial localization

Physical meaning, mathematical requirements and limits of interpretation.

General

Locality concerns how a law relates nearby places or events. Spatial localization concerns where a particular object is concentrated or can be nonzero. They are related questions, but not the same property.

Example and distinction

A differential equation can be local while one of its sinusoidal solutions extends across all space. Keeping only a finite list of such waves does not automatically create a disturbance confined to a small region.

Physics

Locality may refer to local differential dynamics or, in a quantum setting, specified compatibility at spacelike separation. Localization refers to support or concentration of data, fields or states.

Use in the model

Each use needs a precise statement. The finite chiral construction establishes an equation and energy within its family but does not certify spatial localization or a local observable algebra.

Mathematics

Spatial support is a property of a function or distribution. Locality of an operator typically restricts dependence on its input near a point; quantum locality is formulated through algebraic relations and causal regions.

Conditions and consequences

A support theorem, an operator-locality statement and a commutation condition are different assertions. The intended topology and the meaning of concentration must also be specified when exact support is not claimed.

Specialist

Keep local BRST cohomology, localized configurations and Lorentzian causal products distinct. The adjective local does not identify their result types.

Project role and claim boundary

Finite mode truncations can lose locality even when the original differential expression is local. Any interface used to transfer a locality or support claim needs its own preservation argument.
