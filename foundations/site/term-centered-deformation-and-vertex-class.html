Centered deformation and vertex class

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

A deformation class describes an allowed way to change a mathematical theory while respecting its declared equivalences. A vertex class concerns an interaction term in that setting. Centered refers to the convention fixed by the project’s construction.

Example and distinction

These are statements about changing the theory’s data. They are not automatically states of a particle moving through spacetime, even when the notation resembles a familiar mode label.

Physics

The certified centered residual classes W₊² and W₋² have deformation or vertex roles. Their representative, background convention and cohomological quotient determine that statement.

Use in the model

An admissible interaction direction is not an on-shell scattering amplitude or a one-particle graviton. Nonlinear continuation and positive quantum interpretation need additional evidence.

Mathematics

A deformation class is a cohomological object in a specified deformation problem. Its degree, differential and equivalence relation determine what variation it parameterizes; higher-order obstructions can constrain continuation.

Conditions and consequences

A vertex representative is not identified solely by its displayed polynomial. The centered convention and the residual transfer used by the certificate are part of its meaning.

Specialist

Preserve the programme’s authoritative identification: W₊² and W₋² are centered deformation/vertex classes, not one-particle graviton states. Do not substitute a reduced tensor-state basis for them.

Project role and claim boundary

A local quantum correction must pass restoration and transfer gates before being assigned a residual interpretation. Existing class certification does not establish its quantum coefficient, a physical state or Lorentzian causal construction.
