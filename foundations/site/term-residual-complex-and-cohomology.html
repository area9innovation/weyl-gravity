Residual complex and cohomology

How to read the programme’s constructions, dependencies and current status.

General

A residual complex is the smaller mathematical object retained by a specified homological reduction. Its cohomology records what survives the reduced differential, according to that construction.

Example and distinction

Residual here does not mean a prediction error in a data fit. It also does not automatically mean a physical particle or a positive state; the surviving objects can instead describe deformations and interactions.

Physics

Residual data result from a declared contraction or transfer of a complex. Their physical interpretation depends on which degrees, fields and pairings the reduction actually retains.

Use in the model

Centered deformation classes and reduced tensor-state sectors are different objects. A common word such as residual or mode is not a verified identification between them.

Mathematics

A residual complex is a target of a specified reduction with its own differential and structure. Its differential may vanish in a particular model, but that is a property to establish, not part of the general definition.

Conditions and consequences

A cohomology comparison requires valid complexes and compatible maps. Pairings and nonlinear operations may impose further transfer conditions beyond the linear chain-homotopy relation.

Specialist

Keep certified W₊²/W₋² centered deformation classes distinct from the reduced reference’s state/cohomology representatives. They answer different questions and use different carriers.

Project role and claim boundary

The current full-export consistency failure blocks transfers relying on that object. A residual calculation does not retroactively certify the required classical import or establish a full positive Lorentzian state.
