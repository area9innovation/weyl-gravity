Chirality

Physical meaning, analytic conditions and limits of interpretation.

General

Chirality distinguishes two directional or handed sectors of a construction. In the wave benchmark, the chiral data describe the two directions in which the selected wave profiles travel.

Example and distinction

This is a useful way to split a simple wave into pieces. In other physics settings, chirality has a different precise definition, so the word alone does not identify a particle’s spin or a gravity mode’s physical status.

Physics

For the benchmark, chiral components are the left- and right-moving data entering the fixed wave construction. Their domain, zero-mean conditions and detector coupling are specified separately.

Use in the model

This use should not be identified automatically with spinor chirality, helicity or a self-dual curvature sector. The relevant field type and geometry determine which decomposition is meant.

Mathematics

Chiral splitting denotes a declared decomposition into two sectors, not a universal operation on every carrier. In the scalar periodic wave model, characteristic translation gives the concrete split.

Conditions and consequences

The decomposition’s maps, periodicity and coefficient conditions belong to the statement. A finite labelled chiral family does not itself define an infinite completed space or prove support properties of its members.

Specialist

Keep the wave benchmark’s chiral data distinct from W₊²/W₋² labels for centered curvature deformation classes. Similar plus/minus notation does not provide an identification between these objects.

Project role and claim boundary

The finite fixture’s exact residual and energy claims concern its declared sector and normalization. No particle interpretation or full metric BV propagation follows from calling it chiral.
