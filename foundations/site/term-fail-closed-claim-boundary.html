Fail-closed claim boundary

How to read the programme’s claims and evidence.

General

Fail-closed means that a claim is not accepted when a required check is missing, unfinished or unsuccessful. Silence from a check is not treated as a pass.

Example and distinction

If a full construction needs six conditions and only five were checked, the complete claim stays open. The five successes may still be useful, but they do not fill the missing condition.

Physics

A fail-closed gate blocks dependent use of an object until all required acceptance conditions are verified. It distinguishes an unverified construction from a refuted theory.

Use in the model

The current serialized full gravity object fails its consistency prerequisite, and a partial repair has not passed the full gate. Local or reduced results can remain valid, but cannot supply that missing acceptance by analogy.

Mathematics

A fail-closed workflow authorizes a conclusion only after its declared prerequisites have positive evidence. Unknown, timeout and skip states are not identified with truth of the required predicate.

Conditions and consequences

Failure of a particular witness does not logically negate existence of every possible witness. The gate’s domain and completeness requirements therefore remain part of the result, even when the implementation reports a simple pass or fail.

Specialist

The classical import gate requires nilpotency, contraction, chain-map, cyclicity, residual-cohomology and pairing checks for the required snapshot. Partial data cannot claim a freeze certificate.

Project role and claim boundary

Lifecycle promotion must be explicit and scoped. The repaired trace/ghost blocks do not establish full transfer, while a failed architecture does not erase unrelated certified calculations or refute the entire classical programme.
