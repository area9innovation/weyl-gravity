Quantum probabilities must be nonnegative. In this specified model of gravitational tensor waves, we can change the state to satisfy that requirement for any finite set of modes. But no smooth change works for all modes while preserving the commutator and the standard short-distance condition, called the Hadamard condition.

The correction is allowed to depend on the cutoff. Passing each finite test with a different correction therefore does not supply one acceptable continuum state. These finite repairs test positivity; they do not establish positive energy or stable interacting dynamics.

The repair cannot stay uniformly smooth

A fixed measure of the correction's size and spatial derivatives must obey B²M ≥ (K−1)³/(8K). At this cutoff, its necessary lower bound is 27/32.

B depends only on a fixed smooth time filter. M measures derivatives of the correction. The lower bound grows without limit. This is a formula from the proof, not a simulation or a measured probability.
