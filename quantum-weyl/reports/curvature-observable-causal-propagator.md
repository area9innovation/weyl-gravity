# Curvature observable causal propagator

The autonomous Weyl-curvature complex still lacks separately certified
advanced and retarded Green inverses.  Nevertheless, its gauge-invariant
observable commutator is now constructed by exact transport through the
completed full BV causal complex.

Let \(T_{\rm state}=(C_1,\operatorname{div}C_1)\) be the certified
support-local curvature map, with exact BV formal adjoint
\(T_{\rm state}^\sharp\).  With the curvature source lift and solution
restriction

\[
  J_C=i_{\rm aux}T_{\rm state}^\sharp,
  \qquad
  R_C=T_{\rm state}p_{\rm aux}=J_C^\sharp,
\]

define

\[
  \Delta_C^{\rm obs}
  =R_C\Delta_\Lambda J_C,
  \qquad
  \Delta_\Lambda=\Lambda_{\rm full,+}-\Lambda_{\rm full,-}.
\]

All outer maps are finite-order differential operators.  Hence they preserve
causal support.  Exact cyclicity and
\(\Delta_\Lambda^\sharp=-\Delta_\Lambda\) give

\[
  (\Delta_C^{\rm obs})^\sharp=-\Delta_C^{\rm obs}.
\]

The identities

\[
  T_{\rm state}K_{\rm aux}=0,
  \qquad
  K_{\rm aux}^\sharp T_{\rm state}^\sharp=0
\]

prove gauge invariance on both solution and test-source sides.  The resulting
pairing reproduces exactly the curvature-image presymplectic CCR form.
