## Restriction to (D)

The calculation next restricts the local ghost-number-one cocycle to the
one-dimensional algebra generated by \(D\).  A Diff \(\times\) Weyl
reducibility obeys

\[
 \mathcal L_{X_D}g+2\sigma_Dg=0,
 \qquad \sigma_D=-{1\over4}\nabla\!\cdot X_D.
\]

For cylinder time translation,

\[
 X_D=\partial_t,\qquad \sigma_D=0.
\]

Consequently the full local bulk Weyl-anomaly pullback is zero:

\[
 c_D\sigma_D\left({199\over30}C^2-{87\over20}E_4\right)=0.
\]

For real Minkowski dilation,

\[
 X_D=x^\mu\partial_\mu,\qquad \sigma_D=-1,
\]

so the corresponding local cocycle coordinates are

\[
 c_D\left(-{199\over30}C^2+{87\over20}E_4\right).
\]

For both generators \(\sigma_D\) is constant.  The intrinsic type-A lower
descent therefore vanishes after this one-generator restriction, while type B
has trivial intrinsic descent.  Every universal Diff descendant also vanishes:
the top anomaly already contains \(c_D\), and another contraction contributes
a second copy, so \(c_D^2=0\).
