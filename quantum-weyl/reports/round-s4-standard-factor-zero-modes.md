# Round-S4 standard-factor zero modes

Dependency tag: `EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL`.

For transverse spin-\(s\) harmonics on the round unit four-sphere,

\[
-\nabla^2\big|_{(s,n)}=n(n+3)-s,
\qquad n\ge s.
\]

Applying this spectrum to the four standard conformal-spin-two factors gives:

| factor | operator | kernel |
|---|---|---:|
| `physical_depth_0` | `Delta_2_perp(4)` | 0 |
| `physical_depth_1` | `Delta_2_perp(2)` | 0 |
| `ghost_depth_1` | `Delta_1_perp(-3)` | 10 |
| `ghost_depth_0` | `Delta_0(-4)` | 5 |

Both ghost kernels occur at harmonic level \(n=1\). The ten transverse-vector
modes are the Killing vectors. The five scalar harmonics generate the proper
conformal Killing directions. At scalar Laplacian eigenvalue four, the exact
Diff--Weyl Faddeev--Popov matrix is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\-8&8\end{pmatrix},
\]

whose kernel is generated by \((c,\omega)=(1,1)\). The two ghost sectors
therefore reproduce all fifteen conformal reducibility modes.

The corresponding standard determinants are primed by deleting these ghost
kernels; the physical TT determinants are unprimed. The scalar level-zero
eigenvalue is \(-4\), not zero, and its regulator phase remains open.

This is not yet the complete repository zero-mode ledger: the round-\(S^4\)
repository TT Hessian dictionary, finite conformal-group volume, auxiliary
rows, regulator, and phase must still be supplied.
