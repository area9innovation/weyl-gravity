# Relative Einstein--Weyl cyclic-pushforward obstruction

The smallest declared action BV carriers on the compact magnetic product are
the 38-row Einstein--Maxwell and 40-row Weyl--Maxwell cotangent-complete
complexes. Their row layouts independently satisfy the degree-one duality
rule and have nondegenerate action-derived odd pairings.

The canonical 316-row relative carrier also remains valid. It consists of the
160-row five-current resolution, the complete 78-row relative cone, and all
78 shifted cotangent-dual rows. It is minimal within that declared
**full-cone cotangent** class: a full cotangent dual has the same rank as the
cone. This is not an absolute row-minimality theorem. The earlier 28-row rank
lower bound remains only necessary, and no covariant mixed-bundle completion
attaining it is known.

## The decisive obstruction

An action-compatible cyclic inclusion or its adjoint pushforward would induce,
on every generic axial and polar \(q\)-primary solution fibre, an invertible
matrix \(S\) satisfying

\[
S^\sharp\Omega_{\rm WM,q}S=\Omega_{\rm EM}.
\]

Product symmetry and time translation preserve the labelled \(q\) shells.
The extra Weyl \(p\) shell has a different frequency, so it cannot mix with a
\(q\) shell in an equivariant chain map.

For \(\lambda=\ell(\ell+1)\ge 6\), the Einstein action forms have determinant

\[
2\lambda,\qquad 2(\lambda-2),
\]

and inertia \((2,0)\). The Weyl action forms restricted to the complete
\(q\)-primary image have determinant

\[
-\lambda(9\lambda-2),\qquad
-(\lambda-2)(9\lambda-2),
\]

and inertia \((1,1)\). But

\[
\det(S^T\Omega_{\rm WM,q}S)
=\det(S)^2\det(\Omega_{\rm WM,q}),
\]

so an invertible congruence cannot turn either negative target determinant
into its positive source determinant. Chain homotopies, field redefinitions,
and exact current improvements do not change the induced nondegenerate
cohomology inertia.

This generic fibre is enough: an all-sector pushforward would have to restrict
to it. Exceptional and global zero modes therefore cannot repair the
obstruction, although they are not classified by this result.

## Disposition

The canonical 316-row cotangent pairing is not the transport of the two
action-derived pairings. Consequently it cannot supply the
action-compatible pushforward needed for a target-valued relative QME
subtraction. Matched Einstein--Maxwell/Weyl--Maxwell one-loop insertion work
is not authorized on the standard action-pairing route.

The noncyclic contravariant observable cone remains valid. Another possible
programme would explicitly change a pairing or the theory and certify that
new choice; it would not be the requested standard-action theorem.
