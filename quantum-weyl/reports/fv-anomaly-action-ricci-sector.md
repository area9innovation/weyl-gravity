## Exact anomaly action

With

\[
 c=\frac{199}{30},\qquad a=\frac{87}{20},\qquad
 \mathcal E_4=E_4-\frac23\Box R,
\]

the coefficient-bearing Fradkin–Vilkovisky representative is

\[
 \Gamma_{1,\mathrm{FV}}=\frac1{(4\pi)^2}\int\!\sqrt g\,
 \left[(cC^2-a\mathcal E_4)\Sigma_{\mathrm{FV}}
 +2a\Sigma_{\mathrm{FV}}\Delta_4\Sigma_{\mathrm{FV}}
 +\frac a{18}R^2\right].
\]

The exact response ledger gives

\[
 -4a+4a=0,
 \qquad
 \frac{2a}{3}-12\frac a{18}=0,
\]

so its Weyl variation is precisely

\[
 \delta_\sigma\Gamma_{1,\mathrm{FV}}
 =\frac1{(4\pi)^2}\int\!\sqrt g\,\sigma(cC^2-aE_4).
\]

The Riegert–Fradkin–Tseytlin specialization
\(\Sigma_{\mathrm{RFT}}=\frac14G_4\mathcal E_4\) independently reproduces
the stored coefficients

\[
 \left(\frac c4,-\frac a8,\frac a{18}\right)
 =\left(\frac{199}{120},-\frac{87}{160},\frac{29}{120}\right).
\]

## Disposition of the putative nonlocal \(R^2\) datum

In the declared massless conformal FV decomposition,

\[
 \Gamma_1[g]=\Gamma_{1,\mathrm{FV}}[g]+W_1[\bar g[g]],
 \qquad R[\bar g]=0.
\]

The conformal functional \(W_1\) is generated by Weyl-curvature invariants
before evaluation on the scalar-flat representative. Consequently every term
containing at least one Ricci scalar in a generic original-metric expansion is
fixed by the anomaly action and the re-expansion of \(W_1[\bar g]\). A term
written as \(R F(\Box)R\) may therefore appear in that generic basis, but its
form factor is determined; it is not a separately specifiable datum.

This replaces the earlier over-broad gate “compute an independent nonlocal
\(R^2\) form factor.” The remaining coefficient-bearing effective-action
gates are the independent cubic-and-higher Weyl form factors, finite local
\(C^2\) normalization, absolute extended \(R(\widehat g)^2\) normalization,
global inverse/boundary data, and renormalized products.

## Claim boundary

The theorem uses the normalized FV inverse on an asymptotically flat or
fixed-boundary domain and the formal curvature algebra appropriate to a
massless classically conformal theory. It does not apply unchanged to massive,
explicitly nonconformal, or independently symmetry-broken theories. It does
not supply a complete \(\Gamma_1\), complete \(Q_1\), residual transfer, or
any Lorentzian, Hadamard, positivity, particle, scattering, or unitarity
theorem.
