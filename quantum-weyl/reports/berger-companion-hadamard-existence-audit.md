# Berger companion Hadamard existence audit

The Berger companion is certified Green-hyperbolic and null-cone
decomposable, but this does not construct a Hadamard state. Fewster's
Definition 5.2 constrains the wavefront set of the Pauli--Jordan propagator;
the general results then describe and propagate a Hadamard class once a state
exists. Theorem 5.3 supplies existence for normally hyperbolic real formally
hermitian operators with a positive-definite hermitian fibre metric.

Those existence hypotheses do not apply directly here. The companion has
principal symbol

```text
sigma_2(C20)=[[q I10,0],[sigma_2(V2),q I10]],
```

with a nonzero square-zero lower-left null block, rather than the scalar
normally hyperbolic symbol. The complete BV state space carries an odd/Krein
pairing, not the positive-definite fibre metric in Theorem 5.3.

The local base-wave singular parametrix, companion decomposability, graded
causal commutator and 26-to-54 covariance lift are ready. A state still
requires the complete stationary `A104`, Cauchy BRST operator, Cauchy
Lagrange/Krein form, real structure, common closed realization, isolated-zero
ledger, positive or explicitly Krein covariance, and the BRST Ward identity
for the resulting distributional kernel.

The exact next gate is therefore
`IMPORT_BERGER_RETAINED_26_STATIONARY_GENERATOR_V1`. No Hadamard, positivity,
renormalized-product, QME or quantum claim is promoted.

Primary scope reference: [Fewster, *Hadamard States for Decomposable
Green-Hyperbolic Operators*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-025-05512-1),
Definition 5.2 and Theorem 5.3.
