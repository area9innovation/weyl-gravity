# Standard unitary matter does not cancel the obstruction

Dependency tags: `LOCAL-ALGEBRAIC`, `EUCLIDEAN-SPECTRAL`.

In the repository basis `(omega C2, omega E4)`, the strict pure-Weyl vector is

\[
\left({199\over30},-{87\over20}\right).
\]

For nonnegative multiplicities of standard-sign free conformal matter, the
two cancellation equations are

\[
{199\over30}+{N_s\over120}+{N_W\over40}
+{N_D\over20}+{N_v\over10}=0,
\]

\[
-{87\over20}-{N_s\over360}-{11N_W\over720}
-{11N_D\over360}-{31N_v\over180}=0.
\]

The first coordinate is strictly positive on the entire nonnegative matter
cone; equivalently, minus the second coordinate is strictly positive there.
Either coordinate functional is an exact separating dual witness. Therefore
the solution set is empty even over nonnegative real multiplicities, and a
fortiori over physical integer multiplicities.

This rules out cancellation only for free, massless, standard-sign real
conformal scalars, Weyl/Dirac fermions, and gauge vectors with the matched
anomaly sign. It does not cover interacting fixed points, nonunitary or
wrong-sign fields, conformal higher spins, boundary anomalies, or a
Wess--Zumino compensator extension. The strict-field-content QME obstruction
therefore persists throughout this declared ordinary unitary matter class.
