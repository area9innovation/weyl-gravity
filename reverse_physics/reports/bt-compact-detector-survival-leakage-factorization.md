## Result

The coherent compact detector's no-click effect is a valid operational
complement, but it is not generally the same as genuine BT forward survival.
The distinction is scattering outside the selected detector acceptance.

Let `A_C` be the leading transition into the selected compact click region and
let `B_out` contain all leading positive outputs that the detector does not
record. The complete transition column is

\[
 C_4=\binom{A_C}{B_{\rm out}},\qquad
 C_4^*C_4=A_C^*A_C+B_{\rm out}^*B_{\rm out}.
\]

In the order-`lambda^8` cut component generated by this order-`lambda^4`
column, pseudo-unitarity fixes the Hermitian forward coefficient from the
*complete* column:

\[
 \operatorname{Herm}(M_8)_{{\rm source},C_4\text{-cut}}
 =-\frac12\left(A_C^*A_C+B_{\rm out}^*B_{\rm out}\right).
\]

Other perturbative products and cut-free order-`lambda^8` terms are separate
and are not computed here. Even this cut component cannot be inferred from
`A_C` alone. Nevertheless the outside term cancels
when genuine survival and unobserved leakage are grouped into the binary
no-click event. Therefore

\[
 E_{\rm click}=A_C^*A_C,\qquad
 E_{\rm no}=I-A_C^*A_C
\]

remain exactly the correct leading detector effects. The missing BT virtual
graph is not needed to calculate this binary probability. It is needed to
separate “nothing happened” from “something happened outside the detector,”
or to construct a complete finite-time evolution or scattering operator.
