## Result

coupling on the positive auxiliary free-field carrier.  This closes the
operator-domain and finite-detector-strength gap left by the selected-core
matrix calculation.

On the positive carrier the certified adjoint is

\[
  \Omega^*=\Upsilon,\qquad \Upsilon^*=\Omega .
\]

Thus the auxiliary pair is a massless complex scalar field and its adjoint,
or equivalently two ordinary real free fields.  For a smooth compactly
supported switching function \(h\), let

\[
 A_h=D_\Omega(h),\qquad B_h=D_\Upsilon(h)
\]

be the two charged derivative Wick quadratics defined on the common dense
Weyl-invariant exponential-polynomial domain \({\cal D}\), generated by Weyl
translates of finite-particle vectors.  Their local distributional
adjoint relations give

\[
 D_\Upsilon(\bar h)\subset D_\Omega(h)^*,\qquad
 D_\Omega(\bar h)\subset D_\Upsilon(h)^* .
\]

It follows that the column

\[
 K_h\psi=(A_h\psi,B_h\psi):{cal H}\longrightarrow
 {cal H}\oplus{\cal H}
\]

is closable.  No separate essential-self-adjointness theorem for either
charged branch is assumed.  If \(K_h\) denotes the closure, the canonical
ground/click pointer block

\[
 \boxed{
 V_h=\begin{pmatrix}0&K_h^*\\ K_h&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \operatorname{Dom}V_h=operatorname{Dom}K_h\oplus
                         \operatorname{Dom}K_h^*

is self-adjoint.  It is affiliated with the three-pointer matrix

 E_{\rm click}(g)=\sin^2(g|K_h|),\qquad

These are exact positive complementary local effects.  They include the

## Positive local carrier

Let \({\cal H}_1\) be the direct sum of the positive-frequency particle and
antiparticle one-particle spaces and let
\({\cal H}=\Gamma_s({\cal H}_1)\).  The two real components

\[
 T={\Omega+\Upsilon\over2},\qquad
 Y={\Omega-\Upsilon\over2i}
\]

are self-adjoint free fields on this Hilbert space.  If \({\cal F}({\cal O})\)
is their Weyl algebra for test functions supported in \({\cal O}\), the
affiliation target used here is the dual net

\[
 {cal F}^{d}({\cal O})={\cal F}({\cal O}')'.
\]

This is a genuine positive free local carrier.  It is not an assertion that
the original two public fields remain separately Hermitian: the preceding
real-structure certificate proves that those requirements are incompatible
with the public cross two-point function in a positive Hilbert vacuum.

## Closability without branch essential self-adjointness

The derivative Wick quadratics are operator-valued distributions on the
Weyl-invariant exponential-polynomial domain \({\cal D}\).  Their real
differential kernel and
\(\Omega^*=\Upsilon\) give the two adjoint inclusions above.  Each inclusion
places a dense domain inside the corresponding adjoint domain, so each branch
is closable.

There is also a direct column proof.  Suppose
\(\psi_n\to0\) and
\(K_h\psi_n\to(x,y)\).  Pairing the first component with vectors in the
dense domain of \(D_\Upsilon(\bar h)\), and the second with vectors in the
dense domain of \(D_\Omega(\bar h)\), gives \(x=0=y\).  Hence the graph of
the column has no nonzero vertical limit and \(K_h\) is closable.

This argument deliberately avoids a stronger assertion.  Essential
self-adjointness of derivative Wick squares can depend on a one-particle
compression problem, and it is neither needed nor claimed here.

## Canonical self-adjoint block

For any closed densely defined operator
\(K:{\cal H}\to{\cal H}\oplus{\cal H}\), define

\[
 V=\begin{pmatrix}0&K^*\\K&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then

\[
 V^2=\operatorname{diag}(K^*K,KK^*).
\]

At \(z=\pm i\), the explicit resolvent is

\[
 (V-z)^{-1}=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 z(K^*K-z^2)^{-1}&K^*(KK^*-z^2)^{-1}\\
 K(K^*K-z^2)^{-1}&z(KK^*-z^2)^{-1}
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

The positive self-adjoint operators \(I+K^*K\) and \(I+KK^*\) are
bijective, and the mixed resolvent factors in the displayed matrix are
bounded.  Multiplication on the natural domains gives the identity on both
sides.  Thus both deficiency ranges are the full amplified Hilbert space and
\(V\) is self-adjoint.  The certificate contains a nonsquare rational
\(3\)-by-\(2\) witness that independently checks the block ordering,
\(K^*K\) versus \(KK^*\), and the exact inverse of \(I+V^2\).

## Local affiliation and symmetries

Choose a double cone \({\cal O}\) containing the support of \(h\).  Every
Weyl unitary localized in \({\cal O}'\) preserves \({\cal D}\) by its
definition and commutes there with both Wick branches by free-field locality.
The commutation passes
to the closures.  Hence the column and its adjoint commute with the
appropriate commutant unitaries, and

\[
 V_h\quad\hbox{is affiliated with}\quad
 B(\mathbb C^3)\,\bar\otimes\,{\cal F}^{d}({\cal O}).
\]

The two field branches have charges \(+2\) and \(-2\).  Assigning the two
click states charges \(-2\) and \(+2\), respectively, makes the complete
pointer interaction neutral.  Pointer exchange combined with the certified
ghost exchange \(\kappa\) commutes with \(V_h\).  Therefore the self-adjoint
coupling is also ghost even.

Affiliation and self-adjointness imply that every bounded Borel function of
\(V_h\) belongs to the amplified local algebra.  In particular \(U_g\) is an
exact local unitary.  If \(K=W|K|\) is the polar decomposition, its
ground-to-click block is

\[
 P_{\rm click}U_gP_g=-iW\sin(g|K|),
\]

which gives the two effects stated above.

## Pointer-only recovery of the selected transition

For normalized selected packet states \(u\) in the ground sector and \(v\)
in the charge-matched click sector, prepare

\[
 \Psi_\theta={|g\rangle\otimes u+
 e^{i\theta}|{\rm click}\rangle\otimes v\over\sqrt2}
\]

and measure only the finite pointer projection \(P_{\rm click}\).  Set

\[
 p_\theta(g)=
 \langle\Psi_\theta,U_g^*P_{\rm click}U_g\Psi_\theta\rangle,
 \qquad
 C_\theta(g)={p_\theta(g)-p_{\theta+\pi}(g)\over2}.
\]

Differentiating at the origin gives the exact formula in the Result.  The
opposite pointer phase cancels the common baseline, rather than requiring the
field vacuum to be a local measurement outcome.  Optimizing the calibration
phase recovers the absolute selected matrix element.  Its order-\(\lambda^4\)
coefficient therefore yields the certified order-\(\lambda^8\) square.

The functional calculus is exact in \(g\).  The BT coupling expansion remains
perturbative in \(\lambda\): the \(\lambda^{10}\) and higher response terms
have not been bounded.

## Claim boundary

The theorem constructs a selected positive auxiliary local measurement
coupling.  It does not establish:

- essential self-adjointness of either charged Wick branch separately;
- equality of the positive Hilbert Born rule and the public generalized
  Krein Born rule on arbitrary processes;
- an exactly vacuum-dark nonzero local effect;
- an interacting BT Haag--Kastler net or time-ordered detector evolution;
- positivity or convergence of the \(\lambda^{10}\) and higher corrections;
- the scalar-projector pushforward or general Eq. (19);
- a M\o ller, LSZ or all-time scattering operator;
- gravity, BV--BRST transfer, QME restoration or residual transfer;
- anything `LORENTZIAN-CAUSAL`; or
- literature priority.

The bounded functional-calculus unitary is a local operation in the selected
free carrier.  It is not presented as evolution generated by the interacting
public BT Hamiltonian.

## Sources and self-contained content

operator-valued-distribution treatment of Brunetti, Fredenhagen and K\"ohler,
*Commun. Math. Phys.* **180** (1996) 633--652,

*J. Math. Phys.* **53** (2012) 042502,

boundary around essential self-adjointness of derivative Wick polynomials.

Neither source supplies the pointer theorem.  The column closability proof,
self-adjoint block resolvent, total charge/parity balance, local functional
calculus, phase-reversal tangent and exact rational witness are derived and
verified in this repository.  No priority claim is made.
