## Exact carrier

The calculation uses the finite Laurent group algebra

\[
\mathcal A=\mathbb Q[t,t^{-1}],\qquad q(t^n)=n.
\]

Only finite supports are admitted. The BT/Krein adjoint preserves boost charge,

\[
(t^n)^\dagger=t^n,
\]

and the invariant trace selects charge zero,

\[
\tau\!\left(\sum_n a_nt^n\right)=a_0.
\]

Thus

\[
\mathcal N_-=\bigoplus_{q<0}\mathcal A_q
\]

is trace-null. It is not a radical of the full Laurent algebra, since

\[
\tau\big((t^{-1})^\dagger t^{+1}\big)=\tau(1)=1.
\]

It is a relative radical only after restricting the image to charges
$q\le0$. For populated fixture elements $B\in\mathcal A_0$ and
$C\in\mathcal N_-$,

\[
\tau(B^\dagger C)=\tau(C^\dagger B)=\tau(C^\dagger C)=0,
\]

so

\[
\tau((B+C)^\dagger(B+C))=\tau(B^\dagger B).
\]

The producer uses three independent negative charges in $C$; the result is
not obtained by testing a single monomial.

## Inclusive completeness criterion

For a kernel sandwich

\[
\Phi_{L,R}(X)=L^\dagger X R,
\qquad q(L)=q_L,\quad q(R)=q_R,
\]

the BT adjoint gives

\[
q(\Phi_{L,R}(X))=q(X)+q_L+q_R.
\]

Write $r=q_L+q_R$.

- If $r\le0$, every $q(X)<0$ remains negative.
- If $r>0$, the populated witness $X=t^{-r}$ maps to charge zero and has
  nonzero trace.

The producer exhausts all 49 pairs $(q_L,q_R)\in[-3,3]^2$ and independently
checks inputs of charges $-6,\ldots,-1$. The verifier rederives the same
criterion without importing the producer.

For $m=0,\ldots,5$ unresolved Eq. (20) factors, the expanded kernel has
$2^m$ terms. Every term has shift zero, and its action on the generic
negative element is exactly multiplication by $2^m$. A six-multiplicity
fixture with mixed positive and negative rational weights remains a nonzero

not physical real or virtual coefficients.
