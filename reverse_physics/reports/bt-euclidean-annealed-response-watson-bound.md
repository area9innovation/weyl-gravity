## Exact bound for the Watson moment

Let \(A_n\) be the number of closed \(2n\)-step nearest-neighbor walks on
\(\mathbb Z^4\).  The continuous-time heat-kernel expansion gives

\[
 W_4=\frac18\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{A_n}{64^n}.
\]

The coefficients can be reconstructed directly from

\[
 A_n={2n\choose n}
 \sum_{k=0}^n {n\choose k}^2
 {2k\choose k}{2n-2k\choose n-k},
\]

or generated by the exact recurrence

\[
\begin{aligned}
 n^4A_n={}&4(2n-1)^2(5n^2-5n+2)A_{n-1}\\
 &-256(n-1)^2(2n-3)(2n-1)A_{n-2},
\end{aligned}
\]

with \(A_0=1\), \(A_1=8\).  The producer uses the recurrence; the verifier
independently evaluates the multinomial formula through \(n=2500\).

For the tail, let

\[
 \phi(k)=\frac14\sum_{\mu=1}^4\cos k_\mu.
\]

The return probability is

\[
 p_{2n}(0)=\frac1{(2\pi)^4}
 \int_{[-\pi,\pi]^4}\phi(k)^{2n}dk.
\]

The map \(k\mapsto k+(\pi,\pi,\pi,\pi)\) exchanges the positive and
negative regions of \(\phi\).  On the positive region,

\[
 1-\phi(k)
 \geq\frac{|k|^2}{2\pi^2},
 \qquad
 \phi(k)^{2n}\leq e^{-n|k|^2/\pi^2}.
\]

Extending to \(\mathbb R^4\) and using \(\pi<22/7\) gives the rational bound

\[
 p_{2n}(0)\leq\frac{\pi^2}{8n^2}
 <\frac{121}{98n^2}.
\]

Since \(\sum_{n>N}n^{-2}\leq N^{-1}\), the discarded contribution after
\(N=2500\) is at most

\[
 \frac{121}{784N}.
\]

The exact partial fraction has 4,511 decimal digits in both numerator and
denominator and canonical SHA-256

```text
0481e8a91c76f8aa8147f9830d639c4947d4b47a214472850f678c9e620c3cf1
```

The partial fraction plus the displayed tail is less than
\(15499/100000<31/200\).  No floating-point value enters this comparison.

The Bessel representation and return recurrence are standard hypercubic
lattice-Green-function technology; see
[Guttmann, *Lattice Green functions in all dimensions*, 2010](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.1435).

## Exact bound for the derivative moment

Write \(G(r)\) for the four-dimensional massless lattice Green function.
Since

\[
 f'(t)=2e^{-2t}\bigl(I_1(2t)-I_0(2t)\bigr),
\]

expansion of the square gives

\[
 I_4=4\bigl(G(0)-2G(e_1)+G(e_1+e_2)\bigr).
\]

The Green equation says \(G(0)-G(e_1)=1/8\), hence

\[
 \boxed{I_4=1-4\bigl(G(0)-G(e_1+e_2)\bigr).}
\]

Let \(B_n\) count \(2n\)-step walks ending at \(e_1+e_2\).  Then

\[
 G(0)-G(e_1+e_2)
 =\frac18\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{A_n-B_n}{64^n}.
\]

Every summand is nonnegative.  Indeed, for the \(n\)-step transition
probability \(p_n\),

\[
 p_{2n}(r)=\langle p_n,\tau_rp_n\rangle
 \leq\|p_n\|_2^2=p_{2n}(0)
\]

by Cauchy--Schwarz.  A partial sum is therefore a rigorous lower bound with
no tail estimate required.

The endpoint coefficients have the exact formula

\[
 B_n=(2n)!\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}
 \frac{{2k+2\choose k+1}}{k!(k+2)!}
 \frac{{2n-2-2k\choose n-1-k}}{((n-1-k)!)^2}.
\]

Only the terms through \(n=10\) are needed:

\[
 \frac18\sum_{n=0}^{10}\frac{A_n-B_n}{64^n}
 =\frac{2558322539133673}{18014398509481984}
 >\frac{71}{500}.
\]

It follows immediately that

\[
 I_4
 <1-4\frac{71}{500}
 =\frac{54}{125}.
\]

The verifier reconstructs \(B_n\) through a direct four-coordinate
composition rather than the producer's paired-coordinate convolution.
