## Result

The complete perfect-square five-point tree amplitude has an exact and
unexpected cancellation. In the square-free external-virtuality algebra,

\[
J_5=\mathbb Q(s_0,\ldots,s_4)[x_1,\ldots,x_5]/(x_i^2),
\]

the normalized amplitude

\[
A_5=\frac{M_5}{8\lambda^3}
\]

has no terms of total virtuality degree zero, one, or two:

\[
A_5\in (x_1,\ldots,x_5)^3.
\]

All 10 degree-three slots, all five degree-four slots, and the single
degree-five slot are generically nonzero. Thus the full 32-slot jet has the
support split

| total degree | slots | state |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 1 | identically zero |
| 1 | 5 | identically zero |
| 2 | 10 | identically zero |
| 3 | 10 | nonzero rational functions |
| 4 | 5 | nonzero rational functions |
| 5 | 1 | nonzero rational function |

The immediate amplitude-side consequence is

\[
[x_1x_2x_3x_4x_5]|A_5|^2=0,
\]

because every product of two terms of degree at least three has degree at
least six, while the BT fivefold projector can select only degree five. This
remains zero after multiplication by any kinematic prefactor analytic in the
five external virtualities at a nonexceptional regulated point.

Away from channel poles the tree coefficients are real rational functions of
real invariants, so conjugation fixes `A₅` in this algebraic calculation. The
`iε` boundary value re-enters precisely at the distributional gate left open
below.

This does **not** yet prove that the physical integrated `2→3` probability is
zero. The leading nonzero jet coefficients have soft and collinear poles, so
mass differentiation, phase-space integration, and regulator removal may not
be interchanged until a distributional five-body projector is constructed.

## Published input and diagram enumeration

Bateman--Turok Appendix B gives

\[
V_3(p_1,p_2,p_3)=-2i\lambda
\sum_{\rm cyc}p_1^2(p_2\!\cdot p_3),
\]

\[
V_4(p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4)=-4i\lambda^2
\bigl[(p_1\!\cdot p_2)(p_3\!\cdot p_4)+\text{two pairings}\bigr],
\]

and propagator `-i/(K²+iε)²`. At order `λ³`, every labeled five-point tree
has one of two shapes:

1. one cubic and one quartic vertex joined by one propagator: choose the two
   external cubic legs, giving `C(5,2)=10` graphs;
2. three cubic vertices in a chain: choose the central external leg and an
   unordered pairing of the other four, giving `5×3=15` graphs.

The Feynman factors fix their relative sign:

\[
(-2i)(-4i)(-i)=+8i,
\qquad
(-2i)^3(-i)^2=-8i.
\]

Therefore

\[
A_5=\sum_{10\;V_3V_4}\frac{F_3F_4}{(K^2)^2}
-\sum_{15\;V_3^3}\frac{F_3F_3F_3}{(K_1^2)^2(K_2^2)^2}.
\]

The producer lists all 25 labeled trees in the certificate. No graph is
generated by a library diagram enumerator or inferred from the desired
cancellation.

## Exact kinematic chart

All momenta are incoming and obey `k₁+...+k₅=0`. Define

\[
x_i=k_i^2,
\qquad
s_i=(k_i+k_{i+1})^2
\]

with cyclic indices. Momentum conservation gives the nonadjacent channels:

\[
(k_i+k_{i+2})^2
=s_{i+3}-s_i-s_{i+1}+x_i+x_{i+1}+x_{i+2}.
\]

The calculation holds `s₀,...,s₄` fixed while expanding in the five `x_i`.
This is a valid local invariant chart away from channel zeros, but it is not
asserted to be Bateman--Turok's missing five-body phase-space reduction.

The absence through degree two is stronger than a numerical observation. The
producer constructs the coefficients in the canonical rational-function
field `Q(s₀,...,s₄)`; all 16 low-degree entries reduce to the exact zero field
element. The full computation then constructs the remaining 16 functions.
Their canonical strings range from 3,893 to 332,397 characters, so the
certificate stores content hashes and lengths rather than embedding almost a
megabyte of redundant expanded algebra. Three complete 32-slot exact rational
fixtures are embedded as independently reproducible concrete values.

## Soft and collinear faces

The certificate evaluates three exact rational-function trajectories in a
regulator parameter `ε`. A valuation `-p` means a pole `ε⁻ᵖ`.

| declared face | degree 3 | degree 4 | degree 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| adjacent channel `s₀→0` | `-1…0` | `-2…-1` | `-2` |
| nonadjacent channel `(k₀+k₂)²→0` | `-1…0` | `-2` | `-3` |
| external leg 5 soft | `-1` | `-2` | `-3` |

Two facts coexist:

1. the first nonzero amplitude coefficients have at worst simple poles on the
   declared leading faces; and
2. the square-free degree-five coefficient of `|A₅|²` is identically zero for
   every nonzero `ε`.

The second statement does not automatically survive as a distribution at
`ε=0`. A phase-space delta function, Jacobian, or differentiated integration
boundary can be nonanalytic in the same external virtualities. The familiar
operation “differentiate the integrand, then integrate” therefore needs a
proof in this dipole setting.

This is the first precise place where Bateman--Turok's stated collinear
asymptotic-state obstacle enters the calculation rather than the prose.

## Interpretation

The conventional on-shell five-point amplitude itself vanishes. More strongly,
the amplitude vanishes to third order transverse to the all-massless locus.
This is compatible with Bateman--Turok's claim that tree probabilities are
nonnegative: zero is allowed.

For the NLO programme, the result removes the naive pointwise real-emission
term. There are now two possible outcomes:

- the regulated five-body projector preserves the zero, in which case the
  first NLO probability has no ordinary `2→3` real term and the four-leg loop
  jet must be finite or canceled by a different asymptotic-state mechanism; or
- distributional collinear boundary terms survive despite pointwise zero, in
  which case those terms—not an ordinary on-shell amplitude—are the real side
  of the cancellation.

Both are concrete and testable directions. The present certificate decides
neither without the missing phase-space object.
