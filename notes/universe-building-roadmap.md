# Universe-building roadmap and team activation gates

## Purpose

The programme now has enough exact infrastructure to organize its next work in
terms of recognizable physical phenomena: clocks and redshift, gravitational
waves, particles, black holes, galaxies, and cosmology.  This roadmap does not
promote any of those phenomena before their mathematical gates pass.  It says
which present calculation unlocks which later question, and when team capacity
should move.

The shared scientific question remains:

> Is cylinder-time translation `D` genuinely gauge after clocks,
> interactions, quantization, or boundaries are added?

The universe-building language is a map of consequences of that question, not
a replacement for its charge, Cartan, causal, and pairing tests.

## The relative Einstein--Weyl spine

The common organizing object for the next phase is the relation between the
Einstein and Weyl gauge complexes.  It is indexed by the full claim tuple

\[
(\text{theory},\text{background},\text{generator},\text{phase space},
  \text{boundaries},\text{lifecycle}),
\]

and must not be promoted from one setting to another without a certified map.
The preferred linear statement is the derived triangle

\[
\mathcal E_{\rm Einstein}
 \xrightarrow{\ \iota\ }
\mathcal E_{\rm Weyl}
 \longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{\rm extra}:=\operatorname{Cone}(\iota)
 \longrightarrow
\mathcal E_{\rm Einstein}[1].
\]

A strict short exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal E_{\rm Einstein}
 \xrightarrow{\ \iota\ }
\mathcal E_{\rm Weyl}
 \longrightarrow\mathcal E_{\rm extra}
\longrightarrow0
\]

may be claimed only after \(\iota\) is an injective off-shell chain map on the
declared fields, ghosts, antifields, identities, global modes, charge fibres,
and boundary domain.  Until then each setting receives exactly one of four
fail-closed dispositions:

1. `STRICT_SHORT_EXACT_SEQUENCE`;
2. `DERIVED_COFIBER_TRIANGLE`;
3. `ONSHELL_MAP_ONLY`;
4. `MAP_OBSTRUCTED`, with an explicit normalized defect.

The word *extra* therefore names a relative cofiber, not a universal new
particle species.  Its cohomology and physical interpretation may change with
background, boundaries, charge sector, and quotient.

### Structures carried by the spine

The linear certificate must keep three forms distinct:

\[
\Omega_{\rm Einstein},\qquad
\iota^*\Omega_{\rm Weyl},\qquad
\Omega_{\rm extra}.
\]

Equality, proportionality, degeneracy, signature, and boundary defects are
separate outcomes.  A reduced-Hessian form is not silently substituted for an
action-derived Lee--Wald or shifted BV form.

The first nonlinear obstruction is the cofiber class

\[
\mathfrak O_2(x,y)=
\big[\pi_{\rm extra}
q_2^{\rm Weyl}(\iota x,\iota y)\big]
\in H(\mathcal C_{\rm extra}),
\]

or its precise cokernel analogue when only an on-shell map exists.  It must be
reported as zero, exact with a displayed primitive, or nonzero with a witness.
At arity three the mixed \(q_2\) and \(q_3\) terms determine whether an
\(L_\infty\) morphism can correct the linear inclusion.  A graded splitting
\(L_{\rm Weyl}\simeq L_{\rm Einstein}\oplus L_{\rm extra}\) is not a direct
sum of interacting theories: the mixed brackets are the physics to compute.

Residual symmetries must act equivariantly on the triangle or leave a measured
defect.  Observables run contravariantly,

\[
\iota^*:\operatorname{Obs}(\mathcal E_{\rm Weyl})
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Obs}(\mathcal E_{\rm Einstein}),
\]

while observables that annihilate the Einstein image are candidate detectors
of the relative sector.  Relational clock observables must be placed in this
diagram rather than compared only at the level of solutions.

## Physical rails and their gates

### 1. Clocks, time dilation, and redshift

The healthy Berger clock makes this the first accessible observer-level rail.
Its next target is not merely a time-dependent field.  It is a relational,
gauge-invariant comparison of two clock or signal readings.

Activation gates:

1. authoritative `q2` domain;
2. retained causal Green homotopy;
3. interacting Cartan result on the observable's domain;
4. construction of a physical metric/clock observable;
5. a null or characteristic signal comparison giving a frequency ratio.

A passed redshift fixture would show that quotienting total (D) does not
erase operational evolution.  It would not yet establish Solar-System
phenomenology.

The first fail-closed `G0` preparation is now recorded by
`BERGER_RELATIONAL_REDSHIFT_PREFLIGHT`.  It constructs the Weyl-invariant
clock metric, a one-chart complete-observable formula, and an exact
characteristic-probe fixture with `1+z=2`.  This is a local kinematic
frequency comparison.  The full rail remains open on spatial endpoint
dressing, an actual dynamical signal solution, reduced brackets/pairing,
global winding and multiple-path control, and higher-bracket compatibility.

### 2. Gravitational waves

The compact Einstein--Maxwell harmonic quotient supplies exact linear wave
directions, and the standard radiative modes now have a positive covariantly
matched pairing.  To complete their physical interpretation:

1. close the global `ell=0` and axial-twist symplectic sectors;
2. compute the Weyl--Maxwell pullback;
3. separate and norm any extra fourth-order branches;
4. run quadratic extension/Taub tests;
5. construct a relational detector or geodesic-deviation observable;
6. compute boundary flux only after an asymptotic phase space exists.

The generic axial part of step 3 is now complete before residual quotient.
Step 4 has its first full extra-sector result: the fixed-charge `ell=2,k=0`
two-dimensional Taub form is negative definite. Step 5 now has an exact
linear symplectic coefficient detector that vanishes on the Einstein image;
its final residual, relational, and causal realization remains open. Polar
extra branches and generic momentum/harmonic Taub blocks are still required.

Compact normal modes are a wave laboratory.  They are not yet an astrophysical
waveform or an asymptotic graviton state.

### 3. Interactions and stability

This is the central junction rather than a separate destination:

1. complete `q2`;
2. certify the arity-two interacting Cartan identity or obstruction;
3. test the resonant `+lambda,-lambda -> 0` channel;
4. compute the first nontrivial transferred physical vertex;
5. determine whether a negative physical direction is created.

A no-go at any one of these stages is a publishable boundary of the model.  A
pass activates observer and wave calculations on the certified domain only.

### 4. Particles and gravitons

Particle language requires more than a classical normal-mode solution.  The
minimum activation sequence is:

1. Lorentzian Green/Hadamard framework;
2. renormalized observable algebra and restored QME, or a precise anomaly;
3. physical BRST/BV cohomology and pairing;
4. a state or asymptotic structure that supplies positive-frequency data;
5. interaction amplitudes or detector response.

The existing centered classes `[W_+^2]` and `[W_-^2]` remain deformation or
vertex classes; they must not be renamed one-particle gravitons.

### 5. Boundaries, scattering, and black holes

Black holes and scattering require a new phase-space theorem, not merely a new
background substituted into cylinder formulas.

Activation gates:

1. a Lorentzian exterior or asymptotic Bach complex with operator domains;
2. boundary-preserving generators and differentiable charges;
3. horizon and infinity flux balance;
4. classification of Einstein and extra Weyl branches;
5. causal boundary conditions and physical symplectic signs;
6. only then, perturbations, stability, thermodynamics, or Hawking questions.

An exact black-hole metric would establish existence, not physical selection,
stability, entropy, or radiation.

### 6. Galaxies and the dark-matter question

Mannheim-type phenomenology becomes relevant after the static weak-field and
boundary sectors are under control.

Activation gates:

1. identify the physical metric seen by clocks and matter;
2. derive the static weak-field potential from certified field equations and
   admissible boundary data;
3. determine which integration constants are charges, environmental boundary
   data, or gauge;
4. derive lensing and orbital observables from the same solution;
5. fit galaxies only after those constants and priors have physical meaning;
6. compare with dark-matter predictions and cluster/cosmological constraints.

This rail can test whether modified conformal dynamics explains some effects
attributed to dark matter.  It must not announce that dark matter has been
removed on the basis of rotation curves alone.

### 7. Cosmology and dark energy

The cosmological rail needs homogeneous solutions beyond the present clock
fixture and their perturbations.

Activation gates:

1. classify homogeneous/isotropic solutions with matter and clock sectors;
2. compute the physical expansion and redshift observables;
3. establish stability and causal perturbations;
4. derive distance--redshift and growth equations;
5. determine whether acceleration is dynamical, boundary-selected, or a
   parameter choice;
6. compare jointly with supernova, CMB, BAO, and structure growth data.

An accelerating solution is not by itself a theory of observed dark energy.
