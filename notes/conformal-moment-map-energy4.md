## Why the Taub--moment-map relation is block independent

The extension from the two curvature seeds is not an interpolation across
mode labels.  It is the quadratic Noether identity.  Let
`R_epsilon(g)` be the Diff `x` Weyl transformation generated by a background
reducibility `epsilon`, so that

\[
R_\epsilon(\bar g)=0.
\]

On a closed cylinder slice the covariant Hamiltonian identity has no spatial
surface term:

\[
\delta H_\epsilon
=\Omega(\delta g,R_\epsilon(g)).
\]

Expanding around the background gives, on the linearized solution space,

\[
dH_\epsilon^{(2)}[\delta h]
=\Omega(\delta h,R_\epsilon^{(1)}h).
\]

The same second-order Noether identity, written in equation-of-motion form,
is the Taub functional obtained by contracting
`E^(2)[h_1,h_2]` with the reducibility.  Its quadratic polarization is
therefore the moment map of the **same** linear conformal action.  This
statement is independent of which `E/A/L` block is used.

C2f-N fixes the convention factors that this abstract identity leaves easy
to misplace: the reduced action is `-1/2` of the canonical Weyl action
modulo Euler, the selected spherical CK component has phase
`-i sqrt(2)/pi`, and C2a stores the mixed polarization rather than the full
quadratic kernel.  The two directly evaluated, representation-inequivalent
seeds verify those factors.  Once fixed, the Noether identity determines the
remaining low-energy blocks.  Direct curvature calculations of them are
therefore regressions of the field implementation, not additional logical
inputs to the moment-map theorem.

## Exact domain and cutoff boundary

The 132-dimensional object is a **moment-map jet**, not a finite
representation of `SO(4,2)`.  A noncompact lowest-weight module cannot be
cut off at energy four.  The buffer is large enough that every ordering in

\[
[K^-_M,K^+_N]=2\delta_{MN}D+2R_{MN}
\]

is complete on source energies two and three.  All sixteen brackets close
there for both chiralities.  The energy-four source block is intentionally
not claimed: `K^-K^+` passes through energy five, whose blocks are outside
the buffer.

This distinction also blocks a premature global-BRST cohomology
calculation.  The algebra acts exactly on the low-energy interior, but the
differential's image reaches the buffer boundary.  Cohomology requires the
energy-graded module to be extended far enough that both the kernel and the
image at the requested degree are complete.

## The old four-mode cancellation

C2b found a vector whose values vanish for the two seeded proper-conformal
multiplets.  With the newly completed proper-CK blocks, that cancellation
still holds for all four lowering components.  It is nevertheless not on
the full conformal zero locus.  The compact kernels give exactly

\[
\mu_D=-6,
\qquad
\mu_{R_z}=-3.
\]

Thus the earlier cancellation was correctly described as a point on the
**seeded** Taub-constraint zero locus.  It is not a globally conformal
physical configuration.  This is the first concrete effect of adding the
seven compact components rather than reasoning only from the mixed Taub
seeds.
