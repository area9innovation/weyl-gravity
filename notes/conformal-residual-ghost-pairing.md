# C2g-G: residual conformal-ghost pairing

## Primary-source construction

Hamada's residual BRST construction on `R x S^3` expands the conformal-Killing
ghost in fifteen modes:

\[
c,\qquad c_{MN},\qquad c_M,\qquad c_M^\dagger,
\]

with one time-translation mode, six independent rotation modes, and four
proper-conformal lowering/raising pairs. See
[Hamada, arXiv:1202.4538](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.4538), Secs. 3, 5, and 6.
The paper constructs a Hermitian nilpotent residual BRST charge and chooses the
ghost Fock vacuum

\[
|v\rangle_{\rm gh}=\prod_M c_M|0\rangle_{\rm gh}.
\]

It is annihilated by `c_M` and `b_M`, and the ghost Hamiltonian gives

\[
H^{\rm gh}|v\rangle_{\rm gh}=-4|v\rangle_{\rm gh}.
\]

The seven zero modes make the naive overlap vanish. Hamada inserts

\[
\vartheta=i\,c\prod c_{MN},
\qquad \vartheta^\dagger=\vartheta,
\]

and fixes the orientation by

\[
{}_{\rm gh}\langle0|\prod_Mc_M^\dagger\,\vartheta
\prod_Mc_M|0\rangle_{\rm gh}=1.
\]

The factor `i` is forced by Hermiticity: the product of seven Hermitian odd
zero modes changes sign when its order is reversed by the adjoint.

## Exact exterior realization

`symbolic/verify_conformal_residual_ghost_pairing.py` realizes the fifteen
ghost coordinates as an exterior algebra. Use a real six-dimensional basis
for the rotation ghosts and order the coordinates as

\[
(c_M)_{4},\quad(c_M^\dagger)_{4},\quad c,\quad(c_{MN})_{6}.
\]

On the eight dynamic coordinates define

\[
(\alpha,\beta)_\vartheta
=-i\,[\alpha^\dagger\wedge\vartheta\wedge\beta]_{\rm top}.
\]

The phase is the orientation convention equivalent to Hamada's normalized
vacuum expectation. The executable proves exactly:

\[
\vartheta^\dagger=\vartheta,
\qquad (v,v)_\vartheta=1,
\qquad H_{\rm gh}v=-4v.
\]

The complete dynamic exterior space has dimension `2^8=256`. Its Gram matrix
is a Hermitian involution with signature

\[
(128,128).
\]

The insertion occupies seven of the fifteen coordinates. Therefore a nonzero
pairing between dynamic degrees `p` and `q` requires

\[
p+q=8.
\]

After centering ghost number by

\[
N_{\rm gh}^{\rm cen}=p-4,
\]

the paired degrees are opposite. The centered degree-zero sector has dimension
`binom(8,4)=70` and exact signature

\[
(35,35).
\]

Thus the residual insertion supplies the centering datum missing from the
algebra-only CE rail. It does not make the whole ghost sector positive; it
selects a unit positive norm for the particular residual vacuum.

There is also an important relative/absolute distinction. With the insertion
held fixed, any state already containing `c` or a rotation ghost pairs to zero
because that coordinate is repeated against `vartheta`. Hence on the full
`2^15` exterior algebra the inserted form has rank exactly `2^8` and radical
dimension

\[
2^{15}-2^8=32512.
\]

It is nondegenerate only after the seven zero-mode directions have been put in
the standard relative polarization. It cannot by itself serve as the
nondegenerate Gram matrix of an absolute BRST complex.

## Incoming exact space at Weyl weight four

The two relative-primary candidates found in C2g-R lie in

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^2(E_2^+\oplus E_2^-)
\]

and have matter particle number two and compact matter energy four. In the
standard free relative complex, a ghost-number-minus-one preimage capable of
feeding the vacuum `product c_M` has three of the four `c_M` modes. Its ghost
energy is `-3`, so total compact degree zero forces its matter coefficient to
have energy three.

The only term that can fill the missing `c_M` and reach the residual-vacuum
ghost monomial is

\[
c_M Q_M^\dagger.
\]

Therefore the incoming matter image is generated by a conformal raising
operator acting on the pure-Weyl matter space of energy three. Two independent
arguments make its projection onto the Weyl-square kernel zero:

1. The free conformal generators are second quantizations of one-particle
   generators and preserve matter particle number. A two-particle preimage
   would be required, but the minimum two-particle pure-Weyl energy is
   `2+2=4`, not three.
2. A scalar in
   `(1/2,1/2) tensor V_3` requires an energy-three
   `(1/2,1/2)` matter irrep. The actual energy-three irreps are
   \[
   (5/2,1/2),\ (3/2,1/2),\ (1/2,5/2),\ (1/2,3/2),
   \]
   so no scalar descendant exists.

Consequently, in the particle-number-two sector the entire incoming absolute
global cochain space vanishes:

\[
C^3_{\delta=0,N=2}=0.
\]

This agrees with, and is strengthened by, the independent cutoff-complete
C2g-N global-BRST calculation. That calculation constructs the outgoing map,
proves its exact rank is `53` on the `55`-dimensional middle space, and hence
establishes

\[
\boxed{
H^4_{\delta=0,N=2}(\mathfrak{so}(4,2),\mathcal F_{\rm Weyl})
=\operatorname{span}\{|W_+^2\rangle,|W_-^2\rangle\}.
}
\]

Equivalently, in relative language,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{im}Q_{\rm rel}^{(-1\to0)}
\cap\operatorname{span}\{|W_+^2\rangle,|W_-^2\rangle\}=0
}
\]

for the standard **free residual pure-Weyl relative complex**. Thus the two
relative primaries and the two absolute-global classes agree in this lowest
particle-number block. The positive matter Gram `I_2` from C2g-R, multiplied
by the unit residual-vacuum ghost overlap, survives the incoming-exact test.

This last sentence is a statement about the standard relative polarization,
not yet an absolute physical-pairing theorem. The fixed `vartheta` insertion
is degenerate on the full absolute ghost exterior algebra, and the comparison
with the local Diff `x` Weyl BV complex has not been derived.

## Exact global-only class Gram

There is nevertheless a sharp bridge one can make inside the free residual
global complex. C2g-N proves both

\[
C^3_{\delta=0,N=2}=0
\]

and

\[
H^4_{\delta=0,N=2}
=\operatorname{span}\{|W_+^2\rangle,|W_-^2\rangle\}.
\]

C2g-R fixes the normalized matter Fock form on these representatives to
`I_2`, while the present exterior certificate gives

\[
(v,v)_\vartheta=1.
\]

Since the incoming global space is empty, no representative ambiguity can
alter the restriction. The tensor-product form on this global-only residual
cohomology sector is therefore exactly

\[
\boxed{
G^{\rm global}_{H^4,N=2}
=(v,v)_\vartheta
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
=I_2.
}
\]

This is a positive **global-only residual cohomology sector**, conditional on
the pure-Weyl local gauge fixing inducing Hamada's residual zero-mode split
and polarization. It is not yet the full physical pure-Weyl cohomology: the
fixed insertion is degenerate on the absolute 15-ghost exterior algebra, and
the local Diff `x` Weyl BV differential, contractible sectors, anomaly
conditions, and relative-to-absolute comparison have not been derived.

## What transfers to pure Weyl gravity

The following ingredients depend only on the conformal-Killing residual ghost
algebra, its standard cylinder polarization, and the already reconstructed
free pure-Weyl conformal module:

- the `4+4+1+6` residual-ghost decomposition;
- the exterior anticommutation algebra and adjoint exchanging `c_M` with
  `c_M^dagger`;
- the `product c_M` vacuum, its ghost energy `-4`, and the centered insertion
  algebra;
- the relative conditions selecting compact-weight-four primary scalars;
- the empty particle-number-two incoming global space at weight four and,
  using C2g-N, the resulting two global Weyl-square classes.

These statements transfer only **conditionally** on the pure-Weyl local gauge
fixing producing the same residual zero-mode polarization without an anomaly.

## What does not transfer from Hamada's broader model

Hamada's paper contains the Riegert-Wess-Zumino conformal mode and uses it
essentially in several later conclusions. The following cannot be imported
into pure Weyl gravity without a separate local-BV derivation:

- nilpotency and Hermiticity of the complete quantum pure-Weyl BRST operator;
- cancellation of conformal/diffeomorphism anomalies and normal-ordering
  shifts;
- the Riegert background charge, the states labelled by `gamma`, and their
  dual-charge adjoints;
- the full state-operator correspondence and the claim that the absolute
  physical cohomology consists only of real weight-four primary scalars;
- nondegeneracy or positivity of the absolute pure-Weyl physical pairing,
  despite the exact two-dimensional global-only cohomology;
- survival of particle-number conservation and the incoming-exact zero after
  nonlinear interaction corrections to the BRST charge.

The present result is therefore certificate-grade for the finite residual
ghost algebra and the free relative Weyl-square classes, while remaining
fail-closed on absolute local-plus-global pure-Weyl BV cohomology.
