\begin{abstract}
We construct causal chain homotopies for the complete free pure-Weyl
BV--BFV complex on the conformal cylinder
$M=\R\times S^3$.  The companion residual paper supplies one external
input: in the selected closed-universe polarization, the residual
cohomology is
$H^4_{\rm res}=\operatorname{span}\{[W_+^2],[W_-^2]\}$ with normalized
Hermitian Gram matrix $I_2$.  We do not repeat that Chevalley--Eilenberg
calculation here.

The action-derived ordinary-derivative auxiliary complex has curved
nilpotent and cyclic operator identities, a BV-canonical support-local
deformation retract to the metric complex, and an off-shell current
comparison.  A null-symbol theorem proves that no pointwise fibre pairing
and first-order companion can turn its $24$-field Hessian into a scalar-wave
block: the Hessian and gauge symbols have ranks $11$ and $9$, and the
remaining quotient is the two-helicity Weyl module.  We therefore adjoin
curvature rather than project onto helicity.  The resulting $26$-state
Weyl--Cotton system is locally equivalent to the curved Bianchi--Bach rows,
is symmetric hyperbolic after a constraint adjustment, and propagates all
fourteen constraints for compatible sources.

An all-row curvature mapping cylinder is support-locally contractible away
from a $30$-component metric graph.  Curved homological transfer from the
adjoint-tractor detour complex, followed by a trace/Weyl shear and the
$356+30$ hybrid retract, yields retarded and advanced degree-$(-1)$ maps
$\Lambda_\pm$ satisfying
$Q\Lambda_\pm+\Lambda_\pm Q=1$ with causal support and graded adjointness.
Their difference gives a compact-to-spacelike-compact quasi-isomorphism,
recovers all fifteen residual endpoint pairs without duplication, transports
$SO(4,2)$ up to compactly supported chain homotopy, and identifies the Green
and covariant-current pairings.  Consequently
$H^4_{\rm cov}=\operatorname{span}\{[W_+^2],[W_-^2]\}$ and
$G_{\rm cov}=I_2$.  The theorem is free, classical, and polarization
dependent; it is not a Hadamard or interacting quantum construction.  In
particular, causal transport does not itself decide whether compact time
translation is gauge on an unrestricted phase space: the residual conclusion
uses the companion paper's explicit Taub-zero derived sector.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and theorem boundary}\label{sec:intro}

Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cylinder}
 M=\R\times S^3,
 \qquad \bar g=-dt^2+d\Omega_3^2,
\end{equation}
with unit cylinder radius and Lorentzian signature $(-,+,+,+)$.  We study
the free classical BV complex of the Weyl-square action.  Compactly
supported, spacelike-compact, and unrestricted smooth sections are denoted
by $\Gamma_c$, $\Gamma_{sc}$, and $\Gamma^\infty$.

The analytic target is not a scalar wave equation for every off-shell
component.  It is a pair of degree-$(-1)$ maps
\begin{equation}\label{eq:causal-target}
 \Lambda_\pm:\Gamma_c(\cC_{\rm prol})
 \longrightarrow \Gamma^\infty(\cC_{\rm prol})[-1]
\end{equation}
such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:causal-identity}
 Q\Lambda_\pm+\Lambda_\pm Q=\id,
 \qquad
 \supp(\Lambda_\pm f)\subseteq J^\pm(\supp f).
\end{equation}
The Green-hyperbolic-complex formalism turns this identity into a causal
quasi-isomorphism \cite{GreenHyperbolic}.  We realize it directly by
finite-order homological transfer from known hyperbolic blocks; no single
scalar principal symbol on the original field bundle is required.

\begin{theorem}[Residual input from Paper A]\label{thm:paper-A-input}
For the selected closed-cylinder BV--BFV polarization, in which all fifteen
residual conformal generators are gauged, the centered residual complex has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:paper-A-result}
 H^4_{\rm res}
 =\operatorname{span}\{[W_+^2],[W_-^2]\},
 \qquad \operatorname{sig}G_{\rm res}=(2,0),
 \qquad G_{\rm res}=I_2.
\end{equation}
The underlying one-particle curvature form has cylinder signs

Paper A proves \eqref{eq:paper-A-result} by Cartan reduction and an exact

\subsection{Main result}

\begin{theorem}[Covariant causal BV--BFV transport]\label{thm:main}
The complete free pure-Weyl BV complex on \eqref{eq:cylinder} admits a
support-local curvature prolongation and maps \eqref{eq:causal-target}
satisfying \eqref{eq:causal-identity} and
$\Lambda_+^\sharp=\Lambda_-$.  Their causal difference induces a
pairing-compatible quasi-isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-chain}
 \Gamma_c(\cC_{\rm met})[1]
 \simeq \Gamma_c(\cC_{\rm aux})[1]
 \simeq \Gamma_c(\cC_{\rm prol})[1]
 \simeq \Gamma_{sc}(\cC_{\rm prol})
 \simeq \cC_\Sigma
 \simeq \W_+\oplus\W_-
\end{equation}
with the fifteen residual endpoint pairs.  The last identification is
$SO(4,2)$-equivariant on cohomology.  Transporting
Theorem~\ref{thm:paper-A-input} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-H4}
 \boxed{
 H^4_{\rm cov}=\operatorname{span}\{[W_+^2],[W_-^2]\},

\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\section{Curved auxiliary complex, local retract, and current}
\label{sec:auxiliary}

An ordinary-derivative realization of conformal gravity uses a trace-free
metric perturbation $h$, an auxiliary symmetric tensor $f$, a Stueckelberg
one-form $v$, and nine Diff/Weyl gauge parameters
\cite{MetsaevOrdinaryDerivative}.  Direct covariant variation of the action
and gauge fermion gives the Hessian $E_{\rm aux}$, gauge map $K_{\rm aux}$,
companion, and four-row BV operators $Q_{\rm aux},W_{\rm aux}$ and
$P_{\rm aux}$.

\begin{proposition}[Curved operator identities]\label{prop:operators}
In canonical covariant-derivative normal form, the complete curved operators
satisfy
\begin{align}
 E_{\rm aux}^\sharp&=E_{\rm aux},
 &E_{\rm aux}K_{\rm aux}&=0,
 &Q_{\rm aux}^2&=0,\label{eq:operator-first}\\
 Q_{\rm aux}W_{\rm aux}+W_{\rm aux}Q_{\rm aux}&=P_{\rm aux},
 &W_{\rm aux}^\sharp&=W_{\rm aux},
 &P_{\rm aux}^\sharp&=P_{\rm aux}.\label{eq:operator-second}
\end{align}
The coefficient comparison is exhaustive on all $630$ required cylinder
jets and globalizes by homogeneity.  Equations
\eqref{eq:operator-first}--\eqref{eq:operator-second} do not assert that the
field block of $P_{\rm aux}$ has scalar normally hyperbolic symbol.
\end{proposition}

The local BV-canonical variables include
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aux-shift}
 \eta=\xi_0-d\sigma,
 \qquad
 \widehat f=f-A_g^{-1}G^b(g,b),
 \qquad
 A_g^{-1}(s)=-2s+\frac23g\operatorname{tr}_g s.
\end{equation}
The same generating functional transforms fields and antifields, so the
pairing is not adjusted row by row.

\begin{proposition}[Support-local BV retract]\label{prop:aux-SDR}
There are finite-order differential maps
\begin{equation}
 i:\cC_{\rm met}\longrightarrow\cC_{\rm aux},
 \qquad p:\cC_{\rm aux}\longrightarrow\cC_{\rm met},
 \qquad k:\cC_{\rm aux}\longrightarrow\cC_{\rm aux}[-1]
\end{equation}
on every field, ghost, antifield, trace/Weyl, and nonminimal row, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aux-SDR}
 p i=\id,
 \qquad i p-\id=Q_{\rm aux}k+kQ_{\rm aux}.
\end{equation}
Each entry is a finite differential operator or a pointwise inverse; hence
the retract acts on $\Gamma_c$, $\Gamma_{sc}$, and $\Gamma^\infty$ and never
uses a conformal-Killing or transverse projector.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Off-shell current comparison]\label{prop:aux-current}
The action-derived presymplectic currents obey
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aux-current}
 i^*\omega_{\rm aux}-\omega_{\rm met}=d\beta+Q\gamma.
\end{equation}
On the closed Cauchy surface $S^3$, the spatial divergence integrates to
zero, so the induced Cauchy pairings agree on cohomology.  This is an
off-shell variational identity; it is not inferred from equal equations of
motion or mode normalization.
\end{proposition}

\section{Scoped analytic boundary theorems}\label{sec:nogo}

The following negative results prevent stronger but unnecessary analytic
claims.  They are stated only as invariant boundary lemmas; their exact
certificates and scope conditions are recorded in the computational
supplement.

\begin{proposition}[Scalar-wave witness no-go]\label{prop:scalar-no-go}
At a generic nonzero null covector $\zeta$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:null-ranks}
 \rank E_2(\zeta)=11,
 \qquad \rank K_1(\zeta)=9.
\end{equation}
For every nondegenerate pointwise fibre map $J$ and every first-order
companion $C$,
$\rank(J^{-1}E_2)=11$ and $\rank(K_1C_1)\leq9$.  Therefore
$J^{-1}E_2+K_1C_1$ cannot equal the vanishing null symbol of a scalar normally
hyperbolic $24$-field block.
\end{proposition}

The quotient left by \eqref{eq:null-ranks} is physical rather than missing
gauge freedom:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:helicity-quotient}
 \im(J_{\rm act}^{-1}E_2)/\im K_1
 \cong\cH_{+2}\oplus\cH_{-2},
 \qquad
 \sigma(\W)_{\rm red}=\frac14 I_2.
\end{equation}
Thus the correct repair propagates curvature; it does not cancel the two
helicities.

\begin{proposition}[Fixed-temporal first-order no-go]\label{prop:first-no-go}
For the fixed temporal pair-$(1,6)$, cyclic $-2\Pi$ realization, the complete
$46$-parameter invariant spatial family retains the same length-two
polynomial Jordan chain.  The induced sensitivity map from all $46$
parameters to the intrinsic Jordan obstruction is zero.  Hence no regular
member has a semisimple faithful strong linearization or a positive
symmetric-hyperbolic symmetrizer.
\end{proposition}

This proposition is scoped to that temporal incidence and cyclic placement.
Moreover, its Jordan extension becomes triangular/contractible after the
certified auxiliary shift; it is not a no-go for generalized Green
hyperbolicity.

\begin{proposition}[Parallel two-wave-factor no-go]\label{prop:factor-no-go}
On the $30$-row endpoint graph, the complete parallel $SO(3)$-invariant
trace-free-preserving two-wave family
\begin{equation}
 L_\pm=P_{\rm tr}+\Box P_{\rm TF}
       +A_\pm^\mu\nabla_\mu+B_\pm
\end{equation}
cannot factor the endpoint operator.  After the order-three and linear
order-two equations are eliminated, twelve exact rational multipliers obey
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nullstellensatz}
 \sum_i w_i f_i=1,
\end{equation}
so the remaining order-two polynomial system is inconsistent.
\end{proposition}

Proposition~\ref{prop:factor-no-go} excludes only this same-bundle parallel
family.  It does not exclude mixed-order systems, triangular extensions,
larger prolongations, or the adjoint-tractor transfer used below.

\section{Weyl--Cotton evolution and sourced constraints}
\label{sec:curvature}

Introduce an independent algebraic Weyl tensor $\Psi$ through the local graph
$\widehat\Psi=\Psi-C_1h$.  Its electric and magnetic parts are
\begin{equation}
 E_{ij}=\Psi_{0i0j},
 \qquad B_{ij}=\frac12\epsilon_i{}^{kl}\Psi_{0jkl}.
\end{equation}
The ten $E/B$ components do not close at first order.  Adding the sixteen
independent components of the Cotton divergence
$V_{\mu,\nu,\sigma}=\nabla^\rho\Psi_{\mu\rho\nu\sigma}$ gives a $26$-state
bundle.  Direct decomposition of the Bianchi, dual-Bianchi, and Bach rows
produces a $34\times26$ first-order table with eight primary constraints.
Comparison on all $150$ independent Weyl two-jets proves equality with the
covariant rows and globalizes over the cylinder.

\begin{theorem}[Constraint-adjusted Weyl--Cotton system]
\label{thm:WC}
There is a local constraint adjustment for which the $26$-state evolution
system is symmetric hyperbolic.  A positive STF symmetrizer makes every
spatial principal matrix symmetric, and the characteristic speeds are
\begin{equation}
 0,\qquad \pm\frac12,\qquad \pm\frac1{\sqrt3},\qquad \pm1.
\end{equation}
The eight primary and six secondary constraints form a closed causal
subsidiary system.  For an inhomogeneous source $F$, the exact subsidiary
identity has the form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sourced-subsidiary}
 L_{\rm con}\,\mathcal K(U)
 =R\,\mathcal K_{\rm src}(F).
\end{equation}
Consequently retarded and advanced solutions of compatible sources,
$\mathcal K_{\rm src}(F)=0$, remain in the constrained covariant system.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The $3+1$ decomposition of the covariant Bianchi, dual-Bianchi, and Bach
rows gives the stated $34\times26$ table.  Subtracting the local constraint
adjustment changes only combinations of the fourteen constraint rows and
therefore preserves the compatible covariant solution space.  In the
$SO(3)$ irreducible STF/vector/scalar blocks, direct multiplication by the
positive fibre form makes every adjusted spatial principal matrix symmetric;
diagonalizing those finite blocks gives the listed real causal speeds.
Applying the complete constraint operator to the adjusted evolution table
and commuting the $S^3$ derivatives gives
\eqref{eq:sourced-subsidiary} coefficientwise.  Its homogeneous principal
part has the same symmetric-hyperbolic energy estimate.  Uniqueness for that
subsidiary Cauchy problem therefore preserves all fourteen constraints when
the source compatibility rows vanish.  The coefficient tables and the two
independent covariant-versus-$3+1$ jet comparisons are identified in
Table~\ref{tab:ledger}.
\end{proof}

The sourced statement \eqref{eq:sourced-subsidiary}, not homogeneous
constraint propagation alone, is what permits Green operators on the
compatible complex.  The all-level solution audit gives precisely
\begin{align}
 d_E(N)&=2(N-1)(N+3), &&N\geq2,\nonumber\\
 d_A(N)&=2(N-1)(N+1), &&N\geq3,\label{eq:EAL-multiplicity}\\
 d_L(N)&=2(N-3)(N+1), &&N\geq4,\nonumber
\end{align}
with both chiralities.  Character identities prove exhaustion; the Cotton
graph adds no second solution copy.  Thus curvature propagation carries the
entire $E/A/L$ module, not only the Einstein branch.

\section{All-row prolongation and cyclic hybrid retract}
\label{sec:mapping-cylinder}

Curvature is adjoined as a contractible prolongation rather than substituted
for the metric potential.  The state/equation chain square is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chain-square}
 (L,K)T=A_{\rm eq}E_{\rm aux},
 \qquad T=(C_1,\operatorname{div}C_1),
\end{equation}
and the equation/identity square is its fibre-identified curved cotangent
counterpart.  The resulting mapping cylinder includes fields, equations,
identities, all antifields, trace/Weyl rows, nonminimal rows, and the
first-order curvature reduction.

\begin{proposition}[Curved mapping-cylinder SDR]\label{prop:cylinder-SDR}
The complete sixteen-block differential obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prol-SDR}
 Q_{\rm prol}^2=0,
 \qquad PI=\id,
 \qquad IP-\id=Q_{\rm prol}H+HQ_{\rm prol},
\end{equation}
and is odd cyclic.  All entries of $I,P,H$ are finite-order differential
operators or pointwise inverses.  The prolonged current satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prolonged-current}
 I^*\omega_{\rm prol}-\omega_{\rm aux}=d\beta+Q\gamma,
 \qquad \beta=\delta Y_I,
 \qquad \gamma=0.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

A filtered contraction sharpens \eqref{eq:prol-SDR}.  On all $386$
components there are complementary chain projectors
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hybrid-projectors}
 P_{\rm alg}+P_{\rm end}=\id,
 \qquad P_{\rm alg}P_{\rm end}=0,
 \qquad [Q,P_{\rm alg}]=[Q,P_{\rm end}]=0,
\end{equation}
with ranks $356$ and $30$, and a local cyclic homotopy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:alg-contraction}
 QH_{\rm alg}+H_{\rm alg}Q=P_{\rm alg}.
\end{equation}
The endpoint image is a differential graph retaining the metric together
with its locally determined curvature and equation variables.  No inverse
Weyl map, Laplacian, curl, TT projector, or helicity projector appears.

\section{Adjoint-tractor transfer and all-row causal homotopy}
\label{sec:tractor}

The adjoint-tractor Yang--Mills detour complex has bundle ranks
$15\to60\to60\to15$ and a degreewise normally hyperbolic parent witness
\cite{GoverSombergSoucek}.  Kostant contraction and finite homological
perturbation compress it to the trace-free metric ranks $4\to9\to9\to4$.
On the curved cylinder, the derivative commutators occupy ten invariant
$SO(3)$ channels.  Curvature action on the derivative, form, and tractor
slots cancels every channel and gives finite-order cyclic maps $i,p,h$.

\begin{proposition}[Curved HPL/Bach match]\label{prop:Bach-match}
The compressed parent middle operator agrees coefficientwise with the full
metric Bach block at derivative orders zero through four:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Bach-match}
 P_{\rm mid}^{\rm parent,comp}
 =-2S^TJ_{\rm met}\,\bar B\,S.
\end{equation}
Consequently the parent homotopies transfer to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:TF-homotopy}
 \Lambda_{{\rm TF},\pm}=p\Lambda_{{\rm parent},\pm}i,
 \qquad
 q_{\rm TF}\Lambda_{{\rm TF},\pm}
 +\Lambda_{{\rm TF},\pm}q_{\rm TF}=\id,
 \qquad
 \Lambda_{{\rm TF},+}^\sharp=\Lambda_{{\rm TF},-}.
\end{equation}
Their support is retarded or advanced because pre- and postcomposition are
finite-order local.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The curved HPL maps are chain maps, satisfy $pi=\id$, and are cyclic:
$i^\sharp=p$.  The PBW comparison in \eqref{eq:Bach-match} identifies the
transferred differential with the actual trace-free metric Bach complex,
not merely its principal symbol.  Hence the parent identity gives
\begin{align*}
 q_{\rm TF}(p\Lambda_{{\rm parent},\pm}i)
 +(p\Lambda_{{\rm parent},\pm}i)q_{\rm TF}
 &=p(q_{\rm parent}\Lambda_{{\rm parent},\pm}
 +\Lambda_{{\rm parent},\pm}q_{\rm parent})i\\
 &=pi=\id.
\end{align*}
Finite-order pre- and postcomposition preserves retarded/advanced support,
and $i^\sharp=p$ transfers the parent adjoint relation.  This proves
\eqref{eq:TF-homotopy}.
\end{proof}

The trace/Weyl rows attach through the local shear
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shear}
 U_G=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-d&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 U_I=\begin{pmatrix}1&d^\sharp\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad d=\frac12\operatorname{div},
 \qquad U^\sharp=U^{-1}.
\end{equation}
The trace complex consists of pointwise identity doublets with contraction
$h_{\rm tr}$.  Thus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:endpoint-homotopy}
 \Lambda_{{\rm end},\pm}
 =U(\Lambda_{{\rm TF},\pm}\oplus h_{\rm tr})U^{-1}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Full causal homotopy]\label{thm:full-homotopy}
On the complete prolonged complex,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-homotopy}
 \boxed{
 \Lambda_{{\rm prol},\pm}
 =H_{\rm alg}
 +i_{\rm end}\Lambda_{{\rm end},\pm}p_{\rm end}},
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-properties}
 Q_{\rm prol}\Lambda_{{\rm prol},\pm}
 +\Lambda_{{\rm prol},\pm}Q_{\rm prol}=\id,
 \qquad
 \supp\Lambda_{{\rm prol},\pm}f\subseteq J^\pm(\supp f),
 \qquad
 \Lambda_{{\rm prol},+}^\sharp=\Lambda_{{\rm prol},-}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The endpoint inclusion and projection satisfy
$i_{\rm end}p_{\rm end}=P_{\rm end}$ and
$p_{\rm end}i_{\rm end}=\id$.  Therefore
\begin{align*}
 Q\Lambda_{{\rm prol},\pm}+\Lambda_{{\rm prol},\pm}Q
 &=P_{\rm alg}
 +i_{\rm end}
   (Q_{\rm end}\Lambda_{{\rm end},\pm}
    +\Lambda_{{\rm end},\pm}Q_{\rm end})p_{\rm end}\\
 &=P_{\rm alg}+P_{\rm end}=\id.
\end{align*}
The algebraic term and the endpoint inclusion/projection are local, while
the endpoint homotopy has causal support by
Proposition~\ref{prop:Bach-match}; their sum therefore has the required
support.  The identities $H_{\rm alg}^\sharp=H_{\rm alg}$,
$i_{\rm end}^\sharp=p_{\rm end}$, and
$\Lambda_{{\rm end},+}^\sharp=\Lambda_{{\rm end},-}$ give the final adjoint
relation.
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:full-homotopy} does not construct a canonical same-sided
inverse for the endpoint operator or a monolithic prolonged witness
$QW+WQ$ with degreewise Green inverses.  Those two implementation-specific
objects are unnecessary for \eqref{eq:full-properties}.

\section{Causal, equivariant, and pairing transport}\label{sec:transport}

Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:causal-difference}
 \Lambda=\Lambda_+-\Lambda_-:
 \Gamma_c(\cC_{\rm prol})[1]
 \longrightarrow\Gamma_{sc}(\cC_{\rm prol}).
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Causal quasi-isomorphism]\label{prop:causal-QI}
The map \eqref{eq:causal-difference} is a quasi-isomorphism.  Its cutoff
inverse is represented by $[u]\mapsto[Q(\chi_+u)]$; the two inverse identities
follow from the past-compact and future-compact contractions and the support
exact sequence.  Since $S^3$ is compact,
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_{sc}(\cC_{\rm prol})=\Gamma^\infty(\cC_{\rm prol}),
\end{equation}
so the target contains all smooth global cylinder solutions.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Subtracting the two identities in \eqref{eq:full-properties} shows that
$\Lambda_+-\Lambda_-$ is a chain map of degree $-1$.  The standard support
exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow\Gamma_c
 \longrightarrow\Gamma_{pc}\oplus\Gamma_{fc}
 \longrightarrow\Gamma_{sc}\longrightarrow0
\]
together with the two same-sided contractions gives its inverse on
cohomology.  Concretely, for a spacelike-compact cocycle $u$, a temporal
partition defines the compact cocycle $Q(\chi_+u)$; the homotopy identities
send its causal class back to $[u]$.  Conversely, a causal class that is
exact has past-compact and future-compact primitives whose difference is a
compact primitive.  These are the two standard inverse computations for a
Green-hyperbolic complex \cite{GreenHyperbolic}.  Compactness of $S^3$
turns spacelike-compact support into unrestricted smooth support.
\end{proof}

For each conformal-Killing generator $\xi_a$, choose a temporal cutoff
$\chi$ and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:CKV-source}
 j_a=Q(\chi\xi_a).
\end{equation}
Then $j_a$ is compactly supported, $Qj_a=0$, and
$[\Lambda j_a]=[\xi_a]$.  The dual endpoint construction is complementary,
the endpoint pairing is perfect, and the compact grading is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:CKV-grading}
 4_{-1}\oplus7_0\oplus4_{+1}.
\end{equation}
All prolongation and auxiliary additions are contractible, so no second
endpoint copy appears.

\begin{proposition}[$SO(4,2)$ transport]\label{prop:SO42}
The causal/Cauchy bridge is $SO(4,2)$-equivariant on cohomology.  If $\rho$
is a local conformal chain generator and $\kappa=[Q,\chi]$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cutoff-equivariance}
 [\kappa,\rho]=[Q,[\chi,\rho]].
\end{equation}
The homotopy $[\chi,\rho]$ has compact support on spacelike-compact sections.
Together with naturality of the curvature mapping cylinder and the all-level
$E/A/L$ intertwiners, \eqref{eq:cutoff-equivariance} induces the strict
residual action on cohomology.  The chosen Cauchy splitting itself need not
be strictly equivariant.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Local conformal naturality gives $[Q,\rho]=0$.  The graded Jacobi identity
then gives \eqref{eq:cutoff-equivariance} from $\kappa=[Q,\chi]$.  Since
$[\chi,\rho]$ contains derivatives of the temporal cutoff, its coefficients
are supported in the compact transition slab; $S^3$ is compact.  Thus it is
an admissible compactly supported chain homotopy.  Naturality of the two
local retracts and the all-level curvature intertwiners identifies the
resulting cohomological action with the residual action imported from Paper
A.
\end{proof}

Let $\Delta_\Lambda=\Lambda_+-\Lambda_-$.  The adjoint identity in
\eqref{eq:full-properties} makes $\Delta_\Lambda$ graded antisymmetric.  The
common algebraic and trace contractions cancel in this difference, leaving
the parent Green/current identity.
