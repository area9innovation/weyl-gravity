\section{Fail-closed boundaries}

The main theorem depends on the conjunction of the ten claims above.  It does
not promote any of the following:
\begin{itemize}
\item all-orders nonlinear $D$-Cartan contraction;
\item arity-four closure;
\item a general-background nonlinear Koszul--Tate export;
\item Hadamard or Feynman two-point functions;
\item renormalized composites, anomaly cancellation or the quantum master
equation;
\item a positive graviton Hilbert space or interacting unitarity;
\item a boundary, asymptotic, black-hole or scattering charge theorem.
\end{itemize}
The positive matter clock and the vanishing compact-universe tangent charge
must not be transported to those settings without a new phase-space and
boundary calculation.
