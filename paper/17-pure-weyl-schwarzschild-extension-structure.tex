\begin{abstract}
This companion develops the mathematical structure behind the endpoint
nonselection theorem proved in Paper~16.  The axial six-state Bach system is
an exact RW/RW/spin-one filtration whose repeated spin-two sector is a
non-split self-extension.  The extension is the partial first jet of a
four-state spin-two/spin-one system and reduces projectively to a scalar
cocycle paired with the symmetric-square Regge--Wheeler module.  At a simple
spin-two quasinormal frequency the complete connection has precisely two
local Smith branches.  A validated projective Evans calculation selects the
defective branch at one physical damped mode.  The result is currently a
connection-level exceptional point; promotion to a second-order
Green-resolvent pole requires the analytic Fredholm construction stated
here as a separate programme.
\end{abstract}

\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
\]
Introduce
\[
\mathcal B(\tau)=
\begin{pmatrix}
A+\tau E&D+\tau C\\

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

\section{Projective cocycle}

After Liouville reduction put
\[
L=D^2+U,\qquad
Lx=(s_1D+s_0)y,\qquad Ly=0.
\]
The scalar extension density
\[
\mathcal I=s_0-\frac12s_1'

transforms under a rational triangular gauge by
\[
\mathcal I\longmapsto
\mathcal I-\frac12\mathcal K_Uq,
\qquad
\mathcal K_U=D^3+4UD+2U'.
\]
Thus the projective content is the class
\[
[\mathcal I]\in
\C(r,\omega)/\mathcal K_U\C(r,\omega).

\section{Local Smith dichotomy}

At a simple spin-two QNM with a spin-one local unit, the connection reduces
analytically to
\[
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&a\end{pmatrix},
\qquad a(\omega_n)=0,\quad a'(\omega_n)\ne0.
\]
If \(b(\omega_n)\ne0\), the complete three-factor Smith valuations are
\((0,0,2)\); if \(b(\omega_n)=0\), they are \((0,1,1)\).
Equivalently, the second Fitting ideal is respectively the unit ideal or
\((a)\).

\section{Next theorem}

Let \(L(\omega):D\to Y\) be an analytic Fredholm realization of the scalar
QNM problem, and let the extension be analytic and bounded from \(D\) to
\(Y\).  The triangular parametrix then makes the repeated block analytic
Fredholm.  If
\[
\alpha_n=\langle\widetilde u_n,L'(\omega_n)u_n\rangle,\qquad
\beta_n=\langle\widetilde u_n,\mathcal E(\omega_n)u_n\rangle
\]
and \(\beta_n\ne0\), its inverse has upper-right principal part
\[
-\frac{\beta_n}{\alpha_n^2(\omega-\omega_n)^2}
u_n\otimes\widetilde u_n.
\]
Establishing the required endpoint domains and graph-norm bounds is the
remaining hypothesis, not a result of the present connection certificate.
